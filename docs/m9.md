# Milestone 9 Specification: Classes

M8 made the game readable and punchy. The first playtest and the design work since ([classes.md](classes.md)) showed that the classes themselves need work: the Betrayer is weak against the swarm, Kiss of Betrayal is useless outside the boss fight, and every class has only one way to shoot. This milestone reworks the four classes. It adds no new dungeons, enemies or progression.

All numbers are **initial tuning values**, as in the MVP.

## 1. Goals and design rules

**Goals:**
1. The Betrayer feels strong against the swarm, not only against the boss.
2. Every class has two attacks, and both are worth using.
3. The Fallen's leap and the Heretic's heal are visibly big moments.

**Design rules** (from `classes.md`):
- **Two attacks per class.** Left mouse fires the primary, right mouse the secondary. Each is clearly the better one in some situation, so neither is worth holding all the time.
- **Every ability rewards timing.** No ability is worth pressing the moment it's ready.
- **One effect per ability, with no hidden riders.** A second effect is allowed only if it's visible on its own.
- M8's rules still hold: a clean screen, visual feedback rather than text, hellfire colors for players' effects (heals stay green, shields blue).

## 2. Controls and attacks

### 2.1 Controls

| Input | Action |
|---|---|
| Left mouse (hold) | Primary attack |
| Right mouse (hold) | Secondary attack |

- **One attack at a time.** While both buttons are held, the one pressed **last** is used. Releasing it switches to the other if that's still held.
- **Can fire:** Sacrament can fire while the Heretic has a valid ally target (§2.5). Every other attack can always fire. The fire timer (§2.2) doesn't count: an attack waiting for the timer is still held.
- **The Heretic's beam comes first.** While the right button is held and Sacrament can fire (§2.5), the Heretic heals, whichever button was pressed last: the left button can't fire censers or stop the beam. When Sacrament can't fire, the right button doesn't count as held. So a Heretic holding both buttons with nobody aimed at fires censers, starts healing as soon as an ally is aimed at, and returns to the censers when the target is lost.
- **The host decides.** The client sends which buttons are held and which was pressed last (`fire`, §10), and the host picks the attack by these rules, with its own check of the ally target. So when the client sees an ally target that the host finds dead or out of range, a Heretic holding the left button still fires censers. The client applies the same rules to its own view for its cosmetic shots.
- The canvas suppresses the browser's context menu (`contextmenu` is prevented), in game and in the dev modes.
- Both buttons are released and ignored in the same cases as fire today (focus lost, Pause, chat line open, dead).

### 2.2 The shared fire timer

Each player has **one fire timer** for both attacks, replacing the per-weapon timer of MVP §6. Each tick, in this order:
1. If an attack is held (§2.1), it can fire, and the timer is 0 or less: the attack fires once and **its own** time between shots is added to the timer.
2. The timer decreases by 1/30 s, or by **2/30 s while the player is in a Field of Blood** (§3.4). While no attack is held, it doesn't go below 0.

So firing a slow attack delays the next shot of either attack. A Silver Bullet costs 1.5 s of revolver fire, which makes the choice between the two a real one. The client runs the same timer locally for its cosmetic shot feedback, as now, with the field rule of §5.1 (*Standing in the field*).

### 2.3 Attacks

| Class | Attack | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Fallen | Primary: Brimstone Shotgun | As now: 8 hitscan pellets × 12 dmg, spread ±8° yaw and ±4° pitch, range 20 m, 0.8 s. Adds the knockback of §3.1. |
| Fallen | Secondary: Brimstone Slug | Hitscan, 60 dmg to the first enemy hit, no spread, range 50 m, 1.0 s. |
| Heretic | Primary: Censer Launcher | As now. |
| Heretic | Secondary: Sacrament | Heals the ally target 10 HP, 0.5 s (§2.5). |
| Binder | Primary: Chain Gun | As now. |
| Binder | Secondary: Scourge | Melee sweep, 25 dmg, 0.8 s (§2.6). |
| Betrayer | Primary: Silver Revolver | Hitscan, 30 dmg to the first enemy hit (no pierce), no spread, range 60 m, 0.15 s. |
| Betrayer | Secondary: Silver Bullet | Hitscan, 300 dmg carried through the line (§2.4), no spread, range 60 m, 1.5 s. |

**Reviving** (M8 §4.2): every hitscan attack revives souls as now, with `interval ÷ 3` per hit from its own interval. Censers revive as now. The Scourge and Sacrament don't revive.

### 2.4 Silver Bullet: damage carried through the line

- The ray finds every enemy along it within 60 m, nearest first, up to the terrain.
- The bullet starts with `remaining = 300`. For each enemy in order:
  - Let *m* be 2 if the enemy is rooted (pull included), otherwise 1. This is only the pipeline's Bound step (§3.6) worked out in advance: each hit below goes through the pipeline as usual, and its Bound step applies the ×*m*, so it's never doubled twice.
  - If `remaining × m` is at least the enemy's HP, the hit deals `HP ÷ m` (the pipeline makes it `HP`), the enemy dies, and `remaining` drops by `HP ÷ m`. If `remaining` is now 0, the bullet stops at this enemy.
  - Otherwise the hit deals `remaining` (the pipeline makes it `remaining × m`) and the bullet stops at this enemy.
- **The Gatekeeper** takes whatever is left, so it stops the bullet.
- **Where the ray stops** (for souls, M8 §4.2, and the tracer): at the enemy the bullet stopped at; otherwise at the terrain or 60 m.
- **Examples:** 15 Blessed (20 HP each) in a line; 30 bound Blessed; a Chorister (60 HP) and then 12 Blessed.
- **Why 300:** holding the revolver for the 1.5 s a Silver Bullet costs kills about 10 Blessed, so the bullet only pays off when more than 10 enemies line up, and then only a little (15 in a full lane is 10 kills per second against 6.7). Its real gain comes with Chains: bound enemies cost half.

### 2.5 Sacrament

- **Uses the Heretic's ally target** (M8 §3.5), the same one as Martyr's Shroud, with the same range: 40 m.
- The client sends the ally target in every input, as now. The host uses the latest input's `allyTargetId`. The ally must be alive and within 40 m; the host doesn't recheck line of sight.
- **With no valid ally target, Sacrament doesn't fire**: nothing happens and the fire timer isn't touched (the censer fires instead if the left button is held, §2.1). It never heals the Heretic.
- Each firing heals the ally 10 HP, capped at max HP. It fires even at full HP, so the beam (§5.1) stays steady.
- **Why 10:** 20 HP per second on one ally, the same rate Communion gives everyone near the Heretic, so the Heretic's healing on one ally at most doubles. A Heretic in a Field of Blood heals twice as often (§3.4); that's the field's reward, and it lasts 8 s.
- **The `beam` field** (§10) of the Heretic's player record is the healed ally's ID from a firing until 0.6 s pass without one, then 255.

### 2.6 Scourge

- Hits the **6 nearest** living enemies within 3 m of the Binder's body center (distance to the enemy's cylinder, MVP §5.3) whose horizontal direction from the Binder is within **60°** of the horizontal aim direction (a 120° arc).
- Each takes 25 dmg and, if it survives, is slowed for 1 s, like a Chain Gun hit.
- No line of sight is needed, like other area effects.
- It fires whether it hits anything or not.

### 2.7 Speeds

| Class | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| The Fallen | 7 m/s | 6 m/s |
| The Binder | 8 m/s | 6 m/s |

The host's speed check (MVP §9.3) uses the new class speeds. This replaces the decision about outrunning the Blessed "comfortably" (`docs/decisions.md`, §7.1).

## 3. Abilities

### 3.1 Shotgun knockback

- After all 8 pellets of a shot are resolved, each enemy that a pellet hit, that is still alive, and whose cylinder is within **6 m** of the Fallen's body center is **knocked back 2 m** horizontally, directly away from the Fallen, over **0.2 s**.
- It's knocked back once per shot, however many pellets hit it.
- **Not knocked back:** rooted enemies, including during a pull, so the Binder's piles hold; and the Gatekeeper (immune).
- Knockback works as in MVP §5.6 (normal collision, no steering, can fall off ledges). A new knockback replaces one in progress.

### 3.2 Falling Star

- **The landing:** **10 dmg** (was 40) to every enemy within 5 m, then the survivors are knocked back 4 m away from the landing point. **Rooted enemies** (including during a pull) take the damage but aren't knocked back, like the shotgun's (§3.1), so the Binder's piles hold. The damage is small on purpose: the knockback is the ability's point, and the party's fire finishes the scattered crowd. The knockback now takes **0.4 s** instead of 0.2 s.
- **New event `starLanded`** (§10), sent on the landing tick:
  - `launched`: the slots of the knocked-back survivors (not the rooted ones or the Gatekeeper).
- **On every client**, after the render delay, like other events:
  - **Launched enemies** fly an arc: their billboard is lifted by `4 × 1.0 m × u × (1 − u)`, where *u* goes from 0 to 1 over the 0.4 s. The peak is 1 m, at the middle. Their ground position comes from the snapshots as usual.
- The arc is only drawn. In the simulation enemies move along the ground as in any knockback.

### 3.3 Martyr's Shroud burst

- **The host remembers who cast each shield.**
- When **damage** takes a shield to 0, it bursts: 50 dmg to the **8 nearest** enemies within 5 m of the shielded player's body center (distance to the enemy's cylinder, MVP §5.3, nearest by that distance), credited to the caster. The cap keeps it from wiping a packed swarm (as the Censer's splash cap, `decisions.md` §6.2).
- **It bursts even if the hit that breaks it kills the shielded player.** The burst is centered where that player stood.
- **No burst** when the shield expires, when a new shield replaces it, or when its player leaves while it's up.
- **It still bursts if its caster has died or left** meanwhile. The damage is credited to the caster, or to no one if the caster has left.
- **New event `shroudBurst`** (§10) for the effect and sound.

### 3.4 Field of Blood (Betrayer, Q)

Replaces Kiss of Betrayal. Cooldown **30 s**.

- **Where:** tossed in front of the Betrayer, with no aiming. The **field's center** is **3 m** ahead of the Betrayer's feet along its horizontal aim (yaw), at the floor height of the cell there. Pitch doesn't matter: the Betrayer places the field by walking where it wants it.
  - If a wall or closed door is in the way, the center is the last open point before it, checking points every 0.25 m from the Betrayer (like the Chains destination walk, `decisions.md` §6.3), at the floor of that point's cell.
  - It lands on whatever floor is there, so tossed off a ledge it lands below.
- **It always has a place**, so it always goes on cooldown.
- **Effect:** for **8 s**, every living player whose position (the center of their feet) is within **6 m** of the center horizontally is **in the field**, provided their **ground** is within **0.5 m** of the center's floor, up or down. A player's ground is the highest floor among the cells their circle overlaps, as in the host's floor check (`decisions.md` §9.3). It depends only on where the player is, not how high, so jumping in the field or leaping over it with Falling Star still counts; a step of 0.5 m counts; the 1 m ledge, a terrace or a pit doesn't. A wall between them and the center doesn't matter.
  - A player in the field **fires twice as fast**: their fire timer counts down twice as fast (§2.2), for both attacks. Everything that follows the fire timer doubles with it: kills, Sacrament's healing and reviving by shots. Damage per hit and ability cooldowns are unchanged.
  - Being in the field is checked each tick, from the player's latest accepted position.
- **Why fire rate, not damage:** a Blessed has 20 HP, and most attacks (revolver, censer splash, Scourge, slug, Shroud burst) already kill one per hit, so doubled damage would do almost nothing in the swarm. Twice the fire rate doubles every attack's kills there and its damage on the boss.
- **One field exists at a time.** A new field replaces the old one. **A field stays its full 8 s if its Betrayer dies or leaves.**
- **Event:** `abilityUsed` with `slot: 'Q'`, at the field's center.
- **Joining:** a player who joins while a field is down doesn't see it, since events reach a client only once it's in the game (`decisions.md`, M8 §6.2). The host still applies it to them.

### 3.5 Kiss of Betrayal is removed

Remove it everywhere:
- the ability and its constants;
- the marked status and its damage step;
- the snapshot flag bit 1, which stays unused (§10);
- the mark sprite in the world atlas and its red glow;
- the mark beam;
- the *Kiss* sound;
- its texts and hints;
- the debug and test references.

`icon-kiss.png` and `mark.png` stay in `assets/art-src/` but are no longer built into the game.

### 3.6 Damage pipeline

Replaces MVP §5.5 as amended by M8 §8. Every hit is resolved in this order:

1. **Bound:** if the target enemy is rooted (pull included), damage ×2.
2. **Brimstone Hide:** if the target is the Fallen, damage ×0.6.
3. **Invulnerability:** if the target is invulnerable, damage is 0.
4. **Shield:** the shield absorbs damage first; the remainder goes to HP. A shield taken to 0 bursts (§3.3).
5. **Death:** at HP 0 or below, the target dies. The credited player gets the kill.

- Field of Blood isn't a step: it changes how often players fire, not the damage of a hit. On bound enemies in a field, the party's damage per second is ×4.
- Damage-based thresholds (the Judgment interrupt) count damage after step 1.

## 4. The Gatekeeper

Kiss tripled the party's damage on the boss for 6 s of every 10 s, about ×2.2 on average. Field of Blood doubles the fire rate for 8 s of every 30 s, about ×1.3 when the whole party stands in it, and the Betrayer's single-target damage rose from about 170 to 200 per second. In total, party damage on the boss falls to about 64% of M8's.

| Item | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| HP | 45 000 | **29 000** |
| Judgment interrupt threshold | 2 000 dmg | **1 300 dmg** |

Both are still multiplied by the party-size scaling (M8 §6.3). Everything else is unchanged.

## 6. Learning the classes and the HUD

### 6.1 Class cards

- **Weapon lines:** the card's weapon line (M8 §2.1) becomes **two lines**, the primary and then the secondary. Each starts with a **mouse glyph** and is followed by the attack's name, which shows its tooltip on hover.
  - The glyph is a 12 × 16 px mouse outline drawn in code (SVG), with the left or right button half filled ember (`#ff7a3a`).
  - The Fallen's `· Brimstone Hide` stays after the primary's name.
  - Every card has the same rows, so the ability rows still line up: each card's subgrid gets a sixth row for the second weapon line (`decisions.md`, M8 playtest fixes).

### 6.2 Hints panel

Hint lines can now be keyed `LMB` or `RMB`. Those lines show the mouse glyph (§6.1) at 18 × 24 px where ability lines show their icon.

## 8. Bot

Changes to the bot (MVP §2.5, M8 §6.4). Each frame, after choosing its aim:
- **Fallen:** holds the secondary when its aim enemy is farther than 20 m from its eye, otherwise the primary.
- **Heretic:** when no living enemy is within 6 m and a living teammate within 40 m with line of sight has less than 60% HP, it aims at that teammate's body center (the lowest HP fraction first) and holds the secondary. Reviving (M8 §6.4) comes first.
- **Binder:** holds the secondary when at least 3 living enemies are within 3 m and within 60° of its aim, otherwise the primary.
- **Betrayer:** holds the secondary when more than 10 living enemies' cylinders lie along its aim ray within 60 m, up to the terrain (where the bullet pays off, §2.4), checked at most 4 times per second, otherwise the primary.
- Q and E: as now, whenever ready.

## 10. Protocol changes

Changes to MVP §9 and M8 §10. `version` still rejects mismatched builds.

**Input:** byte 24, `fireHeld`, becomes **`fire`**, the mouse buttons (§2.1): bit 0 the left button is held, bit 1 the right button is held, bit 2 the right button was pressed after the left (used only while both are held). The size stays 30 bytes.

**Events**

| Change | Type | Fields |
|---|---|---|
| changed | `abilityUsed` | the Betrayer's Q is at the field's center (Kiss is gone) |
| new | `starLanded` | `playerId`, `x`, `y`, `z`, `launched: number[]` (enemy slots) |
| new | `shroudBurst` | `playerId` (the shielded player), `x`, `y`, `z` |
| new | `silverBullet` | `playerId`, `x`, `y`, `z` (the eye), `ex`, `ey`, `ez` (where the ray stopped) |

**Snapshots**
- **Players:** two new fields after `shots`:
  - `shots2` u8: a wrapping counter of secondary attacks fired;
  - `beam` u8: the ally being healed by Sacrament (§2.5), 255 for none.
  - `shots` now counts primary attacks only.
  - A record grows to **32 bytes**.
- **Enemy flags:** bit 1 (`marked`) is unused.
- **Largest snapshot:** 15 763 bytes, still under the 16 000-byte split limit.

**Debug object:** each player gets `primaryShots` and `secondaryShots`: the host's counts of primary and secondary attacks fired, not wrapped.
