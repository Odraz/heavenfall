# R1 Generated floor

**Status: planned** (revision 9). Dependency: accepted R0. Prepare this brief against the R0 baseline before implementing it ([development-workflow.md](development-workflow.md)).

## Playable outcome

Every run builds a new Pearly Gates floor of 10–11 rooms from the seed. The party starts in the Breach, sees each door's fight sigil, walks into an idle room, the countdown seals it, the waves come, the room clears, the doors open, and the party picks the next door. Cleared rooms trickle a few leashed Blessed when revisited. The minimap and the hold-M full map show what's known. The boss room is entered only through the Confessional and holds the inherited Gatekeeper as a labeled fixture. Co-op and bots work as today.

**Before:** one fixed sequence of arenas. **After:** a different floor every run, and a route choice at every cleared room.

**Fixture limits (labeled in game):** the Pardoner's stall and the Confessional are empty shells (shop R3, Advocate R4); no props, pickups, Dominion or fire yet (R2); clear rewards and door reward plates arrive in R3; the key room, Reliquary and bells are marked by the generator but do nothing until R6; the boss is the inherited fight until R7; kills and clears give no XP or halos yet (R2 halos, R3 XP); countdown holds for shopping and overlays are inert until those overlays exist. The old fixed dungeon stays reachable as a development fixture.

## The R1 room pool

The 10 combat templates that need no Dominion and no Refiner's Fire. They cover every shape and both rectangle orientations, and are enough for any legal floor without repeating a template:

- 1×1: `pg-c-1x1-ranged-01`, `-ranged-02`, `-ranged-03`, `pg-c-1x1-flyers-01`, `-flyers-02`, `-flyers-03`
- 2×1: `pg-c-2x1-horde-01`, `pg-c-2x1-ranged-01`, `pg-c-2x1-flyers-01`
- 2×2: `pg-c-2x2-horde-flyers-01`
- Specials: `pg-s-breach-01`, `pg-s-pardoner-01`, `pg-s-confessional-01`, `pg-b-gate-01`; the fallback floor of rooms.md §5 (its templates outside this pool are substituted by pool templates of the same shape until R2, recorded as a fixture limit).

Prop sockets, hazards and elite waves in these templates are parsed and validated but inactive until R2.

## Ordered stages

1. **Generator and validation.** `src/gen/`, D4 transforms, stamping, the fallback floor, map layers, `validateLevel` changes. Tests: determinism, the 1 000 pinned seeds within 20 attempts, every §3.3 rule, Node/browser identical JSON for one seed. Old fixed dungeon unchanged.
2. **Load a generated floor.** `start` with seeds and run id, generation in the worker and client, the loading text, the Gate's facing. Walk the floor with doors open (no fights yet).
3. **Room lifecycle.** Phases, countdown/seal/teleport-in, waves through the per-room director, clear and respawn at 50%, the 30% clear heal, the leaf rule, trickle. Bots follow the active room.
4. **Map and discovery.** Knowledge states, discovery through vestibules, minimap, full map, door sigils.
5. **Protocol identity.** Run-scoped input/snapshots, header fields, fingerprint check before admission. Two runs back to back without leaking state.
6. **Checkpoint.** Report, fixture limits, play checklist.
