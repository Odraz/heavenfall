# Revision 7: encounter challenge and settlement cross-review

Reviewed 2026-10-11. Owned changes: `docs/rogue/rooms.md` and `docs/rogue/bosses.md`. Read the full room/boss catalogs, relevant generation/lifecycle/progression/provenance sections of `game.md`, lore, base class rules, four recorded playtests, the horde-fun report and the revised class/reward contracts that interact with boss targeting, held movement, ground effects and settlement. ## First challenge/repair round: geometry and readable counterplay

1. **The Bell had two incompatible interaction ranges.** The room catalog measured 2.5 m horizontally from the point under the bell; the shared interaction contract measures accepted-eye 3D distance to a visible anchor. The catalog now places the lower-rim anchor at the continuous center of the four-metre reward square, `(rewardSpot.x +2, rewardSpot.y +2)`, supporting floor +3 m, with 2.5 m 3D eye range and geometry LOS. With eye +1.6 m, legal grounded horizontal reach is approximately `sqrt(2.5² −1.4²) = 2.07 m`. A player 2.4 m horizontally away is about 2.78 m from the rim and cannot ring it yet. Core §4.12 was coordinated to use the same anchor.
2. **Bot Toll avoidance assumed bots could never reach the dais lip.** Cover selection could use that lip, and Called releases can put a bot there regardless of preferred cover. Waiting for the active ring's snapshot tick cannot anticipate its birth disk. Bots now predict disk/front contact during the Toll's tell using its announced impact tick, then the live ring, body radius and accepted motion. The 0.22 s lead applies before impact too; cancellation removes the forecast. Ordinary jump dynamics provide about 1.06 m of rise at 0.22 s, above the 0.5 m band. This is arithmetic, not measured bot performance.
3. **The damaging Toll could be drawn beneath a terrace.** A scaled flat annulus would lie under the +1 m north terrace, despite that terrace being affected. The cue now follows actual affected supporting-floor/stair heights ≤base +1 m, omitting unaffected higher gallery/dais/plinth tops. Cue and host height predicate agree. The personal whoosh also forecasts birth from the tell, rather than starting too late at the lip.
4. **Dominion slam geometry permitted an incorrect low-dais implementation.** Its ring now explicitly uses horizontal radius/feet tolerance and body-center LOS: source feet +1.6 m to victim body center. A floor-to-floor segment must not make the +1 m Hall daises immune. Tall intervening geometry still blocks it; the height tolerance alone never grants a hit. This clarifies the shared radial-query convention without changing the enemy's rule or damage.
5. **A prop shorthand contradicted its attack cylinder.** “Props don't block anything” now says they do not block movement; their accepted attack-query cylinders remain until broken. No new collision behavior was introduced.

No authored grid changed. The two boss-grid copies remain the same authored definition. Rotations still transform geometry/marker identities discretely and Gate boundary planes continuously; valid physical strips remain fixed rather than being carved after transformation.

## Second challenge/repair round: rewards, return and progression

6. **The boss summary awarded XP to offline participants.** “Every human gets 1,500 XP” conflicted with connected-only XP. The catalog now distinguishes connected eligible boss XP, with Sentence, from every eligible admitted unclosed record's victory/clear/unlock/pawn/deed settlement. Temporarily disconnected records receive the latter on supported rejoin, without missed XP or automatic picks. Voluntary pre-win leave closes eligibility.
7. **Developer victory could be read as ordinary persistent settlement.** Boss settlement explicitly honors immutable `progressionEnabled=false`: awards, allocation, claims, inventory and Stair shopping remain in the disposable record, with no profile writes or carried-item stakes. This agrees with the core's overriding disposable-run contract.
8. **Revive-then-heal could silently restore souls to 80%.** The final integrated pass made victory's life-state groups explicit at the latch, after any originating shield-break transaction. Souls restore exactly 50%; only players already living and connected in that captured group get the separate 30% heal. An absent living record gets no heal. This prevents settlement order from changing the stated recovery amount.
9. **D4 transformed a reward rectangle as one corner.** The new Bell-center trace exposed the existing `rewardSpot` ambiguity: it is a top-left 4×4 area anchor, not an ordinary cell point. A raw mirror maps the Gate's `(32,32)` to `(35,32)`, falsely centering a derived Bell/Stair at `(37,34)` rather than `(34,34)`. A mirrored Ziggurat raw corner `(17,14)` would define square x17–20, crossing its summit edge instead of the authored x14–17. The catalogs now transform all four occupied-cell rectangle corners, recover minimum x/y and derive the continuous center afterward. Pedestal points transform with stable player assignments. Core orientation was coordinated to use this same rectangle exception.

Independent shared-core proposals sent to the coordinator:

- Make the newly added free-chest `passRelic` action explicit in the reliable-message summary as well as the main control table; bargaining `declineOffer` and free-Rite `passRite` remain distinct. Pass uses the same life/range/phase/offer-version checks as its claim, grants no fallback payment and is terminal.
- Carry immutable `progressionEnabled` through admission, floorSync, retained run records and cached settlement before any client profile write; a supported debug rejoin may receive no fresh `start` message.
- At boss victory, resolve an admitted temporarily disconnected dead record to alive at 50%, as ordinary clear already does, without an absent living-record heal. Its return restores that actual settlement rather than fabricating resurrection on rejoin.
- Correct the reward catalog's pooled-membership reference from boss hand-in checks (§5) to the actual state contract (§3.6).

The final reread confirmed the integrated Bell anchor, explicit free-relic Pass/CAS, retained-soul victory recovery and debug admission/floorSync/settlement flag. Those coordinated changes close the concrete cross-contract issues above; the life-state grouping clarification was sent as the final settlement-order repair.

## Fun and implementation readiness

Valve's developer-authored [Replayable Cooperative Game Design talk](https://cdn.fastly.steamstatic.com/apps/valve/2009/GDC2009_ReplayableCooperativeGameDesign_Left4Dead.pdf), pages 19–21, 28–38, supports enemies that interrupt familiar tactics, clear anticipation, constrained variation and pressure/relief. My design inference is to preserve real verbs and readable consequences: choose whether to bait the Dominion, switch to the choir, dive for a mote, draw a crowd through fire, take a Bell risk, free a called ally, clear a local eye or relocate, and jump the ring at one's own contact time. Cue/range inconsistencies undermine those choices even when every damage number is specified.

Isaac-style adaptation here comes from run-specific build and resource decisions around a finite authored pool; a fixed learnable boss need not randomize required dodge timing. A shortest route can skip the floor's optional crest/collection branches, while retaining its required early Rite and a complete ordinary-weapon boss solution. Full-clearing and Bells trade time/survival for additional builds and collection; no unearned invulnerable farming is created after victory.

No new known encounter design blocker remains after these paper repairs and the coordinated cross-contract changes. This conclusion does not certify zero bugs or fun. Current expanded/rotated pool acceptance, all-party spawn access, readable crest effects, real bot reaction/movement, worst-case frame/network budgets, class-dependent boss duration and observed repeated-run decisions still need implementation and human-play evidence. Historical r1/r2 geometry data and average-DPS estimates do not prove those properties.
