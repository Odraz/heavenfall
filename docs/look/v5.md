# V5 Props, HUD and finish

**Status: planned** (drafted 2026-10-25). Dependencies: V2–V4 accepted. Rules: [spec.md](spec.md). Tomas's side: [guide.md](guide.md#v5).

## Playable outcome

The last flat pieces join the new look: decorations stand as real 3D objects, breakable props are rendered from models in the same light as the characters, and the HUD matches the concept: the class portrait in a framed diamond with HP and shield bars beside it, ability slots below, and a weapon panel in the lower right. Then the whole game gets a final performance pass and Tomas's acceptance of the look.

Out of scope: new HUD information. The concept shows an ammo count (`6/24`), but the game has no ammunition; the weapon panel shows the weapon and what the HUD already tracks, nothing invented.

## Frame-time share

The 5% reserve (spec §5) is available for fixes found here; the props and HUD themselves should cost nothing measurable (static meshes merged into zone chunks; DOM HUD).

## Ordered stages

### 1. Decorations as 3D

- Candelabrum, lily urn, harp, angel statue and fountain (today single sprites, [art-prompts.md](../art-prompts.md) B2) become static meshes merged into the zone chunks, from Meshy static models (O3–O7) decimated and baked like the kit (V2 stage 1). Same footprints and positions as today; decorations that block movement keep exactly their simulated shape.

Tests: footprints unchanged; geometry rules (a decoration taller than relief stands only where today's sprite stood, which already obeys them).

### 2. Breakable props rendered from models

- Amphorae (four), sanctuary lamp and blessing chalice: Meshy static models rendered by Claude in the cel style and the game's sun, front and three-quarter views where the game rotates them, plus their break pieces. Same sizes, same "telling things apart" colors ([rogue art-prompts](../rogue/art-prompts.md#telling-things-apart)).
- Interaction objects (Pardoner's stall, Confessional grille, reliquary chest, bell, sundial, shop pedestal) stay as they are unless Tomas asks; their lighting follows the new bake already.

### 3. HUD

- Lower left: the class portrait (V3's render) in a diamond frame; HP (red) and shield (blue) bars beside it with their frames; the Field of Blood and buff icons where they are today. Lower middle: ability slots as today. Lower right: the weapon panel with the weapon's side-view icon (cut from the existing `weapon-*-sheet.png` references) and the existing cooldown or charge display.
- Frames from H1–H3 ([prompts.md](prompts.md#h-hud-frames)); text in the existing Cinzel font.
- Respect the HUD rules: no element moves or resizes on interaction; ability slots and HP never hidden by art; readable over any background (a soft dark backing behind bars and numbers).
- Party frames for co-op in the same style.

Tests: the HUD model tests (`hudModel.test.ts`) for any changed logic; end-to-end HUD screenshots at 1 280 × 720 and 2 560 × 1 440 for overlap and clipping.

### 4. Performance pass

- Review every budget across all tiers and templates; fix the worst offenders; finalize LOD distances, bloom and particle scaling, and the Low tier's look.
- The first-run tier suggestion (spec §6) tuned on the reference laptop.
- **Benchmark run** (ask Tomas): the full gate on all tiers, back to back against the baseline build, recorded in [bench.md](../bench.md).

### 5. Acceptance

- Final screenshot set: every `look` view at every tier beside its concept, in `docs/look/v5-report.md`.
- Tomas's sessions: solo per class, co-op with mixed tiers, a full run to the Gatekeeper. Readability and fatigue questions as in R8–R9.
- Merge preparation like R9's: the exact branch SHA, evidence, outstanding issues. No merge or push without the separate instruction.

1. A whole run on your usual tier: does any part of the game still look like the old prototype?
2. HUD in a swarm and over a bright sky: HP, shield and ability slots readable at a glance?
3. Co-op with one player on Low and one on High: same fights, no advantage either way?
4. Break every prop type: do the pieces match the props?
