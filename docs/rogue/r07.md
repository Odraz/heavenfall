## As implemented (2026-10-23)

The contracts below record what was built against [bosses.md](bosses.md) §3; [r07-report.md](r07-report.md) has the checks and the play checklist. Protocol 20 → **21**, rules 27 → **28**. No save schema change. The fixed development dungeon's boss room runs the same encounter; it has no Watcher or gate/field anchors, so its Host uses every spawn point and no eyes open (its completion gate resolves at each c 28).

### Encounter state (stages 1–3, `src/sim/gatekeeperFight.ts`)

- **Seal:** `GatekeeperFight.seal` fixes P, `bossFactor(P)` (§3.4.1), max HP `52 000 × f(P)`, the add cap `floor(250 × mult(P))` and `cycleStart = tick`; it queues the seal wave on the field points. `Simulation.bossCast` is a view of the fight's single cast slot.
- **Schedule:** ticks from `cycleStart`, each scheduled event a consumed bit per cycle (`EV_*` in `src/data/gatekeeper.ts`): Host c 1; Call c 4 (deadline c 10); Tolls c 8 (deadline c 10) and c 15 (deadline c 17, and no sooner than 7 s after the previous impact); Watchers c 17 (phase 2, or from the first cycle under the Trumpet); Judgment c 25, which cancels any wind-up; Volley attempts c 3, 8, 13, 18, 23, skipped when busy, silenced or without sight. At c 28 the scheduled Judgment resolves (a broken or suppressed one still lets the eyes judge), the eye episode's deadline resolves, eyes close and `cycleStart += 840`. Silence breaks Judgment, cancels other wind-ups and holds new casts; events whose deadline passes meanwhile are consumed (a skipped Call resolves its episode).
- **Body damage:** `bodyAmount` refuses hits during the transition, clamps phase 1 at 50% and, while the completion gate is shut (a Call episode or a phase-2 aiming episode unresolved), leaves 1 HP. `bodyDamaged` fills Judgment's break (4.5% of max HP during the cast) and starts the transition. Tethers use the same clamps without break credit or procs (`Simulation.bossTether`).
- **Transition:** phase latches at 2; 45 ticks invulnerable; the cast, hold, rings, eyes, pending Judgments, hostile projectiles (and owed late shots) and pending add batches are removed without credit; at its end `cycleStart = tick − 510` with every pre-c 17 event consumed. If no Call had been attempted, one starts at once.
- **The Toll:** a ring is `{impact, hit bitset, per-player seen radius}`; two may roll at once. Each tick each living, unheld player is swept against the front between the radius their screen showed last tick and now (`tick − viewLag(p.view)`, at most 12 ticks back), and is hit once if the floor under them is at most base + 1 m and their feet are under 0.5 m above it. The header's `tollTick` is the announced impact during the tell, then the newest ring's.
- **Called:** target = the Fallen while he is taunted, else a random living player other than the last called while another lives; tell 1 s (`called` event `tell`), then the hold: actions canceled, any Carrier grab released, feet fixed at floor + 1.5 m, the Seal (`BOSS_PART` with `FLAG_PART_SEAL`) at `sealPoint` with 150 × the low-water living headcount. Each tick: rescale on a new lower headcount, `dragStep` 2.5 m/s toward him (stopping 2.6 m from his center), move the Seal; no legal Seal point releases without a heal. At 6 s he heals 10% of max HP (capped) and the player is set down; a Seal death releases them (`sealBroken`). Held input keeps aim, fire, client shots and non-movement Q/E (`MOVEMENT_ABILITIES`: Falling Star, Shadowstep, the Way Back and Gatebreaker are refused unspent); positions are ignored. Start and release are `teleport` events carrying `hold: [x, y, startTick]` and `hold: null`.
- **Watchers:** 3 + living players; one at the nearest free anchor per living player, the rest random (sim RNG). Each must be within 20 m and sight of a living human's eye (living bots when no human lives), else it moves along the anchor-to-player line to a clear hover point 8–20 m from them, else to the nearest clear point at least 2 m from them; 2 m apart, the whole cylinder clear. 60 HP, grey and refused for 2 s (`FLAG_PART_OPENING`). Each kill sends `8% × maxHp / count` to the body once, after the aiming predicate is updated. Judgment's end captures the open eyes; a player he doesn't see but an eye within 24 m does takes half their max HP.
- **View-time resolution:** each pending Judgment resolves per player once that player's view reaches its tick, at most 12 ticks late. Lethal body Judgment is `damagePlayer(…, lethal)`: invulnerability is checked first, then HP plus shield go through Hide, and a shield still bursts.
- **Volley ledger:** a globe carries its volley id (`pVolley`, late shots too); a volley hurts a player once, direct or by shatter.
- **Parts and the crowd:** `BOSS_PART` (type 5, one 0.6 × 1.2 m cylinder for both kinds) and the body are `isBossBody`: outside `living`, the room's alive count, separation, recovery, director intensity, statuses, launches, Chains and every talent and relic pick. Area damage (a hit with a source point) does half, a censer's direct hit full. Part deaths grant no XP, halos, motes, streak or procs. Eight enemy slots stay free for parts while the boss lives (adds and the bench top-up stop at 1 492).
- **Adds:** spawn batches gain `boss`, `only` (spawn-point indices) and `arrive` (placed evenly over 6 s). A new Host replaces the previous Host's unplaced batch; the add cap holds; boss adds drop at most eight motes in the fight.
- **Victory:** `checkVictory` calls `GatekeeperFight.end` first (cast, hold, eyes, rings and pending Judgments canceled, a held player released safely), then R6's settlement unchanged; `bossPhase {phase: 0}` carries the line.

### Wire (protocol 21)

Header bytes 71–95: `bossPhase` u8 (1 or 2, +128 invulnerable), `bossBreak` u8, `bossFlags` u8 (1 deferred, 2 Watchers open), `bossCycleStart` i32, `tollTick` u32, `heldPlayer` u8, `callTarget` u8, `heldUntil` u32, `heldSince` u32, `bossTransitionUntil` u32 (`HEADER_BYTES` 71 → 96, in every part and the reassembly check). Part kinds ride the enemy record's flags (128 the Seal, 2 opening; the decoder accepts 128 only on `BOSS_PART`). Events: `bossCast` gains `cast` and the Toll's `impactTick`; new `bossPhase`, `hostDescend`, `called`, `sealBroken`, `judged`, `watchersOpen`, `watcherBreak`. Reconnect needs no extra sync: the host keeps the encounter, the header and enemy records rebuild the presentation, and a held client re-enters its hold from the header.

### Client and bots (stages 4–5)

- `src/client/gatekeeperView.ts`: the ring band (solid gold 0.5 m, faint 2 m wisps) sampled on the floors it hurts at the screen's drawn tick, the tell band, coded eye and padlock sprites, the chain from his keys through the Seal (ending at the Seal in the held player's own view), gold threads from eyes that see you; pure `ringContactIn`, `ringHeights`, `sightState`, `sightWindow`.
- `LocalPlayer.hold`: the held screen predicts `dragStep` every frame from the event's position, caught up by the event's delay; movement abilities do nothing; any teleport ends it.
- HUD: `The Gatekeeper II`, a grey bar in the transition, cast labels (Judgment, The Toll, Called to the Gate, Orb Volley), Judgment's red break fill, *Break the seals of Judgment*, the crosshair eye (every 100 ms from c 22 to c 28 and while eyes are open), the held player's line, cues under the bar, the party frame's gold rim; Judgment's pre-warning cue and drone at c 22; a whoosh 1 s before the ring.
- Bots: jump when the front (or birth disk) is within 0.22 s at the drawn tick; from c 22 head for the nearest reachable cell his eye can't see (breadth-first, recomputed per cycle); target any Seal in range, then eyes that see them, then eyes that see a human; never a grey eye.
- Dev co-op setup: a `dev=1` multiplayer host's `seed=` and `room=` fix the floor and start every client at that room's entry (`RunConfig.devStartRoom`; never with saved progression).
