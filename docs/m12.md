# M12: The horde

The second playtest ([playtests/2026-10-08.md](playtests/2026-10-08.md)) found the game on the right track but not yet fun. Fighting was "back up and hold the trigger": the horde merged into one crowd behind the party, being caught was harmless, kills didn't show, and most classes had no way to clear a crowd.

The horde stays: very large masses of enemies are the game. This milestone makes **clearing them fun, with a real risk of being overwhelmed**, before any new enemy types or threats are added. It does four things:

1. **The horde surrounds the party, paced by a director** (§2): bigger waves that keep arriving for 10 s from wherever the party isn't, started by a director that watches how hard-pressed the party is, as in Left 4 Dead: build up, peak, relax, repeat.
2. **Being caught is dangerous** (§3): players wade through crowds, and the Blessed strike sooner.
3. **Every class clears a stack its own way** (§5), each in its own shape:

   | Class | Shape | Its way of clearing a stack |
   |---|---|---|
   | Fallen | **circle around itself** | Taunts the crowd onto itself, then the shotgun at point blank; Falling Star slams down onto a stack anywhere, or at its own feet, and crushes its middle. |
   | Binder | **arc in front** | Chains pulls a crowd into a pile; the Scourge sweeps everything in front. |
   | Betrayer | **line** | Silver Bullet through ten in a line; Shadowstep cuts a lane through the crowd. |
   | Heretic | **area** | Incense on a crowd that stays put; the shield's blast around a swarmed ally. |

   The Fallen and the Binder **stack** crowds; the Betrayer and the Heretic **cash them in**; Falling Star onto a Binder's pile kills the whole pile.
4. **Kills show** (§4): sudden big damage **bursts** an enemy, blasting it back and tearing it apart (a Blessed torn in half, a Cherub losing its wings) in a burst of light and feathers, with a heavier burst for the heaviest hits; your own kills are confirmed on your screen at once; your big moments shake the screen. And players can read their own state (§6).

Out of scope until this core plays well: elites and new enemy types, changes to ranged enemies, co-op markers (§12).

The design intent is in [classes.md](classes.md). All numbers are **initial values**.

## 2. The horde arrives

Replaces MVP §8.2 item 2 (waves) for the three combat arenas. The boss arena (its single wave and the Gatekeeper's summons), the sandbox and the benchmark are unchanged. `noWaves` disables the director.

### 2.1 Waves

More, bigger waves (values for 4 players):

| Arena | Waves (Blessed / Choristers / Cherubs) | Total |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Courtyard of Clouds | 150 · 200 · 250 · 300 | 900 |
| 2. The Cloudbridge | 200 + 10 Cherubs · 250 + 15 · 300 + 15 · 350 + 20 | 1 160 |
| 3. Cloister of Hymns | 250 / 10 / 10 · 300 / 15 / 15 · 350 / 15 / 15 · 400 / 20 / 15 · 450 / 20 / 20 | 1 905 |

Each arena's `waves` array holds these; party-size scaling is as today.

### 2.2 From all sides, over 10 s

- **Far from the party:** in the three combat arenas, a wave's enemies are placed only on spawn points more than **15 m** from every living player (`distToCylinder`, like the 8 m rule). If none are, the 8 m rule applies as today. The boss arena (its wave and the summons), the sandbox and `benchArena` keep the 8 m rule.
- **Arriving over 10 s:** in the three combat arenas, a wave's enemies are placed at an even pace over **10 s**: the arena gains `waveTotal / 300` placements per tick (the scaled wave total over 10 s), spent round-robin on the eligible points, each point still at most 10 per second as today. Budget left when the wave is fully placed is dropped. Eligibility is checked every tick, so the wave keeps arriving from wherever the party isn't, including ahead of a party backing away. The queue and type order are as today.
- **Why:** placed all at once, a wave reached the floor in a few seconds and its streams merged into one column chasing the party, which then backed away from it. Arriving over 10 s, fresh streams keep coming from the far side of wherever the party goes. Later waves arrive faster (Arena 3's last at about 49 per second at 4 players), so escalation is pressure, not just length.

### 2.3 The director

Decides **when** each wave starts, from how hard-pressed the party is. It doesn't choose what spawns or where, and it doesn't change an arena's total. It runs on the host only (`src/sim/director.ts`), per combat arena, from the arena sealing to its last wave starting.

**Intensity.** Each living player has an intensity from 0 to 100, measuring how hard-pressed they are:
- **Damage taken:** on each `damagePlayer` hit, `+ 150 × lost ÷ max HP`, where *lost* is the shield absorbed plus `min(damage to HP, HP before the hit)` (HP goes negative on a lethal hit). Losing 10% of max HP adds 15.
- **Kills close by:** `+ 1` for each enemy removed while alive (`removeEnemy`, the dev key's `killAll` included, the victory sweep not) within **5 m** horizontally of the player, whoever killed it.
- **Decay:** while the player is **not engaged**, `− 20` per second (20/30 per tick). A player is engaged while a living enemy of any kind (Cherubs included, the Gatekeeper never in these arenas) is within **8 m** horizontally, or a `damagePlayer` hit removed their HP or shield in the last 30 ticks. (Not `lastDamageTick`: revive and respawn set it.)
- Clamped to 0–100. Every intensity is set to 0 when an arena seals and when its player dies; dead players don't count. The **party's intensity** is the highest among the living players.
- **Order:** intensity changes as damage and deaths happen; decay is applied once per tick; the director evaluates once per tick in `updateSpawning`, before placement.

**Phases:**

| Phase | Starts | Ends |
|---|---|---|
| **Build up** | A wave is queued. | On a tick when the wave has fully spawned and either the arena's living enemies are at **30%** of the wave's total or fewer (→ the next wave starts at once: a party clearing faster than it's pressed gets the next stack straight away), or the party's intensity is at least **70** (→ peak; it may have reached 70 earlier). If both hold, the 30% rule wins. |
| **Peak** | Intensity reached 70, or a player died. | After **4 s** (→ relax). No wave starts during it. |
| **Relax** | After the peak. | Once the latest wave has fully spawned: when the party's intensity has stayed below **25** for **2 s** (60 ticks) in a row, counted from the start of relax, or **10 s** have passed in relax while the arena's living enemies are at most **30%** of the latest wave's total (→ the next wave starts). |

- **Wave 1** starts when the arena seals, in build up.
- **A death is a peak:** when a living player dies, the director goes to peak (from build up or relax; in peak, its 4 s restart), unless the last wave has started. The party gets its window to revive them instead of a new wave.
- **The living cap:** a wave never starts while the arena's living enemies exceed the arena's **largest wave** (each type scaled with `scaleCount`, then summed); the director waits in its phase until they don't.
- **A party that neither kills nor gets hurt** (backing away out of reach) stays in build up until it fights, and relax's 10 s limit waits while most of the last wave is alive. That's intended: no timer pushes more waves onto a party that isn't fighting the one it has.
- **After the last wave starts**, the director stops. The arena clears as today, when every wave has fully spawned and nothing is alive.
- **Enemies remaining** (HUD) and clearing are unchanged: living enemies plus enemies of waves not yet spawned.
- The old rule (the next wave at 20% or 20 s) and its constants are removed.

**So:** after a wave's last contact, intensity falls below 25 in about 4 s, then 2 s of quiet; the next wave's glows and approach add about 4 s more. About 10 s without contact: enough for Communion (8 s) and most cooldowns, not dead time. A wave cycle takes about 23 s; arenas about 90, 95 and 120 s at 4 players.

**Debug readout.** The director lives in the worker, so the worker adds `director: {phase, phaseTime, intensity, wave, alive, waveTotal}` (the wave 0-based, `phaseTime` in seconds) to each `snap` message for the local client. With `?dev=1`, the F3 debug overlay (`debugOverlay.ts`) shows a line `Director: <phase> <phaseTime> s · <intensity> · <alive>/<waveTotal>` (for example `Director: relax 6.2 s · 23 · 41/250`), on the host and in single player, and `debugState.director` holds the object.

### 2.4 The spawn glow

Each enemy that appears comes out of a golden glow. Client only.

- When an enemy slot appears that wasn't in the previous snapshot, within **2 m** horizontally of a spawn point of the snapshot's `arenaIndex` (`map.arenaSpawnPoints`, cell centers), that point **flares** when the enemy appears on screen (after the render delay).
- **A flaring point** shows a gold glow sprite (the *glow* texture, `0xffd27a`) 1.0 m above its floor, radius 1.6 m, at opacity 0.85, fading to 0 over **0.8 s** after its last flare. At most once per **0.4 s** per point, a flare also draws a gold ground ring (`vfx.ring`, `0xffd27a`, 0.3 → 1.6 m over 400 ms). Over a wave's 10 s, the glows show where it's coming from.
- Gatekeeper summons glow the same way.

### 2.5 A breath in single player

Replaces MVP §5.7's singleplayer regeneration while a combat arena is in combat (sealed, not cleared): regeneration runs **only while its director is in relax**, at **5%** of max HP per second after **3 s** without damage (was 1.5% after 8 s, at any time), so none after its last wave starts. From about 10% of the HP in a short relax up to about a third in a 10 s one comes back, and none while kiting. Otherwise (the boss arena, the corridors, after a clear) regeneration is as today.

## 3. Being caught is dangerous

### 3.1 Wading

Players wade through crowds: each ground enemy pressing against a player slows them.

- **Count:** each frame, the local player counts the living ground enemies of the **newest snapshot** (the interpolated ones lag by the render delay) whose center is within `PLAYER_RADIUS + enemy radius + 0.45 m` horizontally of the player's center (1.2 m for a Blessed, the melee range), and whose feet are within 1.5 m vertically of the player's feet. Snapshots carry no enemy height, so an enemy's feet are `groundHeight(map, x, y, radius, Infinity, false, true)` at its newest-snapshot position, as `enemyHeights` does, computed only for enemies that pass the horizontal test. **Not counted:** stunned enemies (`FLAG_STUNNED`, §5.3) and launched enemies: those listed in a `starLanded`, for 0.4 s from the event's arrival.
- **Slow:** the target factor is `max(0.55, 1 − 0.15 × count)`. The applied factor eases toward it: `factor += (target − factor) × min(1, dt / 0.1)`. It multiplies the class speed for walking, in the air as on the ground. Jumping height is unchanged.
- **So:** three Blessed hold the Fallen and the Binder (3.3 m/s against the Blessed's 4); the Heretic (4.4) and the Betrayer (4.95) can wade out. The Binder escapes after a Scourge kills the Blessed holding it.
- **Not slowed:** the Shadowstep dash and the Falling Star leap, the classes' ways through a crowd.
- **Client only.** Movement is the client's; the host's speed check only caps from above. The bot moves through the same code. The count also runs in the benchmark (§10).

### 3.2 The Blessed strike sooner

- `MELEE_FIRST` = **0.25 s** (was 0.5). With the existing per-tick countdown, the first strike lands on the **8th tick in range** (0.267 s); then every 1 s, 5 dmg, as now.
- **The attack animation** keeps the blow on frame 4, so it starts **267 ms into its cycle** when the state becomes `attacking`: `t = (now − stateSince + 267) mod 1000`.

## 4. Kills that show

### 4.1 Burst deaths (host)

Sudden big damage kills differently: the enemy is blasted apart.

- **Force.** `damageEnemy(slot, amount, source, force = amount)`: every hit has a force, doubled with the damage when the enemy is bound. Exceptions:
  - a **Silver Bullet** hit's force is what the bullet still carried on reaching the enemy, doubled if bound (`silverBulletHits` deals each enemy exactly the HP it has left, so its damage alone would never burst anything);
  - force **Infinity** for: a **Brimstone Shotgun** pellet hitting an enemy within **6 m** (`distToCylinder` from the Fallen's body center, the knockback range); a **Scourge** hit; a **Falling Star** hit within the crater's **3.5 m**; a **Shroud burst** hit. These are the heaviest blows in the game and always burst what they kill.
- The host sums the force of the hits each enemy takes **in the current tick**. An enemy credited to a player dies a **burst death** when that sum, including the killing hit, is at least **2 × its type's max HP** (40 for a Blessed, 120 for a Chorister, 60 for a Cherub), and a **heavy** one when it's at least **4 ×** (80, 240, 120; Infinity included); otherwise a **light** one. The Gatekeeper never bursts. Kills without credit (the victory sweep, `killAll`) are normal deaths.
- **So, on a Blessed:**
  - **heavy:** the shotgun within 6 m, the Scourge, the crater, a Shroud burst, and a Silver Bullet's first 7 (force 200 down to 80);
  - **light:** the revolver, the slug, a censer impact, the Shadowstep cut, and the Silver Bullet's 8th and 9th (60, 40);
  - **normal:** Chain Gun bullets, incense, the shotgun beyond 6 m and the Silver Bullet's 10th (20).
- **Direction**, in whole degrees 0–359, the horizontal angle the body is blasted: from the Shroud burst's center, or from the Falling Star landing point, to the enemy; for the Shadowstep cut, the direction of the dash segment that cut it; otherwise from the credited player's position to the enemy. If the two points are less than 1e-6 m apart horizontally, the angle is `(slot % 16) × 22.5°`, as `knockAway` does.
- **Event `bursts`** (§9): once per tick when any enemy burst that tick, listing `[slot, angle, playerId]` triples (the credited player). A heavy burst's angle is sent **plus 360** (360–719), so no field is added.

### 4.2 Burst deaths (client)

- On `bursts`, the client remembers each slot's angle, tier and player for **1 s**, and forgets it early if that slot appears in a snapshot as a new enemy (slots are reused after 1 s). Events reach remote clients every tick on the reliable channel, before the death plays after the render delay.
- `pendingBursts` entries carry their **slot**, and the corpse is created when the death **plays**, not when the snapshot arrives. When it plays, if the slot is remembered, a **burst death** plays and the slot is forgotten; otherwise the normal death plays as now, with its corpse. **A burst death replaces the normal death's `featherBurst`, chirp and corpse.**
- **The ripple:** within one `bursts` event, the bursts credited to the same player play with an extra delay of `90 ms × (dᵢ − d_min) / (d_max − d_min)` (0 when all are equal), where *d* is each enemy's distance from that player (for the local player, its own position; for others, their interpolated position). A Silver Bullet tears its line front to back, a crater blows outward, a Scourge sweeps; the work is spread over frames.
- **A burst death**, with *d* the unit vector of the angle, *p* the unit vector perpendicular to it (a random one of the two), and *r* the camera's right:

  | | Light | Heavy |
  |---|---|---|
  | **Blasted back (0–80 ms):** the body flies along *d*, lifted, and sideways along *r* by ±0.25 m (random sign), easing out | 0.6 m, lift 0.25 m | 1.0 m, lift 0.45 m |
  | It swells to | 1.15× | 1.3× |
  | **The burst sprite** grows (cubic ease-out over 220 ms) | 0.9 → 1.3 m | 1.2 → 1.9 m |
  | **Feathers** (before the budget below) | 16 | 30 |
  | **Sparks** | 6 | 14 |
  | **Torn pieces' speed** (§4.2.1) | × 0.7 | × 1 |

  1. **Blasted back (0–80 ms):** the enemy's billboard, on its first pain frame (every enemy type has one), drawn from the death's position and height (a Cherub stays at its hover height and flies level) and facing `animator.facing[slot]` with the direction chosen as for corpses, moves as in the table and turns white-gold: glow `{1, 0.78, 0.32}` with `a` rising from 0.35 to **0.75** (never 1, so the ink outline stays and it doesn't vanish into a white crowd or cream stone).
  2. **Bursts (at 80 ms),** at the end point **moved 0.6 m horizontally toward the local camera and 0.25 m up** (cosmetic, per client: the shooter looks along *d*, so the blast would otherwise land behind the next row):
     - **the burst sprite:** `fx-burst` at that point, on a random one of its 4 rotations (§4.2.1), glow `{1, 0.95, 0.8, a: 0.25}` (so the baked light doesn't dull it), at full opacity until 110 ms, then dithered out by 220 ms; it doesn't shrink at the end of its life like other particles (its size is the 220 ms growth). **Its height is at most 0.38 × its horizontal distance to the camera** (about a quarter of the screen's height), so bursts at the player's feet never white out the view;
     - **the torn pieces** (§4.2.1);
     - **feathers:** velocity `d × U(2, 5)` plus `p × U(1, 3)`, `vz` `U(2, 4.5)`, life 1.4 s, size 0.32 m, with the feathers' existing drag and fall;
     - **sparks:** `d × U(3, 7)` plus `p × U(1.5, 4)`, `vz` `U(2.5, 5.5)`, life 0.6 s, size 0.22 m, glow `{1, 0.62, 0.2, a: 0.45}` (hotter than the gold world);
     - the new sound **`burstPop`** (§4.3) instead of the chirp, at most one per 40 ms; deaths in between are silent.
- **The feather budget:** with *n* the burst deaths played in the last 0.5 s (from everyone), feathers and sparks are multiplied by 1 when *n* ≤ 8, by 0.5 when *n* is 9–24, and by 0.25 above 24; and by a further 0.5 beyond 20 m from the camera; rounded, at least 4 feathers and 2 sparks. The burst sprite and the torn pieces always play.
- No blood ([lore.md](lore.md)): Heaven's bodies break like porcelain with light inside; the brutality is force, torn bodies, light and sound. The particle pool (`MAX_PARTICLES = 4000`) and its overwrite-the-oldest rule are unchanged for feathers and sparks.

#### 4.2.1 Torn pieces

- **The Blessed's pieces are rendered, not painted,** so they match the Blessed in the game: a new `shreds` mode of `scripts/blender/blessed.py` renders them from the same model, materials, toon shading and ink outline as its atlas, in the `idle` pose:
  - **The cut** is at **0.95 m** above the feet, just below the sash. Only objects crossing it are cut (the pelvis, sash, buckle and skirt panels). Objects on the arm, shoulder and hand bones go whole to the upper half. The grip, pommel, guard and blade go only to the sword piece. The halo is hidden.
  - **How:** a Boolean modifier (Exact solver, material Transfer) with a zigzag cutter (about 6 teeth, ±0.06 m), inserted after each object's `rig` and `thick` modifiers and before its `outline` modifier. The cut faces get a new `cut` material: a flat emissive `#ffd27a` (`EMISSIVE`), with a 0.008 m white (`#ffffff`) emissive band along the cut edge: cracked porcelain with light inside.
  - **Three pieces**, each rendered on its own: the **upper half** tilted **35°** about the left–right axis, bottom toward the camera, and the **lower half** tilted 35° top toward the camera, so the gold cut faces the camera (the sprite camera looks down only 8°, so untilted the cut would be edge-on); the **sword** alone, laid diagonally.
  - **Output:** each render, with the `ink_frame` contour, is composited onto flat `#00FF00` and saved as `assets/art-src/shred-blessed-upper.png`, `shred-blessed-lower.png` and `shred-blessed-sword.png`, so `cutout.py` keys them like the painted sprites. `shred-blessed.json` holds `{piece: {heightM}}`: each cropped render's height ÷ 190 px per meter. These heights are the Blessed rows' drawn sizes in the table below.
- **Art:** the five `shred-*` images and `fx-burst`, cut out by `scripts/cutout.py` like the other world sprites. The script also stores each in **4 rotations** as separate frames named `<name>-r0` … `<name>-r3` (r1 = 90° clockwise), since billboards can't rotate in place; those names go in `BILLBOARD_SPRITES`. Sizes in the atlas, as the 0° frame's longer side: the Blessed's halves and sword 160 px, the wing 128 px, the wing tip 96 px, the burst 256 px.
- **Drawn size:** a piece's size is its 0° frame's drawn height; each rotation is drawn with `height = size × (that frame's height ÷ the 0° frame's height)`, so a piece keeps its size as it turns.
- **Pieces per burst:**

  | Enemy | Pieces (drawn size) | Speed along *d* | Sideways (*p*) | `vz` | Turns every |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Blessed | upper half, lower half (`shred-blessed.json`), sword | halves `U(2, 4)`, sword `U(3, 6)` | `U(1.0, 2.5)`: the upper half along +*p*, the lower along −*p* | upper `U(3, 5)`, lower `U(1.5, 3)`, sword `U(3.5, 5.5)` | halves 120 ms, sword 60 ms |
  | Cherub | 1 wing (0.55 m), 1 wing tip (0.35 m) | `U(3, 6)` | `U(1.0, 2.5)`, random sign | `U(2, 4.5)` | 80 ms |
  | Chorister | none: the burst and the feathers only | | | | |

  The upper half starts at the body center + 0.2 m, the lower half at − 0.2 m, the others at the body center (all moved toward the camera as in §4.2 step 2); each starts on a random rotation.
- **Flight:** a new particle kind in its **own ring of 512 slots** inside `Particles` (same draw), shared with the burst sprites, so feathers never overwrite them. Gravity 12 m/s², no drag. It **tumbles**: it cycles through its 4 rotations at the table's pace, in a random direction.
- **Landing:** each frame a flying piece reads the floor under its current position (`floorAt(x, y)`; none over a wall cell, outside the map, or where `overVoid`). It lands when its bottom (`z − drawn height ÷ 2` of its current frame) reaches that floor; it then rests at `floor + drawn height ÷ 2` and stops turning, and a half switches to the next 90° or 270° frame in its tumble direction, so it lies on its side. With no floor under it, it keeps falling. Its life is **2.5 s**, shrinking over the last 40% like every particle.

### 4.3 Sounds of the slaughter

- **A limiter:** `audio.ts` puts a `DynamicsCompressorNode` before the destination (threshold −12 dB, knee 6, ratio 6, attack 0.003 s, release 0.15 s), so 24 voices at once don't clip. `audio.play` gets a `rate` argument (playback rate, default 1); a play with an explicit `rate` gets no random pitch variation (`PITCH_VARIATION`), so the ladders below stay exact.
- **`burstPop`**, three layers in the `part()` format of `sfx.ts`; your own bursts play at `PRIO_ABILITY`, others' at `PRIO_OTHERS`:
  - the crack (porcelain): `noise 14000, a .001, d .02, s .2, r .06, v .55, lp 11000→3000`; `sine 3150, d .02, s .25, r .12, v .10, vib 40/1`; `sine 4730, delay .01, d .02, s .2, r .09, v .08`;
  - the body: `sine 140→42, d .07, s .35, r .14, v .75`; `square 220→70, d .04, s .2, r .08, v .18, lp 1200, bits 5`;
  - the flutter: `noise 7000, delay .03, a .02, d .08, s .35, hold .08, r .15, v .22, lp 5000→1500, vib 26/10`.
- **Mass kills:** the client keeps the times of the burst deaths it plays (at the burst, step 2). When one bursts and at least **6** (counting it) have burst in the last **0.15 s**, and no mass kill was triggered in the last **1.0 s**, a mass kill is **triggered** and plays **0.1 s later** at that position, so it can count the rest of the ripple. Its count *c* is the burst deaths played from 0.15 s before the trigger until it plays; the 1.0 s lockout starts at the trigger. It plays **`massKill`**:
  - sub hit `sine 75→28, d .18, s .5, hold .05, r .35, v .85`; blast `noise 9000, d .1, s .35, r .35, v .5, lp 4000→300`; flock `noise 9000, delay .05, a .05, hold .15, r .3, v .25, lp 9000→3000, vib 22/12`; shimmer sines at 1568, 2093 and 2637 Hz with delays .04, .08, .12, `r .35`, volumes .08, .07, .06;
  - scaled by the window's count *c*: gain `min(1, 0.7 + 0.015 × (c − 6))`, rate `1 − 0.01 × min(c − 6, 20)` (bigger is deeper);
  - it's **your mass kill** if at least 4 of the burst deaths in that window were credited to you: it plays without position at `PRIO_ABILITY` and shakes (§4.5); otherwise at its position at `PRIO_OTHERS`.

### 4.4 Your kills, at once

Bursts appear after a round trip and the render delay. The shooter's own screen confirms hits and kills at once, from the same local ray tests that drive the hit marker (and the local Scourge arc test). Cosmetic and local: other players don't see it, and the host is unaffected; if the host disagrees, the enemy just recovers.

- **Predicted kill:** a predicted hit on a non-Gatekeeper enemy whose damage is at least its type's max HP (the damage doubled if the enemy has `FLAG_ROOTED`). A shotgun pellet counts with its damage at that distance (§5.1), and within one shot an enemy predicted killed by an earlier pellet is skipped by later pellets, as on the host. The Chain Gun never predicts a kill. **Predicted burst:** a predicted kill by the shotgun within 6 m, the Scourge, or damage at least 2× the type's max HP.
- **The Silver Bullet:** `estimateSilverBullet` also returns each reached enemy's carried amount. Every enemy it reaches with at least its full HP left in the bullet (doubled if rooted) is a predicted kill, and a predicted burst when the carried amount (doubled if rooted) is at least 2× its type's max HP.
- **Hit flinch** (every predicted hit): the enemy's billboard is pushed **0.15 m** along the shot's horizontal direction (for the Scourge, away from the Binder) plus 0.05 m along the camera's right, squashed to **×1.10 wide, ×0.92 tall**, with glow `{1, 1, 1, a: 0.4}` for 50 ms, easing back over **120 ms**.
- **Kill flinch** (a predicted kill): pushed **0.3 m**, lifted 0.12 m, squashed ×1.15 / ×0.88, glow `{1, 0.8, 0.4, a: 0.6}`, held 60 ms, then easing back over 140 ms. On a remote client this reads as hit, flash, then about 200 ms later the pop, like a fuse.
- **Kill marker and `killTick`** play **on the predicted kill**; the snapshot-driven kill marker and its `killTick` are skipped for 400 ms after a predicted one. A predicted burst shows the **burst marker**: the ticks starting 10 px from the center instead of 7, gold, for 160 ms. `killTick` climbs a pitch ladder: `rate = 2^(min(k, 7) / 12)`, with *k* your predicted kills in the last 0.5 s.
- At most **64** enemies flinch at once; a new flinch on a full list replaces the oldest. A new hit on a flinching enemy restarts it.

### 4.5 Screen shake

Only for big moments the local player causes or stands in; never for ordinary shots (recoil does that).

| Event | Shake | Decays over |
|---|---|---|
| Your Falling Star landing | 1.6°, plus a 1.2° downward pitch dip | 350 ms |
| A Shroud burst within 6 m of you (any Heretic's) | 0.7° | 200 ms |
| Your Blasphemy | 0.5° | 200 ms |
| Your mass kill (§4.3) | 0.5° + 0.02° per burst beyond 6, at most 1.0° | 180 ms |

- The shake offsets the **view only** (camera yaw, pitch and roll), not the aim: shots and abilities use the player's own yaw and pitch. The envelope is `amplitude × (1 − t / decay)²`. Each axis follows noise in −1…1 with new targets every 30 ms, interpolated between them (not stepped); roll is 0.6× the amplitude. Overlapping shakes add, capped at 2.0°.
- **Setting:** a **Screen shake** on/off toggle in the Settings view (with the volume sliders, on Title and in Pause), on by default, saved in `localStorage`. The panel's height is fixed when Settings opens (`volume.ts`), so the toggle row must fit without resizing or moving anything. The setting is read from `localStorage` when each shake starts, so changing it in Pause takes effect at once.
- **Triggers:** your landing when your leap ends; your Blasphemy on its press, locally; a Shroud burst on `shroudBurst`, after the render delay; your mass kill as §4.3.

## 5. Every class clears a stack

### 5.1 The Fallen's shotgun

- **20 dmg** per pellet on an enemy within **6 m** (`distToCylinder` from the Fallen's body center, the knockback and burst range), **10 dmg** beyond (was 12 everywhere). Spread **±11°** yaw (was ±8°; pitch ±4° as now), **0.9 s** between shots (was 0.8). 8 pellets, range 20 m, knockback as now.
- Up close, each pellet kills a Blessed and the next pellet carries on to the Blessed behind it: a point-blank shot bursts 6–8. Beyond 6 m a Blessed needs two pellets and nothing bursts: about 2–3 kills per shot. Backing away and holding the trigger is the weak way to play the Fallen; the slug is its tool at range.

### 5.2 Falling Star: aim anywhere (Fallen, E)

**Controls.**
- **Hold E** while Falling Star is ready and the Fallen is alive: the **preview** appears and follows the aim every frame. A key-down while it cools down never starts a preview, even if the cooldown ends while E is held.
- **Release E** to leap: on the first frame `Input.isDown('KeyE')` is false, compute that frame's landing point and leap if it's valid. If it isn't, nothing happens and there's no cooldown.
- **Cancel** with the right mouse button while holding E: the preview disappears until E is pressed again, and the client clears both `FIRE_RIGHT` and `FIRE_RIGHT_LAST` until the right button is released, so that press doesn't fire the slug.
- Death, Pause, the chat line and losing focus cancel the preview without a leap. They call `Input.release()`, which clears the keys; `Input` counts its `release()` calls, and a frame on which the count changed cancels instead of releasing. The left button keeps firing the shotgun while aiming.

**The landing point**, each frame:
1. **The aim point.** Cast the aim ray from the eye with a new `raycastTerrainHit(map, o, d, maxDist)` returning `{t, side}`: `side` is true when the ray hits at a cell's entry (a wall's side, where `los.ts` finds `z0 < top`) and false when it descends onto a top or floor inside a cell. `maxDist = 30 / max(cos(pitch), 0.01)`.
   - A floor or top within **30 m** horizontally: that point.
   - A wall's side within 30 m: the point **0.5 m** back along the ray horizontally, on the floor of the cell there.
   - Nothing, or only farther than 30 m: the point **30 m** horizontally along the aim's yaw, on the floor of the cell there.
2. **Ally snap.** If a living ally's feet are within **2.5 m** horizontally of the aim point, and `lineOfSight` holds from the Fallen's eye to that ally's body center within 30 m, the landing point is that ally's interpolated feet, and the gold chevron shows on them. (Today's wide ally-targeting cone would steal leaps aimed at a crowd near a teammate.)

**A slam in place:** a landing point under or next to the Fallen is valid (a 0.4 s hop): taunt the crowd onto yourself, then slam it. With no horizontal distance the arc has no samples to check.

**Valid** (gold-orange) when both hold; otherwise **invalid** (red):
- **The landing cell is walkable**: it's in the set of cells reachable on foot from any spawn point of any arena, found by a breadth-first search with the ground step rule (up at most `STEP_UP` 0.5 m, down any height; diagonals need both orthogonal steps, as `stepAllowed`), where door cells (`doorArena ≥ 0`) count as open at their `map.floor`, and solid and void cells are never walkable. Computed once per map on the client. The Gatekeeper's dais and the decoration pedestals have no stairs, so they aren't walkable, nor is any perch reached only by jumping.
- **The arc is clear.** The arc runs from the Fallen's feet to the landing point: horizontally a straight line, in height `z₀ + (z₁ − z₀) × u + 4H × u(1 − u)`, with `H = 1.5 + 0.08 × d` (*d* the horizontal distance; 3.9 m at 30 m). Sampled every 0.5 m along the line, each consecutive pair of samples, raised by 0.9 m (the body center), must have `lineOfSight` on the client's live map. A wall or a closed door in the way makes it invalid: **the Fallen never leaps into a wall.**

**The preview** (only the Fallen sees it):
- **The arc:** a camera-facing strip of 24 segments (Sacrament's `Ribbon`, through a new persistent `Vfx` method), 0.12 m wide, *beam* texture, `0xf08a24` when valid and `0xd02020` when invalid, opacity 0.8.
- **The landing rings:** two flat rings on the landing point's floor while aiming (a new persistent ground ring using `groundGeo` and the *ring* texture, updated each frame): the crater, **3.5 m**, at opacity 0.8, and the reach, **6 m**, at opacity 0.4, in the arc's color.

**The leap.**
- The client moves the Fallen along the arc over **0.4 s**, ignoring collision, as today's leap does along a line; then it's grounded on the landing cell. A landing within 0.4 m of a wall is fine: collision lets a body move out of a cell it overlaps.
- **For the Fallen and the Betrayer, `ePresses` counts executed movement abilities only:** the client increments it when it starts a leap or a dash, never on a key-down that doesn't. The Heretic and the Binder count every E key-down, as now. `allyTargetId` is the snapped ally, or none.
- **Host:** accepts the press when the cooldown is within the grace (0.25 s), **without needing an ally**. **`landingTick` = acceptance + 15 ticks (0.5 s)**, and the Fallen is invulnerable until then (the leap takes 0.4 s; the extra 0.1 s lets the input with the final position arrive). Cooldown **10 s** (was 15). The speed-check skip as now. `abilityUsed` E is at the Fallen's position, with `targets` the ally if it's valid by today's host check, otherwise empty.
- **The landing**, at the Fallen's latest accepted position, **on the floor under it**: distances are measured from `(x, y, groundHeight + 0.9)`, so a late input caught mid-arc still puts the crater on the ground:
  - **40 dmg** to every enemy within **3.5 m**, and **10 dmg** to every enemy between 3.5 and **6 m** (distance to the cylinder). 40 bursts a Blessed (force Infinity, heavy, §4.1); bound enemies take 80 and 20.
  - Survivors within 6 m are knocked back 4 m over 0.4 s and launched, as now (not rooted enemies, not the Gatekeeper).
- **Landing effects** move to the landing itself: the Fallen's own client draws the ring (now to 6 m) and glow, plays `fallingStarLand` and shakes (§4.5) when its leap ends; other clients do it on `starLanded`, after the render delay.

**Bot:** leaps only to an ally target, at once, without the hold, as now.

### 5.3 Blasphemy stuns (Fallen, Q)

The taunt stays (12 m, 5 s, cooldown 12 s). New: **every enemy it taunts, except the Gatekeeper, is stunned for 1 s.** It's a visual beat more than a weapon: the crowd flinches ("what was that?!"), sees the Fallen, and comes for it. Stunned enemies don't count for wading (§3.1), so a swarmed ally walks out while the crowd turns.

**Stunned (host):** `eStunUntil`, like the other statuses (a new stun takes the later end). While stunned, an enemy:
- doesn't steer: it still calls the movement step with zero intent, so gravity, Cherub hovering, knockback and pulls still move it;
- skips `updateMelee`, with its timer at −1, so its first strike after the stun again needs 8 ticks in range;
- can't cast: a Chorister's or Cherub's wind-up in progress is cancelled, and new casts wait, as with silence;
- is in state `ST_IDLE`.

**Snapshot flag `FLAG_STUNNED` = value 2 (bit 1)**, the bit Kiss's mark used. Set exactly while stunned.

**The look, on every client:**
- **The shockwave:** a ground ring (`vfx.ring`, `0xff5a1e`) from 0.5 to 12 m over 300 ms at the Fallen's feet, on top of today's red sphere, whose opacity rises from 0.3 to 0.45.
- **Stunned enemies recoil:** the pain animation plays from the flag's rising edge (ignoring `PAIN_COOLDOWN_MS`) and **holds its last frame** while the flag is set; the billboard **trembles** sideways, offset along the camera's right by `0.04 m × sin(2π × 12 Hz × t + slot)`; and it's tinted red (glow `{1, 0.25, 0.1, a: 0.35}`). Glow precedence: the hurt flash, the stun, the Judgment glow, the wind-up. The taunt `!` pops as now.

### 5.4 The Heretic: incense, Communion and the Shroud

- **Incense:** **5 dmg per pulse** (was 2.5); bound enemies take 10 per pulse. **Each cloud pulses on the tick it appears and every 15 ticks after, while before its end** (at `from + 15k` for `from + 15k < from + 120`: 8 pulses, as today; no longer on a shared clock), and **an enemy takes incense at most once per 15 ticks** (a new `eIncenseTick`, starting at −15 and reset when a slot is reused), so clouds still don't stack. Radius 2.5 m, 4 s, at most 6, as now. A cloud bites the moment it lands, and a Blessed that stays in it dies in 1.5 s (pulses at 0, 0.5, 1.0 and 1.5 s): the censer pays on a crowd that stays put (taunted, piled, or clinging to a shielded or wading player).

### 5.7 Shadowstep cuts (Betrayer, E)

The dash is unchanged (8 m in 0.2 s, invulnerable 0.5 s, cooldown 6 s, not slowed by wading). New: **the Betrayer's dagger cuts every enemy on its path for 40 dmg**, once per dash, credited to the Betrayer. 40 bursts a Blessed (light). It's the Betrayer's way *through* a crowd, out of a trap or into a stack.

- **Only an executed dash counts** (§5.2): the client changes `ePresses` only when it starts a dash.
- **The path (host):**
  - When `applyInput` sees a Betrayer's `ePresses` change, it remembers the accepted position *before* that input and the press input's `seq`.
  - From then, `applyInput` only **appends** each newly accepted position, with its `seq`, to the player's dash record, the press input's own position first; it resolves nothing.
  - If the press is accepted (`useE`), the path is the remembered position followed by the recorded positions of inputs with `seq ≤ pressSeq + 9`. `updatePlayers` resolves the path's new segments each tick, after `useE`.
  - The window **closes** when an input with `seq ≥ pressSeq + 9` is accepted, or 15 ticks after acceptance, whichever is first; then `dashCut` is sent. A press that isn't accepted cuts nothing, and its record is dropped.
  - A segment longer than 12 m (a teleport) is skipped.
- **Hit:** every living enemy whose cylinder is within **0.5 m** of a segment (centers within about 0.85 m for a Blessed, which reaches the neighboring rows of a packed crowd), measured from the closest point of the segment raised to the body center (+0.9 m), and not yet cut by this dash. About 10–15 Blessed in a normal crowd.
- **Event `dashCut`** (§9): `playerId` and the path's first and last points.
- **In first person: the slash.** The Betrayer's left hand lets go of the revolver and cuts across the view, for the dash's **200 ms** from when the local dash starts (like the Binder's fist for the Scourge in M11, as implemented: `decisions.md` M11 §3.6):
  - **The dagger**, held in a reverse grip (the blade down from the bottom of the fist): `fp-betrayer-dagger` is keyed and stored like the M11 weapon images, at the Betrayer's *k*. Claude measures the **fist's center** and the **blade's tip** in the image, and extends the forearm past the painting's edge along its axis, as the Scourge fist's was, so no cut end ever shows. The image is drawn at **s × the view's height** (start at s = 0.6). It's a **backhand cut**: the left forearm comes in from the bottom right, crossed in front of the body, and the fist sweeps outward **from right to left**, the blade's edge leading: the fist's center moves from **(+40, +22) vh to (−60, +22) vh** from the screen's center (x right, y down), turning from **+25° to −15°** (CSS rotation, clockwise positive) relative to the painting, by `smoothstep(t)` with `t` = elapsed ÷ 200 ms; it fades in over the first 30 ms and out over the last 40 ms. Check at 0, 100 and 200 ms that no cut end shows and the tip stays on screen; tune *s* and the path if needed, and record the final values in `decisions.md`.
  - **The revolver** drops **30 vh** out of the way over 60 ms when the slash starts and rises back over 120 ms after it ends. For the slash's 200 ms the client sends `fire = 0`, so neither attack fires, and its local fire timer fires no cosmetic shots; held buttons resume afterwards.
  - **The smear:** the path the blade's tip actually traces in this slash (the measured tip through the dagger's transform): the last 40 vh of that path behind the tip, drawn as an SVG stroke 2.5 vh wide at the tip tapering to 0, `#e8eef6` with a 2 px ink edge, fading out over 80 ms after the slash.
  - It plays only for the local Betrayer; others see the streak below.
- **On every client:** a silver streak along the path, a camera-facing strip 0.35 m wide, 1.0 m above the path, *beam* texture, `0xdfe6f0`, fading over 250 ms, with the new sound **`daggerCut`** (a sharp metallic swish, about 0.25 s, priority `PRIO_ABILITY`) at its start. The Betrayer's own client draws it when its dash ends, from its own start and end; others on `dashCut`, after the render delay. The streak also shows a solo Betrayer its dash (the afterimages need the Betrayer's third-person sprites, which a solo game doesn't load).

## 10. Performance

The game targets 1 000+ enemies; nothing here may cost frame time that a crowd multiplies.

- **Client per frame:** wading is one pass over the newest snapshot's enemies; the spawn glow searches the arena's spawn points per new slot; flinches are at most 64 offsets; burst sprites and torn pieces are billboards in the world atlas, in their own 512-slot ring; feathers and sparks follow the feather budget (§4.2), so the worst case stays near 1 500 live particles; burst sprites near the camera are clamped in size, which bounds overdraw (alpha-tested billboards lose early-z); the Falling Star preview (about 60 `lineOfSight` samples and one ray) runs only while E is held; the walkable set is computed once per map.
- **Host per tick:** the director's intensity is one pass over the living enemies for the engaged test and one per death for kills close by (up to 4 players each); the dash cut tests a few segments once per dash.
- **Peaks:** a wave starts only with at most the largest wave alive, so at most the largest wave plus one more are alive at once: **980** in the Cloister at 4 players (490 + 490), under the 1 500 the benchmark already holds.
- **The benchmark exercises M12:** a new bench parameter **`benchBurst=1`** (`scripts/bench.mjs` gets `--burst` to add it), passed through `HostSession.start` and the worker's `start` message: every 0.5 s the host deals 40 damage, credited to player 0, to the 20 Blessed nearest a point 8 m ahead of player 0 along its yaw (the bench's top-ups refill them). The bench skips `p.update`, so `game.ts` computes the wading count each frame on its own, in the bench too.
- **Gate (end of stage 5):** `npm run bench` meets the MVP's §12, and the uncapped mean frame time is within **+3%** of the baseline, **the commit M12 branches from** (currently 9bf1c3b), both plain and with `benchBurst=1` (the baseline ignores the parameter), run back to back in both orders with `scripts/bench-ab.sh`. Record the results in `docs/bench.md`. If the burst run misses, first halve the feathers and sparks per burst, record it in `decisions.md`, and re-run.
