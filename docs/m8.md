# Milestone 8 Specification: Feel

The MVP ([mvp.md](mvp.md)) works, and the first playtest ([playtests/2026-10-05.md](playtests/2026-10-05.md)) showed what keeps it from being fun to replay. This milestone adds no new dungeons, enemies or progression. It makes the one dungeon readable, punchy and forgiving enough that a party wants to play it again.

All numbers are **initial tuning values**, as in the MVP.

## 1. Goals and design rules

**Goals**, from the playtest:
1. A new player learns what their abilities do without reading anything in the Lobby.
2. Every player sees, without any text, when a teammate helped them.
3. Shooting into the swarm feels powerful.
4. Dying isn't a long wait, and saving a teammate is a fun team action.
5. Friends join with one link, including into a game in progress.
6. The game has sound and music.

**Design rules** for everything below:
- **A clean screen for players who know the game.** No permanent ability bar, tooltips or tutorial text in game. Help appears on demand (§2.2) or once (§2.3).
- **Feedback is visual, never text.** No "Marek healed you" messages. The only new feedback texts are the arena countdown (§5) and the dead player's own line (§4.5); the hints (§2.2) and chat (§7) are the only other new texts in game.
- **Hellfire means a friend.** Effects caused by players are drawn in the players' palette (ember red, soot black, brimstone orange). Heaven's effects stay white and gold. In the bright, gold world, a column of embers always reads as "a teammate did this". Exceptions, kept from the MVP: heals stay green, shields blue, and the ally target's marker gold (§3.5).
- **Teamwork is the best way to play.** A player alone holds the line; combined abilities break it (§8).

## 2. Learning a class

### 2.1 Class cards

The class cards on Singleplayer Setup and in the Lobby (§3 of the MVP) change. The description line is removed. Under the role and HP line, each card shows:
- **The weapon line:** the weapon's name in the system font; for the Fallen also `· Brimstone Hide`. Hovering the weapon name, or the passive name, shows its tooltip.
- **The ability row:** the Q and E icons side by side, 40 px each, inside the slot frame. Under each icon are its key (`Q`, `E`) and its name.
- **Tooltips:** hovering an icon or the weapon or passive name shows a tooltip panel above it, in the panel image style, at most 280 px wide. It shows the name in Cinzel and the description from §2.4. It appears after 150 ms and disappears when the pointer leaves. Hovering the card itself still only changes the card's border, as now, so the two hovers don't conflict. A tooltip never selects the class: only a click does.

### 2.2 Hints panel

- `H` toggles a hints panel in game, in the top-right corner, below the enemies remaining counter. It's a small panel in the panel image style, at most 340 px wide, that lists the class's hints (§2.4) with each ability's icon (24 px) beside its line. The general hints follow the class hints.
- The panel stays open until `H` is pressed again. It doesn't open during the result overlay.
- **Discovery prompt:** until the player has opened the panel once (`localStorage` key `heavenfall.hintsSeen`), a faint `H · Hints` label, 13 px, 60% opacity, shows in the top-right corner while in game. It's never shown again after the first `H`.

### 2.3 Ability ready

When an ability's displayed cooldown reaches 0 (from above 0), its HUD slot plays a **ready glint** once: a diagonal band of light sweeps across the icon over 400 ms, and the slot frame glows gold, fading over 300 ms. The *ready* sound plays (§9.1). Nothing plays for an ability that was never on cooldown.

### 2.4 Texts

These texts live in `src/data/classes.ts` and `src/data/weapons.ts`. Tooltips use the descriptions; the hints panel uses the hints.

## 3. Feedback

### 3.1 Being helped

| Ability | New feedback, seen by everyone in view |
|---|---|
| **Blasphemy** | The flat taunt ring becomes an **expanding sphere**: a translucent red sphere (seen from inside too, like the shield bubble) growing from 1 m to 12 m radius over 0.4 s while fading from 30% to 0 opacity. Each taunted enemy shows a **`!`** above its head: it pops in over 0.15 s (scale 0 → 1.2 → 1), holds 0.6 s and fades over 0.3 s, then stays hidden for the rest of the taunt. The `!` is a new world sprite (§11), drawn 0.4 m tall, 0.3 m above the enemy's head. |
| **Falling Star** | Unchanged (landing shockwave). |
| **Unholy Communion** | The heal ring stays. Every player it healed, **including players already at full HP**, shows a **heal column**: 24 green-tinted ember particles rising from their feet to 2.2 m over 0.8 s. The healed player's own screen gets the green vignette (MVP §10) even at full HP. |
| **Martyr's Shroud** | A **mote** (a 0.4 m ember-orange glow sprite with a short trail) flies from the Heretic's body center to the target's in 0.25 s. The shield bubble appears when it arrives. Self-cast skips the mote. |
| **Revive** | A **hellfire pillar** where the player rises: an ember-orange beam 1.2 m wide and 8 m tall (the beam texture on a camera-facing strip), fading over 1.0 s, with 40 ember particles bursting upward. |

**Party frames** react too:
- **Healed** (in an `abilityUsed` target list for Communion): the frame flashes green, fading over 400 ms.
- **Shielded:** flashes blue.
- **Revived:** flashes ember orange.
- **Damage taken:** the bar border pulses red for 200 ms.
- A dead player's frame shows their revive progress (§4) as an ember bar in place of the HP bar, and no text.

### 3.2 Hit and kill markers

Replace the large X markers with something small and elegant:
- **Hit marker:** four ticks at 45°, 135°, 225° and 315° around the crosshair, each 6 px long and 2 px wide, starting 7 px from the center. White at 85% opacity with a 1 px dark outline, shown for 80 ms.
- **Kill marker:** the same ticks, 9 px long, tinted ember (`#ff7a3a`), shown for 120 ms. It replaces the hit marker while shown.

### 3.3 Muzzle flash

- Drawn **behind** the first-person weapon frame, centered on the muzzle point, so the barrel overlaps it.
- Each shot picks a random rotation (0–360°) and a random scale (0.8–1.2 ×).
- 90% opacity with `mix-blend-mode: screen`, so it reads as light rather than a sticker.
- Shown for 60 ms.

### 3.4 View and weapon bob

Doom-style bob while walking, purely cosmetic:
- **Phase** advances with the horizontal distance walked, one cycle per 2.5 m (the player walk stride).
- **Camera:** the eye moves down by `0.035 m × |sin(phase)|`.
- **Weapon frame:** moves by `1.2% of screen height × sin(phase)` horizontally and `1.0% × |sin(phase)|` downward.
- **Amplitude** is scaled by horizontal speed ÷ class speed, eased toward its target over 150 ms, and 0 while airborne or dead.
- Aiming, hit tests and everything sent to the host use the unbobbed eye.
- No screen shake anywhere.

### 3.5 Ally targeting

Replaces the 10° rule of MVP §5.3. Range and line of sight rules are unchanged.
- **Acquire:** with no current ally target, the candidate with the smallest angle wins, up to **15°**.
- **Keep:** the current ally target stays selected while its angle is at most **25°** and it's still in range and line of sight. Another ally takes over only if its angle is at most 15° and smaller than the current target's by more than 5°.
- **Marker:** the ally target gets a gold chevron (an HTML element projected from the ally's head, 18 px) drawn over everything, including enemies, so it stays visible in a crowd. The gold billboard tint stays.
- **Out of range:** an ally within 15° of the aim that fails only the range check gets a grey chevron instead, so the player knows why E won't work.

## 4. Death and revive

Replaces MVP §5.7 except where noted. Arena clears still respawn everyone (§4.4).

### 4.1 Souls
- A player who dies leaves a **soul** at the place of death. Its **ground point** is the feet position the host last accepted, dropped to the ground height under it.
- **Levitation:** the soul rises from the ground point until its base is **1.0 m** above it, over 1.5 s (easing out), and then floats there. So it stands out from a distance and rises above the Blessed (1.6 m tall), and shots at its upper part pass over their heads.
  - A soul that joined during a fight (§6.2) starts already floating.
  - Clients add a gentle bob of ±0.1 m with a 2 s period; the host uses the fixed 1.0 m.
- **Hit cylinder:** radius 0.5 m and height 1.8 m, with its base at the soul's current height (on the host: ground point + rise), so its top floats 2.8 m above the ground. It blocks nothing, and enemies ignore it.
- **What it looks like:**
  - the player's own billboard (idle frame), tinted ember orange at 55% opacity, with embers rising from it continuously (4 per second);
  - a faint ember tether, a 0.1 m beam from the ground point up to the soul's base, so the spot it rose from stays clear;
  - above it, an HTML marker projected from 2.2 m above the soul's base, showing the class icon (28 px, ember glow) and the revive bar (48 × 5 px). The marker is shown to living teammates within 60 m while the soul is in front of the camera. It's drawn over everything, so a soul behind a crowd or a wall is still found.

### 4.2 Reviving
- Each soul has **revive progress** from 0 to 1.
- **Shooting it:**
  - A hitscan shot (all pellets of one trigger pull together) whose ray passes through a soul's cylinder before it stops counts one hit on that soul. Souls don't stop rays.
  - Censer projectiles hit souls like enemies: a censer explodes on a soul, and a censer explosion within 2.5 m of a soul counts one hit on it.
- **Each hit adds** `interval ÷ 3` progress, where *interval* is the shooter's time between shots. So any weapon firing continuously at a soul adds 1/3 per second, whatever its rate of fire. The **Heretic Saint adds double**.
- **Unholy Communion** adds 0.25 to every soul within its 15 m radius.
- **Decay:** progress drops by **0.1 per second**, every tick, down to 0.
- Players can't shoot their own soul.
- **At 1:** the player is **revived** on the soul's ground point with 50% of max HP and is invulnerable for 2 s. The soul sinks back into the body as the hellfire pillar plays (§3.1). The host sends `playerRevived` to all and a `teleport` to that player, to the ground point.
- **Rough times:** one non-Heretic shooter alone takes about 4.3 s, the Heretic alone about 1.8 s, and two non-Heretic shooters together about 1.8 s.

### 4.3 While dead
- The camera rises with the soul: the eye goes from the death position's eye height to 1.6 m above the soul's base over the same 1.5 s, so the dead player sees the fight from their floating soul. It can only rotate.

Unchanged from the MVP:
- The host ignores the player's position, fire and ability input.
- Dead players can't be targeted, hit, healed (except the soul's revive progress), shielded or used as ally targets.

### 4.4 Respawn and defeat
- **Arena cleared:** every soul respawns at full HP on the arena's entry cell for its index, as in the MVP.
- **Boss arena:** souls can be revived there, which replaces the MVP's "no respawn in the boss arena".
- **Defeat:** all players in the game are dead at the same time. Players still in the in-progress Lobby or Loading (§6.2) don't count.
- **Singleplayer:** death is defeat, as now. The solo regeneration stays.

### 4.5 The dead player's screen
- **Died in a fight:** the grey desaturation as now. The text, in the HUD's center style: *"You have fallen — your party can revive you"*. Outside the boss arena a smaller second line follows: *"or you rise when the arena is cleared"*.
- **Joined as a soul (§6.2):** no desaturation. Instead, an ember vignette at 35% opacity and the text *"Your party must revive you to join the fight"*. Nothing on screen says "dead".
- **Both:** the player's own revive progress shows as an ember bar under the text, 240 px wide, with the *revive* sound (§9.1) rising in pitch with the progress.

## 5. Arena countdown

Replaces the start rule of MVP §8.2 (*Lifecycle*, item 1). The lifecycle becomes `idle → countdown → combat → cleared`.

1. **Countdown starts** when any living player's feet are inside an idle arena's `rect`, and every earlier arena is cleared. Its timer starts at **60 s**. The entry door stays open, and no enemies spawn. An arena's exit door, toward the next arena, stays closed until the arena is cleared.
2. **Everyone inside:** when every living, connected, in-game player's feet are inside the `rect` and none stands on a door cell, the timer is set to the smaller of its current value and **5 s**.
3. The countdown never stops or restarts once started, even if players leave the `rect` again.
4. **At 0** the arena **seals**: it enters combat exactly as the MVP's start (doors close, players outside the `rect` or on a door cell are teleported to their entry cells, wave 1 starts). Party-size scaling is computed at this moment (§6.3).
5. **Arena index:** the countdown counts as having left *idle*.
6. **HUD:**
   - During the countdown, a small top-center line in Cinzel: `The doors seal in 0:47`.
   - In the last 5 s, a large centered number (5, 4, 3, 2, 1), each with the *tick* sound (§9.1).
   - At the seal, the existing *"The doors are sealed"* text.

## 6. Joining

### 6.1 Invite link
- **The link:** `<page URL without query or hash>?join=<game ID>`. It never contains the password.
- **Lobby:** the host's `Copy` button becomes `Copy invite link` and copies the link (and says `Copied` for 1.5 s, as now). The game ID stays shown large, for typing.
- **Opening a link:**
  - The Title shows an extra button, `Join game <ID>`, above `Singleplayer`, enabled once the name is valid.
  - It opens the Join screen with the ID filled in and the password field focused. `Join` works as before.
  - Once the Join screen opens, the page removes `join` from its URL (`history.replaceState`), so returning to Title doesn't offer it again.
  - An invalid `join` value (not 6 characters from the game ID alphabet after normalizing) is ignored.

### 6.2 Joining a game in progress

**The host accepts a `hello` during the game.** Its checks, in order:
1. version;
2. Loading in progress → `reject { reason: 'in_progress' }`, shown as `The game is starting — try again in a moment`;
3. password;
4. full.

**The flow:**
1. **`welcome`** carries `inProgress: true` during the game. The client shows the Lobby in **in-progress mode**:
   - no host `Start` button;
   - the game ID and the players in game with their classes, whose classes are taken;
   - an `Enter game` button, enabled once the player has picked a free class.
2. `Enter game` sends **`enterGame`** (C→H). The host sends that client a **`start`** with the current players, as in MVP §3.
3. The client loads and sends **`ready`**.
4. The host **adds the player** to the simulation (§6.3) and sends that client **`go`**. Its 20 s load timeout and `load_timeout` reject work as in the MVP.

**Where the player enters**, by the state of arena `arenaIndex` at `go`:
- **Combat:** as a **soul** on the arena's entry cell for its index, with progress 0. The party must revive them (§4.5).
- **Countdown:** alive, on the arena's entry cell for its index.
- **Idle or cleared:** alive, next to the living player with the lowest id (on that player's feet position). If no player is alive, on the entry cell of the next arena for its index.

**Who's in the game:** the host sends the `lobby` message on every change during the game too (a player joining, leaving or entering), so every client's party frames and names stay current.

**Leaving:** a player who leaves or disconnects mid-game is removed; their soul, if any, disappears. Rejoining works like any join; kills don't carry over.

**Game over while joining:** `gameOver` also goes to clients in the in-progress Lobby or Loading. They return to Title with the message `The game has ended`.

**Player index:** from M8 a player's **index is its player ID** (host 0, clients 1–3), in singleplayer too. The simulation has 4 player slots; a joiner takes the slot of its ID. This replaces "the position in `start.players`" (MVP §3). It also replaces the decision that keeps a player dropped during Loading in the player list to preserve indexes (`docs/decisions.md`, §3): they're removed like any leaver.

### 6.3 Party-size scaling
The multiplier of MVP §7.5 is computed **at each arena's seal** from the connected players in game (not loading) at that moment. It applies to that arena's waves, and for the boss arena to the Gatekeeper's HP, the Judgment interrupt threshold and every summon. Disconnects and joins after the seal don't change it.

### 6.4 Bot auto-join (dev)
For testing co-op abilities alone: `?bot=1&autojoin=1&join=<ID>[&class=<classId>][&name=<name>]` skips the Title and Join screens. Without `autojoin=1`, a bot uses the menus as before, and `join` only offers the invite button (§6.1).
- It joins with an empty password and the name `name` (default `Bot`).
- It picks `class` if free, otherwise the first free class in the order fallen, heretic, binder, betrayer.
- In in-progress mode it presses `Enter game`.
- Errors show on the Join screen as usual.

The host opens an open game (no password) and three tabs with this URL to get a full party.

**Bot changes** (MVP §2.5):
- **Countdown:** while an arena is in countdown, the goal of every bot, host's and clients', is its own entry cell in that arena.
- **Reviving:** when a teammate's soul is within 30 m with line of sight from the eye, and no living enemy is within 6 m of the bot, the bot aims at the soul's center (1.9 m above its ground point) and fires, instead of aiming at enemies.

## 7. Chat

- **Lobby:** a chat panel of its own in the bottom-left corner: the last 50 messages (`Name: text`) and a text field. Enter sends.
- **In game:** **Enter** opens a chat line above the HP bar.
  - While it's open, every key types into it: movement, fire and abilities are released and ignored, and pointer lock is kept.
  - **Enter** sends the message and closes the line. Enter on an empty line just closes it, which is how a player cancels a message.
  - Esc isn't a chat key: it opens Pause as usual (MVP §3), and opening Pause closes the chat line and discards its text.
  - Messages show above the chat line for 10 s, at most 6 at once, oldest first, fading over the last 1 s.
- **Messages:**
  - C→H `chat { text }`;
  - H→all `chat { playerId, text }`;
  - the host's own player goes through `LocalTransport` like other `ctrl` messages.
  - The host trims the text and drops it if empty; it cuts it to 120 characters.
  - Shown as plain text, never as HTML.
- Not in singleplayer.

## 8. Balance

**Principle:** every weapon gets stronger, abilities get stronger still, and enemies don't change. Breakpoints matter more than raw damage: a Blessed has 20 HP, so damage of 20 or more kills it in one hit.

| Item | Before | After | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Brimstone Shotgun | 8 × 10 dmg, 0.9 s | 8 × 12 dmg, 0.8 s | +35% damage per second. |
| Censer splash | 10 dmg to the 6 nearest others within 2 m | 20 dmg to the 8 nearest others within 2.5 m | The splash now kills Blessed: up to 9 kills per shot instead of 1. |
| Chain Gun | 10 shots/s | 12 shots/s | +20%. Now fires on alternating 2- and 3-tick gaps. |
| Silver Revolver | Pierces 3 | Pierces 6 | Shooting along a swarm clears a lane. |
| **Bound** (new) | — | Rooted enemies take ×2 damage | The reward for Chains combos: bound Blessed die to one pellet, bullet or splash. |
| Chains of Tartarus | Cooldown 10 s | Cooldown 8 s | More combos. |
| Falling Star | 30 dmg on landing | 40 dmg on landing | |
| The Gatekeeper | 40 000 HP | 45 000 HP | Party damage on one target rose about 13%; the fight keeps its length. |

**Damage pipeline** (MVP §5.5) gets a step after Kiss of Betrayal: **2. Bound:** if the target enemy is rooted, damage ×2. This includes the 0.3 s pull, during which enemies already count as rooted (`docs/decisions.md`, §5.6). The later steps move down by one. Marked and bound multiply (×6). The Gatekeeper can't be rooted, so it's never bound.

Everything else is unchanged.

## 9. Audio

### 9.1 Sound effects

**Synthesized in code**, retro style:
- Every sound is a parameter set in `src/audio/sfx.ts`, rendered once at load into an `AudioBuffer` by a small synthesizer in `src/audio/synth.ts`, deterministic for a given parameter set.
- **Synth parts:** square, saw, triangle, sine and noise oscillators; an attack/decay/sustain/release envelope; pitch slide; vibrato; a low-pass filter with sweep; bit crushing; and layering of up to 3 parts per sound.
- Each play varies pitch randomly by ±5% (UI sounds excepted), so repeats don't sound identical.
- Each sound is a named entry, so a file can replace it later without touching the callers.

| Group | Sounds |
|---|---|
| Own and others' weapons | shotgun blast, censer launch, censer burst, chain gun shot (short and light: it plays 12 times per second), revolver crack |
| Hits | hit tick (own hits, quiet), kill tick (own kills, slightly brighter) |
| Enemies | Blessed death (a soft feathery chirp), Chorister wind-up (a rising choir-like tone), orb fired, Cherub wind-up, arrow fired (whistle), enemy melee hit on you (thud) |
| Gatekeeper | Volley wind-up, Volley fired, Judgment charge (3 s rising drone), Judgment blast, interrupted (glass break) |
| Players | hurt (own), death, revive, healed, shield up, shield broken |
| Abilities | Blasphemy (low growl), Falling Star leap and landing boom, Communion (a warm chord), Shroud (shimmer), Chains (chain rattle), Discord (a dissonant gong), Kiss (a sharp sting), Shadowstep (whoosh) |
| Arena | countdown tick, doors seal (heavy slam), arena cleared (short fanfare), victory and defeat (used only when there's no music sting, §9.2) |
| UI | button hover, button click, ability ready (soft chime), revive progress (a hum whose pitch rises with progress, while you hit a soul or are being revived) |

**Playback rules:**
- **Triggers:** sounds are driven by what each client already knows:
  - local input (own shots);
  - snapshot changes (an enemy slot appearing, disappearing or changing state; new projectiles; `bossCast`; HP and shield changes);
  - events (`abilityUsed`, `playerRevived`, `arenaStarted`, `arenaCleared`, `bossCast`, `gameOver`).
- **Other players' shots:** the player snapshot record carries a `shots` counter (§10). A client plays the difference as that many shot sounds, spread evenly over the time to the next snapshot.
- **Positioning:** world sounds have a position. Gain falls linearly from 1 at 2 m to 0 at 40 m; a `StereoPannerNode` pans by the horizontal angle from the camera. Own weapon, own hurt and UI sounds aren't positioned.
- **Limits:**
  - at most 24 sound-effect voices at once;
  - per sound, at most 4 at once and at least 30 ms between starts;
  - when full, a new sound replaces the oldest playing voice of lower or equal priority, or is dropped.
  - **Priority:** own weapon and own hurt > abilities, revive and Gatekeeper > others' weapons > enemies > hits.
- **Start:** the `AudioContext` is created on the first user gesture (a click or key on the Title). With `dev=1` or `bench=1` it's created at load and may stay suspended until a gesture, which is fine.

### 9.2 Music

- **Tracks:** in `assets/music/` as MP3 (Git LFS), listed in `src/data/music.ts` with their loop points in seconds. A track not listed isn't loaded, so missing music never causes an error.

| Track | File | Plays |
|---|---|---|
| Calm | `calm.mp3` | Title, menus, Lobby, Loading, and in game outside combat (idle, countdown, cleared) |
| Arena 1 | `arena-1.mp3` | Arena 1 in combat |
| Arena 2 | `arena-2.mp3` | Arena 2 in combat |
| Arena 3 | `arena-3.mp3` | Arena 3 in combat |
| Boss | `boss.mp3` | Arena 4 in combat |
| Victory | `victory.mp3` | Once, at Victory |
| Defeat | `defeat.mp3` | Once, at Defeat |

- **Switching:** a 2 s crossfade between tracks. At the seal, the arena's track starts. When an arena is cleared, the calm track returns. At the result, the playing track fades out over 1 s and the sting plays once; Results stays silent after it. The sandbox uses the Arena 1 track.
- **Loops:** each looping track plays through an `AudioBufferSourceNode` with `loopStart` and `loopEnd`. The agent picks the loop region (at most 150 s, on bar boundaries, where the end flows into the start) by analyzing the files, and records it in `src/data/music.ts`.
- **Memory:** the two short stings stay decoded during the game. Besides them, at most 2 tracks are decoded at once: the one playing and the one that plays next (in combat, the calm track; outside combat, the next arena's). The others are released.

### 9.3 Volume
- The Title and the Pause overlay get three sliders, 0–100%, in a Settings view opened by a `Settings` button, with `Back`:

| Slider | Default | Controls |
|---|---|---|
| `Master` | 80% | everything |
| `Music` | 50% | the music (§9.2) |
| `SFX` | 70% | every sound effect, UI sounds included (§9.1) |

- **Mixing:** a track's or sound's gain is multiplied by its slider and by `Master`, through one `GainNode` per group (music, sound effects) feeding a master `GainNode`.
- **Saving:** the sliders are saved in `localStorage` (`heavenfall.volume.master`, `heavenfall.volume.music`, `heavenfall.volume.sfx`) and take effect immediately, while dragging. Gains change over 50 ms, so dragging doesn't click.
- **Preview:** releasing the `SFX` slider plays the *button click* sound at the new volume, so the player hears the level.
- **Layout:** each slider shows its label on the left and its value (`70%`) on the right; at 0% the value reads `Off`.
