# Playtest 2 — 2026-10-08

Two players, on the build with the M9 classes, the M10 levels and the M11 weapons. The talk afterwards was mostly about whether the game is fun. The party reached the Gatekeeper.

| Player | Class | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Tomas | Heretic Saint | Healer |
| Marek | Betrayer, then the Fallen, then the Binder | Damage, Tank, Support |

## What Marek said

Translated from Tomas's notes.

**Betrayer**
- Good, but all he had to do was keep running backwards from the horde and hold the trigger.
- Silver Bullet didn't feel strong. The first enemy it hit was covered at once by the next one walking up, so the bullet did its damage but didn't look like it.
- The Blessed take a while to swing, so he could run around them or jump out of the crowd at little risk and get away.

**Fallen**
- Liked that it's slower.
- Never used Falling Star: finding Tomas to leap to was hard. A skill like it makes sense, but it would be better if it could be used any time, anywhere.
- Suggested a charge that runs into the crowd and throws enemies aside, and a War Stomp (stun, knockback).
- The shotgun again didn't feel strong. Nothing in the enemies it hit showed they were being hurt. Not satisfying.
- No AoE to get rid of the horde.
- Barely used Blasphemy. It doesn't feel useful yet.

**Binder**
- The best one. A good weapon and a good cleave: "finally I felt I was killing someone".
- Chains of Tartarus is great and combines well with the weapon.
- Discord on a caster is fine, but hard to aim.

**In general**
- He likes the hordes in Diablo because there are many AoE skills to get rid of them. Heavenfall has few, so you run from the horde and kill it, "waiting" until they all fall. It needs more satisfying AoE ways to clear them.
- Killing masses is a good idea, but as it is now, a bit boring. Talking about shooters in general, he'd maybe enjoy hiding from projectiles and taking cover more.
- The boss fight is too long and boring at first, until Judgment comes, which is great. He tried to hide behind an angel statue, but it didn't protect him.
- The look and the atmosphere are good. He liked how the arenas are laid out.
- He has trouble tracking his HP, barely notices taking damage, and can't tell when he's healed or shielded.

## Tomas's notes

- **Heretic:** healing and finding the player to heal was fun, and so was handing out shields. The Censer Launcher could kill faster. The incense clouds are fine and don't get in the way visually.
- **Spawning:** the enemies spawn at the same time, so they soon form one clump that chases the party, and the player only runs backwards and shoots into it. Spawning continuously instead of in waves might help, maybe the first wave at once and then continuously. A visible sign of a spawn, like a golden glow where it happens, would be good.
- **HP, heals and shields** should be shown visually, not by text, maybe by lighting up the screen's edges.
- **Cherub arrows** can't be seen at all, so they can't be dodged. New players won't notice that Cherubs shoot.
- **The boss arena:** the Gatekeeper could fly from one perch to another during the fight, so the party must watch where it is while Judgment charges. Remove the angel statues and the fountain and keep only the pillars to hide behind. Spawn grounded ranged enemies on the ledges around, and add a second pedestal on the opposite side of the arena.

## Analysis

- **The horde has no shape.** A wave spawns in about a second and merges into one crowd behind the party. A threat from one side is answered by walking the other way, so the fight becomes "back up and hold the trigger".
- **The horde has no weight.** Players walk through enemies, and a Blessed strikes only 0.5 s after reaching them. Being surrounded isn't dangerous, so clearing space with AoE isn't needed.
- **Kills don't show.** A Blessed has 20 HP, so almost every hit kills, and every death looks the same whatever killed it. The next Blessed steps into the gap at once, so a shotgun blast and a revolver shot look alike.
- **Few ways to clear a crowd.** The Binder, whose Scourge hits several enemies at once, felt best. The Fallen's landing deals 10 dmg by design and needs an ally to aim at; the Betrayer has no area attack besides Silver Bullet; the censer has no splash since M9.
- **The feedback exists but is too short.** The red, green and blue screen-edge flashes are already in (MVP §10), but they last about 0.3 s. Nothing stays on screen while a shield holds or while HP is low.

## Decisions

Agreed after the playtest and several reviews of the spec (research on horde games, the enemy side, the class side, game feel, and the code). The reviews proposed many new things; the scope was then narrowed on purpose: **first make clearing the horde fun, with a real risk of being overwhelmed, and give every class its own way to clear a stack; only then add new enemy types and threats.** Moved to [m12.md](../m12.md) (the horde). Halos and ultimates move to M13.

- **The horde:** bigger waves that keep arriving for 10 s from wherever the party isn't, paced by a director like Left 4 Dead's (build up, peak, relax) that watches how hard-pressed the party is; a gold glow where enemies spawn; in single player, healing only during the breath.
- **The risk:** players wade through crowds; the Blessed strike sooner.
- **Each class's way of clearing a stack:** the Fallen a circle (a shotgun that kills with every pellet up close and much less at range, Falling Star aimed anywhere or slammed at its own feet, Blasphemy's 1 s stun as a visual beat); the Binder an arc (the Scourge hits everything in it); the Betrayer a line (Silver Bullet through ten, Shadowstep cutting its path); the Heretic an area (incense that bites the moment it lands, Communion 120 every 8 s, the Shroud's blast over 3.5 m).
- **Kills show:** sudden big damage blasts an enemy back and tears it apart (a Blessed torn in half, a Cherub losing its wings) in a burst of light and feathers, heavier for the heaviest blows, with its own sounds; your own kills are confirmed on your screen at once; screen shake for your own big moments, with an off switch.
- **Feedback:** a steady blue edge while shielded; a low-HP heartbeat and a crimson edge; stronger flashes for damage and heals; Cherub arrows in pale cyan, the only cyan in the world.
- **Later, once the core plays well:** an elite charger (the Exalted), leading shots and Chorister changes, surges aimed ahead of the party, markers for swarmed teammates and rescues, a grab special, a crowd effect for Discord, the boss arena changes above, a charge for the Fallen.
