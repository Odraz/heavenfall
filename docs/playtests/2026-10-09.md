# Playtest 3 (solo) — 2026-10-09

Tomas alone, single player, on the M12 build, each class in turn. M12 is heading the right way; what's left is tuning. Moved to [m12-followup.md](../m12-followup.md).

## Tomas's notes

**Enemies**
- The Cherubs' blue arrows could be a bit slower, so dodging them is easier and fairer.
- Choristers could be slightly bigger (taller and wider), to be seen better in a crowd. Their projectiles could be slower too, but break on impact, shattering like glass and dealing golden area damage around. A globe destroyed mid-air could instead deal area damage to the enemies around it.
- Cherubs could be a little larger.

**Fallen**
- The abilities are fun. The shotgun could be a little faster, both primary and secondary.
- Seems fair: reached the boss arena.

**Binder**
- The hardest class to play: very hard to get past Arena 2.
- Very few escape options, but a fun melee class. The Scourge is strong, but risky that close. Maybe the bullets could slow more.
- Could be a tiny bit faster and have a bit more HP.
- Bound enemies could stay bound a bit longer: sometimes they were free again before he reached them with the Scourge.
- Players should see better that enemies are bound: red-hot chains, for a moment, coming down from a wide circle above onto a narrow circle on the ground, like an inverted cone, in the spirit of the golden spawn ray.

**Heretic**
- Quite fun, almost unkillable without mistakes. Damage seems okay, but killing the boss takes impossibly long. The censer's cloud could be a bit larger.
- Could be a little more fragile.

**Betrayer**
- Fragile but a good toolkit.

**Overall**
- Single player is hard for some classes, probably because there's little or no healing. Healing in the relaxed times could be faster, or pickup heals could come in the future.
- The ledge in the first arena has a single way up, so you can stand on it and shoot at the stairs. It needs a second staircase.
- A few Cherubs in the middle of the first arena in the middle or late stages of the fight would be welcome, and the same with a few Choristers on top of the bridge in Arena 2.

## Decisions

Agreed after going through the damage math of each class with Claude.

- **No healing in a well-played solo arena:** the breath ran only in the director's relax, which only followed a peak, so clearing a wave fast skipped it. Solo now always gets a breath of at least 6 s before the next wave, and a full heal when an arena is cleared, so there's no waiting in the corridor.
- **Heretic:** it's near-unkillable because of its self-sustain, not its HP, so its HP stays 150 and its heal and shield come less often instead. Its censer hits harder, and the solo Gatekeeper's HP drops to 0.3 of its full value (was 0.4) for every class.
- **Fallen:** the shotgun stays as it is (the Fallen was already the strongest class); only the slug fires faster.
- **Binder:** more HP and a longer bind; its speed and slow wait for the next playtest, so it's clear which change helped.
- **Globes:** shooting one down would have beaten every ability (a 3 m blast, no cooldown, often hit without aiming), so its hit zone is the globe itself and its blast 1.5 m. Cover must protect from the blast.
- **Pickups** stay for later, as the notes say.

The details are in [m12-followup.md](../m12-followup.md).
