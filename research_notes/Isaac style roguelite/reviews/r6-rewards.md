# Revision 6: items, relics and reward-source review

Reviewed on 2026-10-11 against core rules, all four current class catalogs, the Pearly Gates acceptance checklist, lore, the horde-fun report and the October playtest notes. This is a specification review through adversarial reasoning and mental runs. No tests, game sessions, implementation scripts or benchmarks were run, and no game/UI code was edited.

## Definite gaps repaired

## Adversarial mental runs

1. **Dormant chain with no seed.** A solo Betrayer has replaced Field with Judas Coin, no bind-producing augment, no relevant item and no active hazard. Equipped Tile cannot bind without a pooled direct kill; Link cannot launch without a bound direct kill. The source closure adds neither output. A prospective state-reader offer cannot use this loop as evidence of usefulness. Add a carried Chain Vial: bind is now a real source, so Link can launch survivors on later direct bound kills; that launch remains relic-origin and cannot dispatch a landing passive. Item coverage itself also supplies pooled while active, enabling Tile's separate later-primary chain. No effect rewrites the first victim's state snapshot.

2. **Source expires while an offer waits.** A surviving ally area supplies a state after that ally dies; it can support the original fixed card while actually active. Expiry removes that source. The card disables without replacement, fallback money or a new RNG draw. Restore a legitimate kit/item source or pass. A disconnected ally's removed area and uncast ability never act as phantom sources.

3. **Returned boss globe.** A Ward catches an Orb Volley projectile that still has 15 base damage and a remaining lifetime. It returns as one friendly item-origin/secondary projectile; the bound multiplier is applied once at impact. It cannot shatter for the old 40-damage hostile-globe blast, cannot reflect again, cannot gain Q/E attribution and cannot cause another kill/tier proc. Ordinary credited enemy rewards remain subject to existing reward entitlements.

4. **Open doorway and straight-up throw.** A player near an open doorway throws a pool toward the next room. The trajectory stops just inside the original room; the floor projection/backward fallback is resolved there, visibly matching ownership. A straight-up throw still travels at most 20 m/one second and spends its already-reserved landing entitlement once. No distant endpoint can reserve a fifth area, and no second item-use event appears on landing.

5. **Developed Betrayer preserves the party tool.** Field/Way Back supplies pooled setup and direct E kills. Tile and Last Plea are useful independent choices; First Plea has no direct Q source and is identified accordingly. Choosing Judas Coin changes the Q tool and can remove Tile's sole pooled setup unless another actual area source exists; the preview must name that loss. Reversal is never sold as a functioning Betrayer combination.

6. **Two humans at the chest amid continuous shared XP/halos.** Both retain their own fixed offer IDs and reservations. Unrelated host revisions or another player's wallet update cannot invalidate their own otherwise-legal claim. The host revalidates each card against the latest effective kit/phase and atomically marks its entitlement. A request repeated after acknowledgment returns its original settlement rather than claiming another card. Scoped concurrency rules belong in core §14.

7. **Exhausted profile on a full route.** Four active Rites close further free earned sources for six personal halos each. A bargain whose initially legal Rite becomes a fifth or invalid card refuses without spending max HP and permits terminal decline. A Sundial fallback pays only at the qualifying boss seal. The fallback wallet credit is never a world pickup and cannot fire Quill. Prior-win/start eligibility, recorded death count, immutable Sentence and timed-seal qualification settle deeds once at victory.

## Accounting and fun conclusions

The item weights still total 100 (20/12/14/8/14/14/10/8); the eight prices remain 25–45, and two-slot stacks still make stocked items a choice. The twelve relics retain six Common/four Rare/two Legendary entries and six disjoint replacement pairs; a combo remains two equipped effects, with the sacrificed ordinary benefit visible. Two relic slots, individual cooldowns, at most one activation per relic per resolving tick, bounded target lists and the four owned projectile/area entitlements already bound their output. A later native cloud pulse can activate again after cooldown; talent kill discharges retain their stronger once-per-node/root flag. The core's separate maximum of two active kill-triggered talent discharge nodes should not consume those relic slots.

The collection is finite: eight successful chest/bargain claims acquire eight chest relics. With every earned claim made and every accepted pawn won, that can take four floors; using only chests takes eight. A claimed 5–8-floor expectation needs play data or assumptions about bargain refusal, rather than relying on rarity: already-owned relics are excluded, so rarity changes discovery order rather than duplicate frequency.

The horde playtests identify repetitive safe-ring firing as the actual problem. The catalog's useful tradeoffs are where/when to spend limited items, pickup risk, state setup and a combo's lost ordinary benefit. Collection count alone is not a measure of replayability; collected players need legal, effect-changing Rites and varied route/risk decisions. The revision-6 Delivery/Payload composition makes a newly assigned alternative shapeable while preserving explicit visible-effect and proc boundaries.

McMillen's primary postmortem describes randomized resources and physical run growth, then the need for increasing authored content as players complete goals; it also records rare stacking bugs and platform/performance failures. The design inference is to permit visible composition while making ownership and bounded event rules explicit. This supports an Isaac-inspired one-dungeon slice; it does not establish unlimited experiences or a proof of zero defects. [The Binding of Isaac postmortem](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/postmortem-mcmillen-and-himsl-s-i-the-binding-of-isaac-i-).

## Evidence still required

Observe item relevance during a real crest, usefulness of rare seal cards for the actual assigned kit, whether a combo meaningfully changes position/timing, source-expiry behavior while a fixed offer waits, returned-globe readability, post-win inventory settlement, and whether collected players vary builds/routes voluntarily. These are implementation/play evidence requirements, not unresolved permission questions or claims that a paper review measured them.

## Integration cross-review

Reread the revision-6 core, updated class source/Delivery/Payload tables and the encounter ownership contracts after parent integration. The reflection allocation repair was found in this second pass and added to core §14.12 and Ward. The root-dispatch wording was clarified separately: one relic activation per resolving tick under CD, versus one talent kill-discharge bundle per node/root at its first eligible tick. Native Witnesses can therefore create another Q seal after five seconds without rearming its consumed talent callbacks. Catalog wording now explicitly includes world/prop secondary deaths in derived-streak-proc suppression while preserving real assists and ordinary death entitlements. Scoped transaction versions avoid wallet/XP starvation and retain card/loadout revalidation; the accepted input/state IDs and bounded retry high-water mark survive reconnect.

The final mental pass covered fresh base kits, maxed/exhausted profiles, Field with the E return, fixed offers during source loss, primary item/secondary reflection provenance, delayed native pulse after cooldown, all-dead/host-loss boundaries and post-victory claims/carry. No additional definite item/reward contradiction was found after those repairs. The catalog is ready to implement against the integrated contracts; observed balance, generated-floor implementation, transport/storage behavior and repeat-run enjoyment remain unmeasured.
