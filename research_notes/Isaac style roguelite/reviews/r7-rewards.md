## First adversarial round: retained revision-6 boundaries

1. **Fresh solo shortest route:** the first required fight's guaranteed Rite is a useful tier-1 build choice; a two-fight rush does not promise a learned point at the Confessional. The timed Sundial may add a second temporary choice. Skipping the key/chest means giving up collection, halos and optional pages; victory cannot unlock those skipped encounters while invulnerable.
2. **Collected, nine-point account:** same-channel Delivery/Payload substitution still makes unlearned shapes eligible without a third effect or second capstone. Free Pass is an informed refusal, not a new draw or fallback money. The useful initial pool does not imply four desirable subsequent Rites after earlier choices occupy channels/gates.
3. **Four overlapping pools:** staggered Bitter Water landings share one per-enemy half-second damage interval across all owners, even after exit/re-entry. Newest-source ownership cannot add an extra landing pulse inside that interval. Chain/Plume spend their first-membership attempt even on bound/immune targets; an expired immunity does not queue a surprise retry.
4. **Item launch versus reflected projectile:** a direct Plume launch has a bounded direct-item landing bundle, no Q/E/capstone attribution; a relic/combo launch has none. A Ward reflection mutates one existing hostile-allocated projectile to friendly secondary collision, removes hostile shoot-down/shatter behavior and cannot trigger another talent/relic chain. Independent later direct attacks can read the resulting states.
5. **Claim/bargain race:** pending shown relic IDs remain reserved per player. Owning or declining one source releases exactly its reservations; collection exhaustion is different from a pool temporarily reserved elsewhere. A paid Rite preview that later reaches four Rites refuses with no HP price/node/halo payment. Victory closes unpaid bargains before post-win harmless shopping; accepted pawns promote once.
6. **Death/rejoin/exit:** accepted item flights retain their spent landing entitlement through owner death; leave/disconnect removes their effects without refund. Relic timers retain identity through combo changes and rejoin. A known offline record receives its qualified victory/deed settlement within the declared recovery cache, but no intervening ordinary XP/halos. Untouched earned pages/chest rights receive the bounded exit allowance; voluntary pre-win leave closes its record.
7. **Boss/proc boundaries:** actual raised body/part geometry governs pooled/incense membership. Floor tools cannot reach through the dais. Watchers/Seals cannot become autonomous targets or count toward Comb/kill/deed streaks; accepted incidental area damage gets the part modifier. Normal Host enemies can support combat procs/streaks, with no currency/XP/items and one bounded ordinary ranged-add mote allowance.

## Second round: repaired event sequences

- **Manual refusal followed by success:** slot 1 Balm at full HP is refused; slot 2 Feather succeeds in the same tick. A same-tick world Seal can only store or remain under the cooldown. If Balm instead healed, Feather would be refused; no rejected press is queued for next second. Two stored Feather stacks route into the lower nonfull slot, not a host/client guess.
- **No-forced-collection chest:** two dormant cards may still be claimed for another character. Pass instead closes the source, releases the shown IDs and permits a later paid offer; Esc preserves the exact offer. Duplicate/refused/late requests cannot create another claim, free Rite or halo fallback, and a passed source no longer delays the exit.
- **Downward censer, delayed first pulse:** the root stores cast yaw as its fallback heading. First-pulse hit count is distinct positively damaged enemies, excluding boss parts. The player's later turn does not rotate Comb; Harrow reads the resulting stored lane and its same Wheel cooldown, with its ground mask and target cap.
- **Long throw followed by quick placement:** Water accepted at tick 0 lands at 30; Ward placed at 15 is newest only until Water becomes real. Appeal on the throw's use reads an already-landed area or produces no effect; the landing does not retry. A later item use reads Water as newest if its normal range/room predicates pass. A recovered snapshot preserves activation times.
- **Finite collection/deeds:** exactly eight distinct chest IDs are claimed, whether four winning chest+bargain floors or eight chest-only claims. Rarity cannot generate extra claims because already owned IDs are excluded. A qualifying second victory may grant several deed relics together; it never copies a deed into the chest pool or grants a third passive effect.
- **Retained native setup after a menu change:** when an authored cleanup permits an accepted native original-Q source to remain after assigning a healing Q, First Plea still reads that source's positive Q kill. The card separately reports the currently dormant assignment and the pending source's original expiry. An actually retained taunt token/incense area similarly supports Usher/Thurible. A retained censer window with four distinct accepted hits and enough unconsumed native first-group budget for one more can support Comb; a consumed first group followed by later pulses cannot. Removed contributors, expired effects and reflected/secondary damage never qualify. No particular Shroud survival is assumed.

## Arithmetic and replay pressure

Drop weights remain `20+12+14+8+14+14+10+8=100`. At modelled income 105, Chart 30 + Balm 25 + Ash 35 costs 90; Chart + Ward 45 + Plume 45 costs 120 and is unaffordable at that income. Stock is four distinct weighted items, so these are budget examples rather than guaranteed pedestals. Two inventory slots make carrying/use relevant, and each human has separate purchases despite shared world-coin credits.

The catalog remains eight items, twelve relics with six/four/two rarity distribution and six disjoint authored replacement pairs. Each pair sacrifices an ordinary benefit and preserves two total equipped effects. Four persistent item/relic area entitlements per owner include accepted thrown reservations; four relic projectile entitlements are separate. No repair adds an item, combat input, rarity damage multiplier, hidden currency generation, extra slot, unrestricted recursive proc or second dungeon.

Two pressures remain intentional choices to observe rather than defects patched with new mechanics. First, an untouched source samples its eligible pool only on first offer creation; free Confessional preparation can influence that initial pool, though it cannot redraw a created offer and held Rite gates constrain later edits. Second, a full inventory has no in-run discard; a player must spend charges to free a slot, even in a valley. These should be observed for repetitive respec preparation or wasteful inventory friction. The current spec does not claim to have measured either behaviour.

The October notes identify safe-ring/backpedal trigger holding, imperceptible Silver Bullet/shotgun results, absent recovery and bot-masked difficulty. The rewards answer only part of that problem: resource dives, limited tool timing, visibly different run shapes and route/risk decisions. The Dominion, room pressure, boss rescue/local eyes and sound/readability contracts must produce the remaining decisions in play. Relic collection cannot make repetitive combat interesting by itself.

## Research and evidence boundary

McMillen's primary postmortem describes randomly distributed optional resources, visible run growth and progressively unlocked authored content; it also records late combination bugs and launch failures. The inference for this slice is to keep effective temporary choices available after collection completion and to make their exact interaction/settlement rules explicit. One generated dungeon should not claim the endless content of Isaac's expanding chapters. [The Binding of Isaac postmortem](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/postmortem-mcmillen-and-himsl-s-i-the-binding-of-isaac-i-).

The existing research report's 30–60-second decision target is a design hypothesis, not a studio-proven universal interval. Reward count, possible node combinations and mental walkthroughs do not measure fun. Implementation must establish actual item usefulness during a crest, voluntary veteran build/route variation, combo costs, fixed-offer/source-expiry behavior, post-win inventory/claim durability, performance and co-op readability. No unresolved item/economy design blocker remains from these repaired findings; that conclusion does not certify executable correctness or unlimited enjoyment.

## Independent integration round

Reread the revised effective alternate-source tables, public loadout/admission protocol, debug/persistence boundary, Red Iron, Bread, attached incense and encounter geometry after coordinator/class repairs.

- **Attached native incense:** Altar/Witnesses remains in its emitting room. Wearer death/disconnect/teleport/room crossing detaches at the last valid point for the remaining original lifetime. Native Q/LMB provenance is retained; a new room does not inherit that cloud, a renewed lifetime or extra area slot.
- **Accepted Red Iron hit:** invulnerability-blocked melee gives no spark; an accepted HP/shield-removing hit that kills the wearer gives no spark; only a surviving accepted hit dispatches its secondary return after life-state changes. It cannot manufacture a post-defeat native action. Native accepted Shroud break remains its separate synchronous ability consequence.
- **Bread's six entitlements:** one eligible primary clearance can admit six distinct normal motes, subject to the world/pending queue. Rejected capacity consumes neither quota nor a delayed death marker; resolved corpses are never retried. Already admitted motes keep their original twenty-second expiry, even queued, and no world/secondary/boss/add/part/trickle death feeds the harvest. Mote collection remains one accepted ID and an individual heal, with bounded Lazarus/Mercy responses.
- **Effective peer kit:** public loadouts include accepted learned nodes, Rites, suppressed learned IDs, assignments, equipped relics and effective max HP. A peer can resolve the accepted movement/shape/hint kit rather than treating a Rite-shaped charge as an illegal base move. Scoped versions still prevent unrelated XP/coin packets from starving a valid choice.
- **Disposable developer attempt:** immutable disabled progression clones the legal profile/inventory into run memory without stored admission/charge/stat writes. Debug allocations, loss and post-win claims/shopping disappear when that attempt ends. An unresolved normal attempt must settle first; export remains the original accepted persistent profile.

This round found and repaired the yaw-only item replication gap above and a separate native-movement refinement: Way Back's damaging return direction is admitted on its second press, not its first marker-placement yaw; Gatebreaker can steer. Their Comb heading comes from the accepted native damage leg/first-hit segment while retaining the original root action and used-node/cooldown records. The first hit point remains available after its victim dies. One swept native leg accumulates distinct positive hits to a single five-hit event, rather than creating one attack/allowance per segment; cloud effects remain restricted to their first pulse. Stationary delayed pulses still use captured cast heading. These are geometry/event identity repairs, not permission for recursive discharge opportunities. The stale catalog reference for boss pooled membership now points to the actual state table in bosses §3.6.

The last cross-check pinned a censer threshold boundary that was previously interpretable two ways. A shot's native direct impact and first native cloud-pulse group share one distinct-victim hit-count window: one killed impact victim plus four new positive first-pulse victims is five. Scapegoat's delivered first-pulse group stays on that same root. Retain the first hit position/incarnation/order; dispatch once when five is reached, never again from later pulses or another five victims. Separate later native kill events retain their normal source-slot/cooldown rules. This closes the hit-count ambiguity without converting a delayed pulse into a new proc root.

That attribution trace revealed one last source-predicate mismatch. The old Seal table checked only the assigned Q/E even though a surviving accepted native action retains its original input slot. The corrected closure also reads actual same-room native state/damage capabilities remaining after authored cleanup; Usher/Thurible accept retained taunt/incense, and Comb accepts only an unconsumed native first window with enough remaining hit budget. This is a conditional source rule, not a new promise that Shroud or any other outgoing effect survives reassignment. Metadata from removed, expired, canceled and secondary effects supplies nothing. Recompute current dormancy/pending setup without altering a fixed offer's IDs, original behavior or lifetime.

The final repaired-contract reread found no remaining definite item/economy blocker. The protocol's accepted three-dimensional flight/landing records, native damage-leg heading, hit-count window and retained-source usefulness now agree with catalog placement, attribution, cooldowns and recovery.
