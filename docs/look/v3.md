# V3 Characters

**Status: planned** (drafted 2026-10-25). Dependencies: V1 accepted (compressed atlases); can run alongside V2 once V1 is done, since it touches different files. Rules: [spec.md](spec.md). Tomas's side: [guide.md](guide.md#v3) and [meshy.md](meshy.md).

## Playable outcome

Every character is re-made from a detailed 3D model and rendered in the game's inked cel style at a higher resolution: armor with engraved trim, cloth with folds, real props. Silhouettes, colors, sizes, weapons and animations stay the same, so players still read every enemy at a glance (decision D4).

Before/after: in the `arena` view, the Blessed look like the concept's armored soldiers; a swarm at 40 m (`swarm-far`) still reads as ivory figures with dark outlines.

Out of scope: new animations or gameplay poses; new enemies; first-person weapons (painted, M11, stay).

## Frame-time share

0%: a sprite costs the same however detailed its model. The limits are memory and download: all character atlases together at most **260 MB** of GPU memory compressed (today about 560 MB uncompressed, 140 MB once V1 compresses them), and the first-load download within spec §5.

## Two paths

| Path | For | Who rigs and animates |
|---|---|---|
| **A: Meshy rig** | humanoids with legs visible below knee-length clothing | Meshy's auto-rig and animation library ([meshy.md](meshy.md) §4); Claude retimes the clips to the game's frames |

## Ordered stages

### 1. Pipeline with the Blessed pilot

- `scripts/blender/meshy_char.py`: import the Meshy GLBs ([meshy.md](meshy.md) §6 layout), normalize scale (the game height), orientation (faces −Y), feet on the ground; remove root motion; attach parts to bones.
- Textured cel material: the Meshy texture's color, lit by the game's sun in 2–3 quantized tones as `common.py`'s `_toon_nodes` does for palette colors, with the inverted-hull ink outline in `PAL['ink']`. Painted light left in a texture by Meshy is flattened first (a high-pass on luminance).
- Retiming: pick or resample each Meshy clip to today's frame count and loop points; the attack's hit frame lands where today's does (the game times hits from frame indices).
- Render 8 (or 5) directions × frames at the target px/m, pack into the atlas JSON format unchanged, encode KTX2, and the portrait.
- Show the new Blessed beside the old in the game (`?blessed=v2`), the `arena` and `swarm-far` views; Tomas approves the look before anything else is rendered.

Tests: atlas JSON compatibility (names, frame counts, directions, anchors within today's bounds); the game loads the KTX2 atlas and the fallback; memory within the roster's share.

### 2. The Blessed's family

- The Blessed final; its torn halves and sword (M12 §4.2.1) rendered from the new model, so kills match.
- The Watchman: the Blessed's model with the helmet and Clarion parts; its aim pose.

### 3. Robed characters (path B)

- Chorister, then Cantor: Claude's Blender rig with skirt bones, as the primitive scripts do now; the Cantor's wings and candle crown attached.

### 4. The Dominion

- Path A body; folded wings and mace attached and animated with the slam.

### 5. Fliers

- Cherub and Stray-Carrier: Meshy bodies, wings rigged in Blender (modeled from the wing sheets, or Meshy static wing models), the Carrier's shroud as a cloth strip.

### 6. The Gatekeeper

- Meshy body, six wings with the watching eyes, the trumpet; the 6 m scale and the dais anchor exactly as today.
- **Recommended benchmark point** (ask Tomas): load time and memory with every enemy atlas.

### 7. The players

- Fallen, Heretic Saint, Binder, Betrayer: third-person sprites for co-op. Their first-person weapons and today's HUD stay (D7), including its class portraits unless Tomas asks for new ones.

Each stage: Tomas's look check in the game at real size; old primitive scripts deleted only when their character is replaced and accepted.
