# The Heretic Saint: talent trees and new abilities

**Purpose:** the Heretic Saint's three talent trees and its three new abilities for the roguelite. [game.md](game.md) §0 fixes precedence: its rules win over this catalog, [classes.md](../classes.md) supplies class identity/design intent, and [lore.md](../lore.md) supplies lore. Core §9.3–§9.4's explicit composition rules supersede the older one-effect wording. All numbers are initial values.

**R06 follow-up (2026-10-22):** the in-game card wording lives in `src/data/talents.ts` and was rewritten in plain words; the Rule column below keeps the design wording. The Altar and Scapegoat now combine: the middle cloud is worn, the two side clouds stay at the impact. See [r06-followup.md](r06-followup.md) §1.

**Status:** completed design baseline (2026-10-11), reviewed through solo/co-op scenarios (§6). Numbers require playtesting; the scenarios are reasoning, not measured balance results.

## 1. Requirements (from game.md)

**Structure (game.md §9.3–§9.4)**
- **3 trees**, each a playstyle domain (e.g. ranged, melee, area, damage over time, slowing, hazards, traps, or a combination). One tree deepens the Heretic's verb, **sustain**; the other two bend it. Each must be fun in a pure swarm of Blessed and playable solo.
- **7 nodes per tree**, 1 point each, one rank:

  | Tier | Nodes | Opens with distinct strictly lower-tier nodes in the tree (core §9.3) |
  |---|---|---|
  | 1 | 2 | 0 points |
  | 2 | 2 | 1 point |
  | 3 | 2: exactly **one ability node** and one other | 3 points |
  | 4 | 1 capstone | 5 points |

- **One capstone per player** (taking one, learned or as a Rite, locks the other two). The Pearly Gates allows **9 points** (one capstone build of 6 plus 3, or a spread) plus up to **4 Rites** per run; later heavens at most 14 points.
- **Each tree's ability node** teaches one new ability for **Q or E**, replacing a named base ability (Unholy Communion or Martyr's Shroud) when assigned. LMB and RMB are never replaced; no new keys.

**What a node may do (game.md §9.3)**
- Change a **visible count or shape** (a wider cloud with a visibly larger ring, one more cloud allowed); **replace** an attack's or ability's effect with a different visible one; or **add one visible effect** to an attack or ability that has only one.
- **Never:** an invisible percentage, a hidden or conditional rider, a "press when ready" buff, a stat node, a multi-rank node.
- Augments declare a **Delivery** channel (count, trajectory, placement or footprint of an existing effect) or a **Payload** channel (adds/replaces one emitted effect). One active augment per channel and resolved named input is allowed; different channels compose in Delivery-then-Payload order, with at most two visible effects. Same-channel alternatives exclude; an ability unlock occupies neither channel. A Rite may substitute for a learned augment only in its own channel, never a capstone/another Rite/explicit exclusion. Passive/movement nodes have no implicit channel exclusions. Explicit target sets below define alternative-ability inheritance; no other node silently inherits. Every six-point capstone path and nine-point extension obeys these rules and the two kill-discharge-node limit.
- Applying a state (game.md §4.11), or one more visible effect on enemies in a state, counts as the node's one visible effect.

**The class's rules (classes.md)**
- **Healing and shielding stay separate:** no node or ability both heals and shields. No taunt.
- The Heretic stays **the healer**: items heal at most 40 HP to the user only (game.md §7.4), so nothing here may be made worse by them; a tree that bends away from healing must still leave the Heretic the party's best answer to a bad moment.
- Its weakness stays: fragile, weak against crowds alone, needs the party close. A damage tree may soften crowds better but keeps the Heretic's swarm kill rate below the damage classes' (classes.md: about 1 kill per second plus clouds, on purpose).
- Sacrament never heals the Heretic. Clouds don't stack and at most 6 exist (keep the cap or say what replaces it).
- Don't propose again: censers healing teammates (Sacrament has its own button); overheal turning into a shield; healing in the cloud.
- **Bots play the base kit** (game.md §12.4): no tree may be needed for a bot to work.

**Enemy states (game.md §4.11): the bridge to relics and teammates**
- The Heretic's base kit **applies** `censed` (enemies inside its incense clouds; only the Heretic's clouds apply it); it reads no state yet (its clouds are the place others' states pay off).
- **Each tree** has at least one node that makes an attack or ability **apply a state the base kit doesn't**: `slowed`, `bound` (short), `airborne` or `pooled` (an area other than its cloud); never `taunted` or `silenced`.
- **The class** has at least **two nodes that read a state another class's base kit applies**: `taunted`, `airborne` (the Fallen), `bound`, `slowed`, `silenced` (the Binder), `pooled` (the Betrayer's Field of Blood) (e.g. "kills of `censed` enemies by the slug burst into a 2 m ring").
- Fill the `States` column (`applies slowed`, `reads bound`); a relic or a teammate's kit can then be named that pays off on it.

**Rites (game.md §9.10): every node is also a run-only pick**
- A node is offered as a Rite only with its lower-tier gate, exclusions, current ability and usefulness conditions satisfied (game.md §9.10). Its card explains one visible rule; conditional readers require a producible state source. Learned-only gates never borrow temporary Rite points.
- **Source closure:** use game.md §9.9 and items-relics.md §2.1.1's finite producibility closure after resolving channels and substitutions. Seed only effective native kit, actually available carried items, active same-room areas and usable same-room hazards; admit a triggered emitter only when its trigger is reachable from those seeds. An equipped but untriggerable relic, an absent ally, a surrendered payload or an unseeded cycle cannot qualify a state reader. `own` predicates require the owner's producible source, not an ally's flag. Timed/source prerequisites are checked when offering/taking, then ordinary loss of a source makes the held effect dormant without erasing ownership.
- **Temporary substitution:** a Rite augment may suppress one conflicting learned augment in the same Delivery/Payload channel of its implicit named-input group; explicit exclusions, another Rite and capstones are never displaced. Suppressed ownership remains for gates, but only effective nodes produce effects, metadata or passive dispatch. Evaluate usefulness after suppression; show the sacrificed effect and any effects losing their sole state source. Table annotations explaining same-input/same-channel conflicts are implicit-group documentation, not extra `Excludes` prerequisites. No point refund or cooldown/source refresh is granted; the learned effect returns at the run's end.
- An augment of Q or E is offered only while that ability is assigned, so **each tree has at least 2 tier-1/tier-2 nodes on `LMB`, `RMB` or `passive`** to support early offers. These still obey gates, exclusions and state-source usefulness checks.
- An ability node taken as a Rite goes straight into its slot (Q or E) with the replaced ability's remaining cooldown; it must be playable without any of its tree's other nodes.
- Rite choices and valid Confessional loadout edits may commit during a valley or an encounter countdown, with the same living-player, range and room checks. At the actual combat seal, close overlays and reject later commits without discarding their pending offers. This does not widen the valley rules for items, regeneration or teleporting.
- Bots never get Rites; nothing here may be needed for a bot.

**Quantities:** 21 nodes, 3 new abilities, 3 capstones.

## 3. The trees

| Tree | Name | Domain | Identity (one line) | Its ability node | Capstone |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | Forbidden Physic | healing and revival (deepens sustain) | The physician: heal two at once, throw the crowd off the party with Communion, and turn a timely harvest of healing into a rescue. | Bread of Angels (E, replaces Martyr's Shroud) | Resurrection of the Body |
| B | The Burning Tombs | damage over time and hazards: fire on the floor (bends sustain into a solo kiter) | The pyre-walker: set Heaven's marble alight, lead the horde through the fire, run a ring of it round them, and let it spread from every body. | Trial by Fire (E, replaces Martyr's Shroud) | The Sixth Circle |
| C | Martyrdom | incense on allies, close-range holding and binding (bends sustain into protection) | The martyr-maker: put your smoke on whoever holds the crowd, let them walk free in it, chain the crowd when the shield breaks, and raise incense high enough to catch Heaven's flyers. | Cloud of Witnesses (Q, replaces Unholy Communion) | Pillar of Cloud |

**Why these three.** Forbidden Physic is the party healer turned up and stays playable alone through motes. The Burning Tombs is the solo kiter: at 8 m/s the Heretic can lead Blessed through fire without becoming the Binder's pile maker. Martyrdom puts incense on whoever holds the crowd. Each tree replaces only one base ability; a spread may replace both Q and E and therefore gives up the base burst heal and shield. The assignment row names that cost. Sacrament always remains an ally-only heal, so the class remains the best sustained healer. Every heal remains green, every shield blue, and all angel deaths remain feathers and gold sparks; the fires recall the Saint's burning tomb, with no blood or human remains.

**Shared rules for the nodes below** (so each card stays one line):

- **Effects already full.** The censer has its hit (60) and its cloud; Martyr's Shroud has its shield and its burst. Nodes on them reshape or replace the cloud or the burst (bigger, longer, more of them, somewhere else, a different area), never add a third effect. Sacrament and Unholy Communion have one effect each, so a node may add one.
- **Your clouds.** The censer's clouds, Scapegoat's three, the Altar's and Cloud of Witnesses share **6 active areas per Heretic**; a seventh puts out the oldest by creation tick, then area ID. Ploughshares's furrow occupies one of those slots too. Only incense makes `censed`; fire makes `pooled`. Each area retains its emitting room. Attached clouds detach and remain at the last valid point in that room when their wearer dies, disconnects, teleports or crosses its room boundary; their duration never restarts and an attachment never carries damage into another arena. An area created by Q/E remains on that button's provenance even if the player changes assignment later.
- **Burning ground** (The Burning Tombs' fire): 1 m floor cells that burn for a set time, drawn as red-orange flames with rising embers (the hazard color language of game.md §4.10). An enemy whose actual feet contact a burning floor surface is `pooled` and takes the Heretic's pulse. Ordinary Cherubs hover above it; a Brought Low flyer with actual floor contact can burn while grounded. Players are never hurt by it; enemies don't avoid class fire (authored environmental hazard avoidance is separate). **At most 400 burning cells per Heretic** from Kindling, Trial by Fire, Wall of Fire and The Sixth Circle together; evict oldest first-lit tick, ties owning-room row-major cell ID. Refreshing a still-lit cell does not reset that age, and eviction removes all its cell contributors. Ploughshares's furrows are capped by the 6 clouds instead. Merging cells preserves each contributing source's own expiry/depth/slot; a secondary spread refresh keeps terrain lit without extending Trial/Wall/furrow primary kill attribution.
- **One pulse.** Incense, the Saint's fire and Pitch share the existing **global per-enemy** pulse budget across all covering effects: 5 every 0.5 s, an immediate first pulse, then no pulse before the next eligible tick. Choose the newest currently active covering area contribution (creation tick, then source ID); credit its owner. Only when no actual area covers the target may the newest eligible Pitch mark supply that pulse; carried embers never steal a covering cloud's attribution. A continuously covered Blessed dies on pulse four at 1.5 s; a bound one on pulse two at 0.5 s. A pulse is still possible after its creating Heretic dies. Separate Fallen fire can deal its own pulse; the same enemy can be both `censed` and `pooled` without taking extra Heretic damage.
- **Button kills** (game.md §8.1 and §14.11): the original censer hit/cloud/furrow are LMB, including The Altar's attached cloud and Scapegoat's delivered clouds; Shroud's burst and Trial by Fire are E; Cloud of Witnesses and Communion's Wall of Fire augment are Q. Effects added by passives or kill triggers, including Kindling, Pitch, The Sixth Circle and Lazarus, are secondary effects. Secondary kills never feed another damage-on-kill proc or button-kill trigger. Deaths in fire may spread The Sixth Circle, which spreads area only; the pulse budget still applies. Mote pickups and revivals have their own bounded event lineage, not a fresh damage action.
- **States applied here:** `airborne` 0.4 s (Falling Star's arc: about 1 m high, 4 m out, M9), `bound` 1.5 s, `pooled` while on burning ground. Bound enemies are never thrown (as with Falling Star, so piles hold). The Gatekeeper is never `bound` or `airborne`; it can be `pooled` and `censed`, and takes the damage parts.
- **New ability ids:** `breadOfAngels` (E), `trialByFire` (E), `cloudOfWitnesses` (Q). Ability unlocks occupy no augment group. Native censer incense keeps its actual accepted **impact altitude** and 3 m spherical reach, including a hit on the elevated Gatekeeper body; it is not projected down to the main floor. Fire/furrows remain connected ground surfaces; attached incense follows the wearer's local floor. Walls/closed doors/room boundaries block every effect. Pillar uses the local surface of its cloud's impact, with actual base incense reach retained where it would be taller; a capstone never makes a previously hittable body immune. Use canonical host area/LOS/movement validation. Native slot damage metadata: Communion Q **false** at base/Exorcism/rally, **true** with Wall of Fire; base Shroud E **true**, or **false** with The Stake; Bread E **false**, Trial E **true**, Witnesses Q **true**. Lazarus remains a secondary passive discharge, never Q/E damage.

**Composition data (game.md §9.3).** All nodes of Kind `augment` appear exactly once here. Passive/capstone rules do not consume either channel; they still obey source predicates, effect rules and discharge limits.

| Channel | Node IDs |
|---|---|
| Delivery | `heretic.a.3b` |
| Payload | `heretic.a.1b`, `heretic.a.2a`, `heretic.b.1a`, `heretic.b.2b`, `heretic.c.1a`, `heretic.c.2a`, `heretic.c.2b` |

**Enemy event eligibility.** Trickle kills/hits never dispatch enemy-triggered talent/relic/combo discharges or advance kill meters. Ordinary native attacks, their direct shape/state rules and defensive passives still work. Watchers/Seals never supply kill-listener or hit-count events; authored incidental damage uses the boss-part area modifier. Ordinary boss summons permit their explicitly bounded ordinary combat procs, combat streak credit and Armageddon eligibility, but never Bread/extra passive mote harvest or item/halo/XP rewards. Boss parts and trickle never build deed streaks. World damage supplies the core death entitlement and recent setup assist only, never a fabricated player/button kill. Area-only propagation keeps its explicit core exception.

**Kill-discharge data.** `heretic.b.1b` Kindling is this class's kill-triggered talent discharge node. At most **two** are effective in one build, counting learned nodes, Rites and capstones together. A listener still counts when its read state or target is temporarily absent; only actual named-input dormancy or suppression removes its active count. Use real remaining source/phase predicates for offers, not this limit as a dynamic target counter. Each root collects an active node once at that root's **first eligible resolving tick**, bundling all qualifying deaths of that root/tick after native damage; the row's origin/victim caps apply. Later native pulses retain the dispatch flag and cannot re-dispatch that node. Never collect separately per pellet, branch or fatal victim. Eligibility and ordinary damage happen before the secondary bundle; secondary kills cannot seed another bundle. Eligibility still uses per-victim pre-fatal state; the flag and any cooldown remain through death/reassignment/reconnect and the root's delayed lifetime. The Burning Plain/The Sixth Circle's existing area-only propagation exception, native ability damage, Bread's bounded harvest, landing effects, motes and revival listeners are outside this kill-discharge category. There is no banked third proc or retroactive state qualification.

In-tree exclusion: none. Solo capstone with 6: `1a → 1b → 2b → 3a → 3b → cap`; co-op can substitute `2a` for `1b`. The capstone is a revival/mote passive; it does not lock an Exorcism Rite out of the tree. Cross-tree: `a.2a` is the only RMB Payload; `a.1b` and `b.2b` are alternative Communion Payloads. The Long Chain is LMB Delivery and composes with Ploughshares, The Altar or Scapegoat; those three mutually exclusive LMB Payloads define the delivered area.

**Combos.** Bread of Angels feeds on everyone's eligible native kills, so it's best a second before the Fallen's crater or the Betrayer's Silver Bullet through a Binder's pile; Cunning Folk then steers those motes to whoever is hurt, and every mote is a pickup for any "a mote pickup" relic. Exorcism makes the Heretic a source of `airborne` for an independent native hit/kill reading that state and for the Binder's Where They Fall. The Fallen's authored landing passives require its own launch, so an allied Exorcism cannot dispatch them. Exorcism flings the crowd out of the Heretic's own clouds' reach or into the next one. In Both Kinds in a Field of Blood heals two allies at 60 HP/s each. Lazarus and Resurrection together turn three downed allies into the class's big moment: three souls in the swarm, one Communion, three visible blasts with merged damage. Every revive is a "reviving" relic trigger.

**Solo and two-player parties.** In Both Kinds has no second eligible ally and is filtered from those Rite offers; its fork needs the Saint plus at least two other living players/bots. Learned ownership remains legal if an ally dies or leaves, but the fork then falls dormant. The six other nodes are a complete solo capstone path: Communion throws the crowd back, Bread creates a bounded mote harvest, Lazarus turns collecting it into close-range clearance, and the capstone rallies existing motes after a dangerous hit. Sacrament never heals the Saint. A healer build still relies on censer hits and careful cloud placement for damage.

`Kind`: `augment`, `ability`, `passive`, `capstone`. `Modifies`: `LMB`, `RMB`, `Q:<abilityId>`, `E:<abilityId>`, `passive`, `movement`. `States`: what the node applies or reads (game.md §4.11), else empty. `Requires`: extra prerequisites; a predicate explicitly labelled for a Rite filters that offer only and does not invalidate persistent ownership when an ability is unassigned. `Excludes`: only extra exclusions beyond sharing the same resolved named input **and channel**. `Targets` may be an explicit set of Q/E ability IDs; a node occupies its one declared channel only for the currently assigned matching ability. Delivery/Payload below are catalog data, not a freeform guess by implementation.

## 6. Scenario review and implementation handoff

These are reasoned scenarios with 150 HP, 8 m/s, Blessed 20 HP/4 m/s, not executed tests or claims that numerical balance is proven.

**Physician, solo:** A `1a → 1b → 2b → 3a → 3b → cap`, plus B `1b → 2a` and C `1b` = nine. Assign Communion and Bread. Exorcism supplies `airborne` to Pitch; The Long Chain puts the cloud beyond the front rank, Kindling seeds fire from direct kills, and Walk in the Smoke frees movement inside incense. Save Communion until hurt; Bread before a dense clearance yields at most 72 HP if all six motes reach the Saint. Lazarus supplies at most 40 secondary damage/s in a 2 m circle during pickup, and its kills cannot generate another heal harvest. The capstone rallies existing/bounded bonus motes, never creates more. In co-op substitute In Both Kinds for Long Chain: heal two allies at 30 HP/s each, or 60 each while in a Field. The mote rally is still a real rescue moment when no soul is present.

**Pyre-walker, solo:** B `1a → 1b → 2a → 3a → 3b → cap`, plus A `1a → 1b → 2b` = nine. Assign Communion with Exorcism and Trial by Fire; skip Wall of Fire because it excludes Exorcism. Trial draws a U-shaped ribbon; Ploughshares covers the lane in front; throwing an ember-marked crowd out with Exorcism preserves its burn for 2 s through Pitch. Cunning Folk brings scarce ordinary motes toward the hurt Saint and Lazarus makes collecting them a close-range clearance. Fire and incense share the pulse budget, so a spread trades states and coverage rather than multiplying damage. Losing Shroud matters: a bad turn at a wall can still kill this 150-HP caster.

**Martyr, solo:** C `1a → 1b → 2a → 3a → 3b → cap`, B `1b`, and A `1a → 2b` = nine. Assign E Shroud and Q Cloud of Witnesses. The Altar puts ordinary incense on the Saint; Kindling seeds `pooled` from a direct censer kill, The Common Cause slows that overlap, and Shroud's stakes hold the close crowd. Cunning Folk and Lazarus keep the solo mantle build supplied by nearby existing motes; restore Communion at the Confessional if burst healing matters more than the moving mantle. The class never pretends both Qs are castable together. For co-op, place The Altar and the mantle on the Fallen while healing with Sacrament; Pillar reaches flyers in the same floor column.

**Spread 3+3+3, co-op:** A `1a → 2a → 2b`; B `1b → 2a → 2b`; C `1a → 1b → 2a`. Keep base Q/E. Sacrament forks, Communion draws fire, attached incense frees the wearer, and the broken shield supplies a bind for Pitch. No capstone or new ability, but every node is applicable. Solo uses one of the focused paths above rather than paying for a fork with no second ally.

Every displayed sequence opens tier 2 with a tier-1 node and tier 3 with three lower-tier nodes. Each capstone has five lower-tier nodes; the three dip points also have learned-only valid gates. Rites may open higher **Rite** tiers but cannot license persistent higher nodes whose learned-only allocation would fail after the run (§9.5).

**Rites on a fresh solo Saint.** Exorcism immediately adds an exit; Kindling changes where the next chase walks; The Stake exchanges a burst kill for a held cloud opportunity; Walk in the Smoke makes standing inside an existing cloud a movement decision. In Both Kinds is filtered unless at least two other living allies exist. An ability Rite carries the old slot's remaining cooldown and immediately changes the slot; the card states the lost base ability. Cloud of Witnesses can't silently heal or revive, and Bread cannot be mistaken for an immediate rescue shield.

**Boss and range.** All trees retain the 25 m, 20 m/s censer projectile and 60 direct hit; the enemy interaction must provide visible/reachable targets within that range. Base stationary boss damage is about `60/0.85 + 10 = 80.6 HP/s` before downtime, not the Betrayer's output. Fire adds no pulse over cloud coverage. Pillar improves vertical coverage without changing the pulse. Bread on a boss without eligible room enemies is inferior to Shroud; Communion should be restored for an emergency healing build before sealing the boss. Scenario timings and required Watcher range belong in bosses.md; a talent cannot repair a fresh character's mandatory-target reach.

**Remaining validation:** real playtests must measure Saint clear speed, whether The Stake buys enough safety to offset the lost blast, whether attaching incense makes positioning too automatic, and whether six Bread motes are worth losing Shroud. Implementation uses shared root-action limits, deterministic state ownership, revalidated Rite offers and area snapshots from game.md. No test was run or created for this design pass.

### 6.1 Collected veteran: preserve a choice beyond the fixed tree

Use the solo Pyre-walker nine-node build above, with Communion/Exorcism and Trial by Fire. Its three A lower nodes already license A Long Chain; its three B lower nodes license B tier 3. A **Long Chain Rite** adds Delivery beside learned Ploughshares: censers pass the front rank and paint their existing fire furrow at the accepted impact. It does not restore incense or remove the furrow. Walk in the Smoke stays ineligible while this kit has no own incense; a later Altar/Scapegoat Payload substitution or Witnesses ability is needed to provide one. Alternatively, a **Wall of Fire Rite** substitutes for learned Exorcism: Communion loses the launch escape and gains a burning ring. Pitch remains useful through the already learned Irons in the Fire's bind of pooled survivors, not the suppressed Exorcism. Their cooldown, cast and source histories never restart at substitution.

For the Martyr nine-node build, a **Scapegoat Rite** substitutes for The Altar and changes following incense into a cross-lane curtain; a **Ploughshares Rite** substitutes for that same learned Altar and changes LMB into a fire strip. The assigned Cloud of Witnesses still supplies own incense for Walk in the Smoke, Common Cause and Pillar in both cases. Without Witnesses or another effective own incense source, fire-only substitution would make those incense-dependent effects dormant, and no new incense-dependent card could be accepted on stale metadata. A second LMB Payload Rite cannot replace the first; one compatible Delivery Rite may coexist. Learned ownership remains for gates, not for a duplicate cloud or passive callback. These paths trade positioning and emergency tools; their relative clear speed still requires play observation.

### 6.2 Composed and alternative kits

**Long burning delivery:** A Cunning Folk + Exorcism + Lazarus opens A Long Chain; B Ploughshares may then coexist as LMB Payload. The censer passes the front Blessed without direct damage and paints its one furrow at the accepted terminal impact. It never also creates an incense cloud. With The Altar/Scapegoat instead of Ploughshares it delivers that chosen incense geometry beyond the front rank; all six-area and per-enemy pulse limits remain.

**Improved alternatives:** Trial by Fire supplies own pooled/fire for Pitch (when a reachable slow/bind/launch exists) and Sixth Circle; Kindling cannot dispatch twice from its delayed root. Cloud of Witnesses gets Walk in the Smoke, Common Cause's reachable pool/slow setup and Pillar's explicit 8 m volume. Bread of Angels supports Cunning Folk and pickup-side Lazarus; six admitted motes are its resource cap, independent of the kill-discharge channels. These are authored passive synergies, not an assumption that every Communion/Shroud augment transfers to an alternative.

**Survivor prerequisite:** Irons in the Fire cannot pin a fresh 20-HP Blessed or 60-HP Chorister killed by its direct 60. On an all-fodder authored floor with no eligible survivor it is not offered as a Rite. Persistent ownership is allowed, with the card stating its survivor condition; that is an intentional elite tool rather than hidden bonus damage on every kill.
