## Tomas's notes

- Players were eager to play and didn't read the instructions in the Lobby. In game they were lost: they didn't know what the abilities do, whether they do anything, or that they exist at all.
- Everyone agrees weapons feel weak, with little impact. Same in singleplayer testing; more players didn't change it.
- The tank feels like the right direction.
- Can't join mid-game.
- Once a single player enters the next arena, everyone is teleported into it.
- Liked obstacles that break line of sight between players, so abilities can't be used on someone you can't see.
- Being dead until the arena is cleared feels strict and punishing; a reasonable respawn timer could fix it.
- Aiming at a friendly target is hard when they're far away, when overwhelmed by enemies, or when moving fast. Frustrating.
- The muzzle flash looks bad: it's drawn over the front of the gun. Random rotation and some transparency would help.
- Sharing a game means sending the link and the game ID separately. A link with the game ID in it would be easier.

## Analysis

What the observations point to, mapped to the spec.

- **Abilities are invisible to the people they help.** §10 shows the *Healed* vignette only when HP goes up, so healing a full-HP player shows nothing on their screen, and Blasphemy shows nothing to the ally it saves. Nothing tells a player who helped them, and the HUD shows only icons, without names or what they do.
- **Nobody learns the abilities in the Lobby.** The class cards are the only place they're described, and players skip them. The explanation has to be in game, when the ability is ready.
- **Weapons feel weak** although hit markers, kill markers, recoil and tracers are all in (§10). Missing: any sound (§14), screen shake, and a big enough reaction from the crowd. There's also a design cause: against hundreds of 20 HP Blessed, the Betrayer's single-target revolver (60 dmg, at most 3 enemies per shot) wastes most of its damage and removes one or two enemies at a time from a wall of hundreds. Its strength (marks, high damage) only pays off against Choristers and the Gatekeeper, which the party barely met.
- **Ally targeting** needs the ally within 10° of the aim (§5.3), which is small at long range and on fast-moving allies.
- **The muzzle flash** is drawn at full opacity over the barrel, the same image every shot (§11.2).
