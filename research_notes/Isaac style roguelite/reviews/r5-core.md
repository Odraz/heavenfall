# Revision 5: adversarial core and lifecycle review

Reviewed 2026-10-11. This pass reviewed the entire revision-4 `docs/rogue/game.md`, the lore and original brief, horde-fun research, all four available playtest records, revision-4 review conclusions, and the relevant room/boss/item/class catalog contracts. It repaired the existing specification rather than claiming the previous design as new work. Edits in this pass are confined to `game.md` and this review; catalog reconciliation was coordinated with the build/reward and encounter agents.

The method was manual specification reading, arithmetic and repeated adversarial walkthroughs. No executable game code, frontend, tests, builds, benchmark or generator script was created, changed or run. Research interpretation follows the coordinator's verified primary-source review; existing prototype figures are explicitly historical.

## Repeated paper walkthroughs

1. **Fresh solo:** follow the required-route Rite preview, collect useful crowd resources, choose optional key/relic route versus a short boss route, and inspect current H hints. A two-fight rush has 940 pre-boss XP and 2,440 on victory; there is no invented guaranteed learned point before the Advocate. Rite and Sundial choices still function with the base kit. A full clear follows the longer illustrated leveling path.
2. **Veteran solo:** fully allocated Fallen takes a visible Weight of Sin or Heave substitution, with the sacrificed learned effect and state-source dependencies shown. Held ownership licenses only legal subsequent Rite gates, never permanent learned gates. A second Rite cannot displace the first; a capstone or explicit exclusion cannot be hidden. Pass preserves the current build. End/defeat removes the temporary suppression and restores legal learned effects.
3. **Free-respec adversary:** reopen a pending offer, alter Q/E or learned allocation repeatedly, and restore the starting kit. The same original IDs disable/re-enable; no desirable third card or repeated fallback appears. Cap reached through another source pays its bounded once-only fallback when resolving the old source.
4. **Speed-route adversary:** seal before 14:00, qualify, beat the boss, then return to skipped rooms, available Bells and a previously viewed unpaid bargain. All new risk/reward commitments reject. Existing page/chest choices and safe item carry still settle until the bounded exit.
5. **Party admission:** original encounter seals with two admitted profiles; a third joins as a soul, gains future connected XP/halo income and revives normally, but cannot claim that encounter's page/chest. A later Bell captures its own three-profile set. Temporarily offline original members retain source entitlements without receiving intervening ordinary XP/wallet credits.
6. **Reconnect:** live low-HP body times out; HP/dead state at removal is retained. A dead record returns as a soul, unless a real room clear occurred while absent. Cooldowns/proc meters, fixed offer IDs and consumed Confessional heal survive. An active Dominion cast resumes at its original remaining deadline. Host loss still follows declared carried-inventory restoration rather than inventing migration.
7. **Post-win persistence:** store a free placed/thrown drop, buy another charge, auto-use a novel self pickup, claim an earned relic, then close/rejoin. Accepted higher revisions preserve inventory/ownership/Codex without another boss XP/clear/win increment. Untouched earned pages can hold the exit only within its existing bounds.
8. **Spatial generator:** compare identity, mirror and quarter-turn content with canonical socket offsets. Transpose preserves every strip-dependent rectangular distance; invalid raw rotations are excluded before selection, not repaired by damaging authored geometry. The fallback retains fixed safe assignments. Spawn affinities prefer authored ranged sides but player proximity can redirect arrivals safely.

## What remains empirical

The repaired contracts provide declared outcomes for the reviewed scenarios. They do not prove an executable free of defects or enjoyment from indefinite replay. Current generator attempt distribution/loading limits, crest performance/readability, boss duration and healing, relative substitution strength, pickup satisfaction, meaningful decision count, sound fatigue and repeated-run route/build dominance require observation in an implemented playable slice. Optional changes should be guided by those results; no unsupported balance rewrite or claim of infinite authored content is introduced here.

## Final independent integration pass

After the class/item/room/boss repairs landed, another manual cross-read followed paid/free/Sundial source identity, outgoing-action cooldown cancellation, transformed spawn preferences, special-template/fixture validation, Seal headcount creation and synchronization, recipient/rejoin state and victory's bounded exit hold. The catalog and core contracts now agree on those traces.

The pass exposed and repaired one further phase contradiction: `valley` excludes countdown, while the countdown explicitly holds for choices and the worked party learns a point during that hold. Choice/Advocate transactions now accept during a valley **or countdown** under their existing living-player, room, range and source checks; the actual combat seal closes the view and rejects later clicks without consuming an offer. Bell ringing, teleport, item pickup routing and passive regeneration retain their narrower valley/combat rules. Class/item catalogs mirror this boundary.

Three last interpretations were made explicit. Sundial's six halos require the stored initial no-node/cap fallback or a later reached cap; disabling sampled cards never manufactures that fallback. Present sources require a living connected acting human/active bot or an already-emitted area's remaining lifetime, preventing an offline ally's uncast kit from qualifying a new card. An outgoing augment-shaped native area belongs to substitution cleanup: Ploughshares removes retained Altar/Scapegoat censer incense while preserving accepted hits and unrelated Witnesses/Kindling sources.

The choice-rate target is now an **initial per-human playtest hypothesis**, counting intelligible tactical/resource/route tradeoffs rather than repeated shots, automatic confirmations, passive following or a route leader's choices credited to everyone. It does not prescribe extra compulsory menus to hit a number. Adopted decisions now include revision-5 substitution, fixed-card and victory/countdown boundaries; the claim that every gated node matters from a fresh first run was removed.

A final encounter-agent cross-read caught the runtime companion to the special-template validation fault: the old arena loop still said to skip every room without ordinary waves, which would skip the correctly authored `boss` with `waves: []`. Dispatch now uses retained explicit room `kind`: only Breach/Pardoner/Confessional auto-clear without a fight; ordinary combat uses nonempty valid waves/director; the boss seals and runs its authored encounter, clearing only at the victory latch. The ordinary clear/XP/heal path cannot settle an empty boss wave list. Runtime room records retain `kind`, and loader/simulation notes no longer infer non-combat from `waves.length`.

No additional definite contradiction was found after reconciling these cases. This is the stopping point of the manual audit, not a proof that executable behavior, performance, balance or sustained enjoyment is correct. No tests, builds, scripts or play sessions were executed.
