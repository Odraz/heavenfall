## Playable outcome

A generated run offers the complete route/reward loop: key and Reliquary, personal stock, two relic slots, replacement combinations, bargain or refusal, Bells, timed Sundial, Chart and safe teleport. Persistent collections and exhausted-collection Rite fallbacks keep choices available. Progression includes deeds, Edicts, Sentence, Absolution and streaks/assists/crown.

The temporary boss can exercise terminal plumbing; boss-dependent deed/ending evidence remains pending until the full R7 encounter. Optional skipped encounters cannot be silently completed by a reward screen.

## As implemented (2026-10-18)

The contracts below record what was built; [r06-report.md](r06-report.md) has the checks and the play checklist. Protocol 14 → **15**, rules 25 → **26**. No save schema change: schema 1 already held `relics`, `deeds`, `characters.*.relics`, `bestStreak`, `bestSentence` and `lastEdicts`.

### Relics and combos (stage 1)

- **Catalog** (`src/data/relics.ts`): the twelve rows with rarity, source, trigger, rule, numbers, states, combo, cooldown and flavor; the six combos with the benefit each gives up. `RELIC_IDS` is the stable evaluation order. The profile's id lists read from it.
- **Triggers** (`src/sim/relics.ts`): `damageEnemy` reads a one-call context (`Simulation.hitCtx`). `Talents.primary` sets the root's input slot (LMB, RMB, Q, E); Bitter Water and Plume set `item`. Nested damage (a death's discharge, relic damage) has none and can't start a relic. The victim's states are sampled before the killing damage removes it. A native root's positive hits feed the Comb's five-victim window: the tick of its first hit, or 1.5 s for a movement leg (Shadowstep, the Way Back, Gatebreaker; `Root.leg`). The comb lies at the first hit, across the cast's yaw (for a leg, across its path). World halo and mote pickups by a human, successful item uses and streak tiers crossed by a native or direct-item kill are the other events.
- **Evaluation**: events queue while the tick resolves and are evaluated once after pickups (`Relics.flush`): per owner, relics in stable id order, the first event each can use. At most one activation per relic per tick. A no-target attempt starts no cooldown; an area or projectile refused at the cap starts it (§2.4 rule 5). Cooldowns are per relic id on the player (`relicCd`), kept through death, reconnect, unequip and combo changes; a combo uses relic b's timer.
- **Effects**: floor areas (Usher's circle, the First Plea's rune, the Comb's 6 × 2 m rectangle) slow and pool grounded enemies on their connected floor cells for 4 s. Launch rings (Link, Last Plea, Quill, Expulsion, Reversal) launch the 24 nearest unbound, grounded enemies 2 m outward through `Launches` with kind `other`, so no landing bundle. Tile binds the 24 nearest for 0.5 s. Single-target projectiles (embers, the Wheel, Abandoned Mercy) fly a fixed line and hit the first enemy on it. Harrow pierces a 2 m band along the comb's lane, 12 to each, at most 12. The Counterseal flies to a wall- and room-limited end along the accepted aim and bursts there. Choral Fragment, Broken Chorus and Appeal are 3D bursts (Appeal only on the newest landed item area's actual members). Relic damage credits the owner (kills, XP, drops, streak) through `Simulation.relicDamage` and never starts a relic or talent proc. Four persistent areas per owner, item areas included; four relic projectiles per owner.
- **Lifetime**: death and leaving remove the owner's relic areas and projectiles; a cleared room removes those in it; victory removes all. Unequipping or breaking a combo removes that effect's outgoing pieces.
- **Streaks** (`src/sim/streaks.ts`): m12-followup-2.md §3 plus assists. A kill counts once for its killer and for every other living human who damaged it, taunted it with Blasphemy or bound it with the Tile in the last 1.5 s. The Gatekeeper's body and trickle don't count. Tiers 10/25/50/100/200, at most one `streak` event per player per tick; `streakEnd` at 10 or more.
- **Presentation**: `src/client/relicView.ts` draws the areas in the item areas' Field of Blood language (violet ash; Usher's red edge and badge; the rune's burning circle and spokes; the comb's six teeth), projectiles as the ember sprite or the relic icon, rings and bursts in each effect's color. `src/client/relicHud.ts`: the relic strip beside the ability box with each cooldown swept over its icon and the combo's name; the streak banner 18% from the top; the key holder and the Sundial under the minimap. Sounds are synthesized stand-ins, one per kind per 0.25 s. Another player's tier is a chat line.
- **Equipping**: the Lobby's Relics view on each class card (`src/ui/relics.ts`: twelve fixed cells, unowned dark with `?`; two slots; the card with its dormancy note; the six combos as far as discovered) saves to the profile. In a run only the Advocate equips (`relics` request): owned or pawned, distinct, outside combat.
- **Usability** (`src/sim/relicUse.ts`): the §2.1.1 table for the chest's first card and every card's note: Blasphemy in the party, incense, bind/pool/slow producibility through the build resolver's closure, and the Q/E direct-damage table.

### Key, chest, offers, Advocate (stage 2)

- **The key** (`src/sim/route.ts`): a `KEY` pickup (structural kind 11, never fades) at the center of the key room's 4 × 4 reward square on its clear, in a gold pillar. A living human within 1 m takes it; a holder who dies or leaves drops it at the nearest legal dry floor of their room (else the active room's entry). The holder is named under the minimap; the map shows the key glyph on the key room while it lies there.
- **The chest** appears at the Reliquary's square center on its clear, in a 30 m white beam with a pale blue core until opened. F within 2.5 m (eye to lid, in sight): the key holder opens it and the key is used up; then each human in the Reliquary's seal opens their own offer. Later first-time joiners get nothing.
- **Relic offers** (`src/sim/relicOffers.ts`): two unowned chest relics (one if one is left). Card 1 is weighted 60/30/10 among the presently usable ones if any, card 2 among the rest, from `mulberry32(fmix32(seed ^ offerId ^ playerKey ^ 0x5e1ec7))` with run-global offer ids shared with Rites. Reopening shows the same cards. Shown ids are reserved against the player's other pending offer (`Finish your other offer first`). A complete chest collection gets a Rite instead (`source: chest`, free, with Pass). Take saves ownership at once (`ProgressState.relics`); Pass settles with nothing; Esc keeps the offer; a claim must be made at the open chest. An owed, reachable chest holds the exit like a page.
- **The Advocate** (F within 2.5 m): one view with Talents, Relics, the bargain and, while lit, the Sundial. The Confessional heals each human to full on their first entry this floor.
- **The bargain** (`src/sim/bargain.ts`): once per human per floor. Two unowned relics, or a legal Rite with a free slot once the collection is complete, or no bargain (never halos for health). Accept charges 30% of the class's max HP (rounded down) until the run ends, shown as a dark striped segment at the HP bar's end; the pawn can be equipped at once and is saved, with its slot, only by the victory settlement. Decline closes it. Victory closes every unaccepted bargain. A paid Rite at the cap or with an illegal card is refused with no HP charge.

### Bells and the Sundial (stage 3)

- **Bells**: the floor's two bell rooms hang a gold bell 3 m over their square's center after the ordinary clear (and a bell glyph on the map). F within 2.5 m of its rim, in a valley, before victory rings it: `pending`, and the cleared room counts down again from its retained entry socket, its cleared bit unset so the doors close normally. Leaving cancels it back to `available`. The seal spends it and swaps in one Judgment wave (`judgmentWave`: 40% of the room's TP budget in its last wave's mix, plus two Dominions at four players, three under Host; its XP ledger is 0.4 × budget + 120). No halos; its own mote cap. On its clear the room is cleared again, the dead respawn, the living aren't healed, no clear XP or reward, and one Rite page (`source: bell`) waits for each human in its seal; the room's own waves come back.
- **The Sundial** is lit until 14:00 of run time (25 200 ticks) or the boss seal, shown under the minimap. At the Advocate its offer shows two cards to select (`source: sundial`, Select/Unselect; nothing is applied or spent); with no node or a full hand it is a pending six-halo fallback. The boss seal strictly before 14:00 sets `sundialQualified` on every admitted record and applies a selected, still legal card as a Rite (or pays the fallback, or six halos if the hand has filled since). At 14:00 the pending offers disappear. Sundial and bargain offers never hold the exit.

### Pilgrim's Return (stage 4)

Hold M and click a cleared room (non-combat rooms once visited, the boss room only after victory, not your own): a 1.5 s channel (gold rings closing, a chime, the target outlined on the map). It breaks on releasing M, moving more than 0.3 m, taking damage, death or any countdown. Arrival is the target's lowest-numbered socket's entry cell for the player's index, as a host teleport; the key travels with its holder. The marked amphora has dropped the Chart since R3; the Chart is unchanged.

### Deeds, Edicts and records (stage 5)

- **Edicts** (`src/data/edicts.ts`): the host's choice in the Lobby (`edicts` request: host only, in the Lobby, once the host profile has cleared the heaven or the run saves nothing; clients see the row read-only), or the solo player's on the Singleplayer setup. Saved as `heavens.lastEdicts[1]`; they travel in `start` and `floorSync`. Host: a wave with a commander brings one more before scaling (alive cap 3), a Bell three; the extra pays 60 XP. Penance: no living heal at clears or victory. Swift Hymns: Chorister globes and Orb Volleys ×1.3. Poverty: coins and gold halos fade after 10 s. Trumpet: the inherited Gatekeeper's first Judgment comes at 3 s (R7 owns the authored phase-2 move). The Sentence multiplies every XP gain by 1 + 0.15 × Sentence, shown under the XP bar and on Results.
- **Deeds** at the boss's death, for a saved run and a record present at the start whose profile had won the heaven before: Unbroken (no death), Mercy (`sundialQualified`), Armageddon (best streak ≥ 200), Under Sentence (Sentence ≥ 3). Each once per profile; its relic is owned at once and saved with the victory (`ProgressState.deeds`). Results names each.
- **Records**: the best streak per character (max) and the best Sentence won per heaven are saved by the settlement. The class card shows Absolution past level 10 and the records in its tooltip; the Profile screen shows them in its rows. Results shows a Best streak column, the streak crown (best streak; ties: the earlier to reach it), relics claimed or pawned, deeds and the Sentence.
