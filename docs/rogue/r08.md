## Playable outcome

Every shipped feature can be understood from what it shows and plays, and from its descriptions. **Before:** buying an item, being refused, taking a Rite, opening the Reliquary or picking up the key makes no sound. A thrown Bitter Water sounds like a breaking pot. A Watcher is a coded eye glyph. The H panel never mentions `F`, `1 / 2`, `M` or `Esc`, and in solo it shows no general lines at all. **After:** each of those events has its own cue. Boss and elite warnings can't be stolen by gunfire in a crest. The Watchers and the Seal use the supplied art. The hints describe the controls and the kit you actually have. The asset manifest says what is really in the game.

Frequent combat sounds keep the recorded takes already installed. Infrequent cues stay synthesized where audio-prompts.md allows it. Optional music, voices and passive-node paintings are not part of R8.

**Exclusions:** no new mechanics, no menu redesign, no protocol/rules/save change (if one turns out to be needed, it is recorded in implementation-decisions.md first). No benchmark. Full-content loading/performance evidence belongs to R9.

## Implementation contracts

| Concern | Contract |
|---|---|
| Ownership | Sound is client presentation of **accepted** host facts: events, snapshot/route/kit diffs, and own interaction results. A cue never decides timing, damage or success. A refused request never plays a success cue. |
| Identity | **`SfxName` = prompt ID.** Rename cues to their audio-prompts.md IDs (hyphenated ones as quoted keys, e.g. `'item-balm'`, `'relic-rune'`), so a future `<id>-NN.wav` replaces the cue with no code change. Renames: `tollWindup→tollTell`, `tollStrike→tollImpact`, `ringWhoosh→tollPersonalWhoosh`, `callWindup→callTell`, `sealJudged→callReleased`, `watchersOpen→watcherOpen`, `phaseChoirThreat→bossTransition`, `bellToll→judgmentBell`, `relicRune…relicChorus→relic-rune…relic-chorus`. Cues with no prompt ID (`judgmentPrewarn`, `cantor*`, `carrier*`, `crest`, `spawnWarning`, `rewardArrive`, `relicRing`) keep their names and get coverage rows. |
| Event → cue | A new pure module `src/client/cueMap.ts` holds the mapping as data: `Record<GameEvent['type'], CueRule \| 'silent'>` plus `Record<TalentFxKind, …>`, so a new event type fails typecheck until it is mapped or declared silent. `GameSounds.event` dispatches through it; special cases (tells with resume windows, mass kill) stay in `sounds.ts`. |
| Purchase vs refusal | The client records `requestId → sourceId` for its own `interact` requests. `interactionResult.accepted` on a shop/Chart source → `purchase` (Chart → `chartReveal`); `accepted === false` → `interactionReject`; chest/Bell acceptance plays nothing here (their state change plays `chestOpen` / `judgmentBell`). |
| Duplicates | Own actions keep the existing pattern: predicted own cue immediately, the echoed event skipped for `playerId === localId`. Others' events play after `renderDelay` at their position. Diff-driven cues (`chestOpen`, `riteTaken`, `relicClaim`, `levelUp`) fire once per observed transition and never on the first sync after join/reconnect. |
| Bounds | State cues (`state-bind`, `-launch`, `-land`, `-fire`, `fireOut`) play **once per event, never per slot or per tick**. Each is limited to 4 per second per family by a shared limiter that replaces `relicSound`'s private 250 ms map. Loops: at most one `incense` voice (the nearest active cloud within 12 m) and one `confessionalAmbience` (local player inside the Confessional), like `hazard()`'s fire voice. No loops for the alternatives in R8 (see inputs). |
| Priority | Add `PRIO_WARNING = 5` for cues that warn of incoming damage: `judgmentPrewarn`, `judgmentCharge`, `tollTell`, `tollPersonalWhoosh`, `callTell`, `volleyWindup`, `dominionTell`, `watchmanAim`, `cantorTell`, `carrierMark`. They're never stolen by lower priorities. The voice cap and limiter don't change. |
| Hints | `GENERAL_HINTS` gains the four §1.4 control lines and shows in solo too; the revive and chat lines stay multiplayer-only. LMB/RMB lines append the active augment's one-line hint (new `hint` field on augment nodes, ≤ 90 characters); dormant augments appear only in the T cards. |
| Art | Cutouts go to `assets/items/<id>.png`, RGBA and trimmed, exported at the size their consumer draws: 256² for the Watchers and the Seal (they are drawn ≤ 1.2 m). Sources become `assets/art-src/<id>.png`; the JPEG originals are archived in `tmp/asset-originals-2026-10-25/` with SHA-256, as earlier receipts were. |
| Compatibility | Client-only. Protocol 21, rules 28 and save schema unchanged. |
