# Items and relics

**Purpose:** the complete consumable, relic, combo and deed catalog for the Pearly Gates vertical slice. [game.md](game.md) is authoritative; this file supplies content and item-specific resolutions. All values are initial tuning values. No content here requires a second dungeon.

**Status:** revision-7 content review (with revision-8 shared-contract/asset reconciliation) covers solo, four humans, bots, full inventory, exhausted collections, dependent state sources, deterministic area/attack geometry and proc loops. The rules are an implementation specification; implementation and observed play are still necessary to validate correctness, feel and performance.

## 1. Items (consumables)

### 1.1 Shared rules

- Two inventory slots; one type per slot; stack cap in each row. Purchases add one charge to inventory. Storage/purchase chooses the lowest-index matching slot below its row's cap, then the lowest-index empty slot; two stacks of the same type are legal. World drops follow game.md §7.3: always store, or remain on the floor if both slots are full. No automatic activation or refresh, in combat or valleys. Bots never consume items.
- Manual uses share the player's **1 s cooldown**. Contact storage does not depend on cooldown and never uses the item. Simultaneous contacts resolve by distance, then player ID. Remove the pickup only after one successful transaction.
- Resolve that tick's accepted manual item attempts before item storage contacts, with slot 1 before slot 2 if both were requested. A successful first use starts the shared cooldown and refuses the second; a refused first spends nothing and leaves a legal second attempt possible. Store-or-remain still applies to subsequent pickup contacts under that cooldown; no input ordering can produce two uses inside one second.
- A refused use (full HP, stronger shield, invalid placement, cooldown, dead player) costs nothing and emits no relic event. Successful use consumes one charge, marks `tried`, starts cooldown and emits one `itemUsed`. Storing a drop emits only `itemCollected`; contact never emits `itemUsed`. No catalog relic reads item storage.
- Same-type `self` effects refresh, never add. Manual Feather reuse refreshes its 8 s; Seal reuse/refresh must pass §1.3's shield-strength check. Different types coexist. Each owner has at most one placed/thrown instance of each type, **including flight**. Successful manual reuse replaces its previous instance; pickup contact only stores, regardless of whether an instance exists. Accepted flights continue through owner death using their accepted trajectory and reserved area entitlement; landed areas outlive owner death to normal expiry. Disconnect/leave removes that owner's areas/flights. Death clears self effects/shields. Combat clear removes offensive areas/flights; surviving self effects otherwise expire normally. Active effects never persist to another run.
- Areas belong to one room. Radius uses horizontal distance on connected walkable floor in that room; walls/closed doors block effects. Separate inaccessible terraces are excluded. Every eligible enemy inside is affected. Overlapping same-type item areas provide one ongoing pulse/state membership, owned by the newest covering instance (tie: lower owner ID); different types coexist. Independent one-time Chain/Plume attempts still obey their per-instance rule and cannot stack control multipliers/duration. Bitter Water shares a **per-enemy 0.5 s minimum damage interval across all owners/instances**, retained on exit/re-entry: landing or a newer staggered pool cannot reset it. At a scheduled pulse, only the newest covering instance may damage; skipping a duplicate pulse never queues it. States refresh, never stack.
- **Area ordering:** an item's immutable `activatedTick` is its successful placement tick or its thrown landing tick, when its area first becomes real; an accepted flight has only its reserved entitlement until then. `Newest` means greatest `activatedTick`, then lower owner ID, then lower instance ID. Each instance retains its accepted identity and activation tick through synchronization; reopening a menu or reconnecting never makes it newer. This order governs overlapping same-type item ownership and Appeal's area selection. An older long throw landing after a newer placed tool legitimately becomes the newest landed area, without emitting another `itemUsed`.
- Thrown items fly at **20 m/s** along the accepted normalized 3D aim, landing at the first wall/enemy, **owning-room boundary** or **20 m of total 3D path** (at most **1 s** in flight), on the legal supporting floor below that endpoint. Clip a boundary crossing to the last point 0.01 m inside the cast's owning room, including an open doorway; neither flight nor area may enter a vestibule or another room. A straight-up aim in this roofless dungeon therefore expires its flight and lands on the floor beneath the bounded endpoint; it cannot reserve an area forever. Ignore allies and souls. Before consuming a charge, validate a legal same-room starting floor projection and that the bounded endpoint has a legal projection or backward fallback. A later intercepted flight uses the same floor resolution; for a blocked cell, walk backward along the accepted travelled trajectory to the last legal floor projection in the user's room, including the already validated starting projection. No legal admission projection → refuse without charge. Duration starts on landing; flight counts as active. No area crosses a wall or room boundary.
- **Accepted effect records:** replicate an accepted use's immutable source/instance ID, host start tick, owning room and normalized 3D aim; yaw alone cannot describe a vertical throw. A thrown-flight record includes accepted start, static terrain/room/path bound, expiry and its reserved area entitlement. The host resolves any earlier moving-enemy interception and publishes the actual landing floor anchor, `activatedTick` and end tick when converting that reservation. Rejoin restores the existing trajectory/deadline or landed area, never a fresh flight or another use. Counterseal's body-transparent flight can publish its already resolved terminal endpoint at creation. Clients interpolate accepted state and cannot choose collision victims, landing points or effect lifetime.
- Areas make enemies inside `pooled`. Chain Vial and Plume attempt their additional state **once per enemy per instance**, at first covered membership (including enemies already inside on landing); record the attempt even if control immunity, `bound` or an existing launch prevents it. Re-entry or expiry of that immunity never schedules a later attempt. States retain independent source/expiry tokens: a new bind contributes its own 2.5 s source, never adds duration to another source or shortens that source's remaining duration. Bound or already-airborne enemies cannot be launched again by this catalog; boss immunities are game.md §4.11. Item damage uses the usual bound multiplier, with `origin=item`, never Q/E damage.
- Bitter Water, Ash, Chain Vial and Plume affect ground enemies with feet within 1 m vertically of the **area's supporting floor** on connected reachable cells; ordinary flyers pass above these floor tools. An explicitly grounded Cherub qualifies during actual floor contact, as game.md §14.11 requires; its remaining bind still prevents a launch. An elevated terrace does not inherit a pool from the floor below. Ward intercepts the sphere of radius 4 m centered 1 m above its placement floor, and `pooled` means that enemy's cylinder intersects this sphere with geometry line of sight. Relic floor areas/control rings use the same ground/1 m rule; their floor origin resolution is §2.2. Projectiles and the explicitly volumetric damage bursts can hit flying enemies normally. Gatekeeper/part `pooled` membership uses bosses.md §3.6: a floor pool at the lip cannot reach the raised body through the dais; only an actually intersecting volumetric area or a legal floor area on the dais can supply it. Control immunities still hold.
- Every class has useful swarm choices. Mirror Ward is deliberately specialized for ranged/boss rooms; door sigils make buying it an informed inventory decision. It has no unrelated second swarm effect.

### 1.3 Seal, healing and movement

- Seal shares the existing shield channel with Martyr's Shroud: never a second channel, extra shield, Shroud extension or conversion into a Shroud burst. If current shield is **≥50**, manual Seal is refused and a world Seal is treated as presently useless for pickup routing. Otherwise replace with 50 for 8 s. Replacement never bursts the old Shroud. Seal expiry/replacement/depletion never bursts. Its source ID cannot become a Heretic E kill.
- A Seal is active for routing only while its own shield remains. Shroud replacement ends it immediately. A legal reuse/refresh when the current shield is **<50** restores 50 and 8 s; one use, never stacking. At ≥50 a full-inventory world Seal remains on the floor instead of consuming itself for a duration-only refresh. Balm honors the bargain's reduced max HP. Healing and shielding never convert or affect another player.
- Feather affects speed only, before normal slow/wading factors. No invulnerability, knockback or teleport; collisions, melee, hazards and Judgment still apply. Slow uses the strongest factor, not multiplied overlapping sources.

### 1.4 Mirror Ward and reflection

- Reflectable: traveling Cherub arrows, Chorister globes and boss Orb Volley projectiles. Melee, beams, environmental damage, Judgment, Smite and contact damage are not projectiles. Boss phase changes never make Judgment reflectable.
- Intercept crossing the dome's radius; a hostile shot already inside at placement is intercepted once on the next tick. Switch to friendly collision immediately and remove hostile shoot-down/shatter behavior: a returned globe cannot be shot down to create its former hostile-globe blast. Aim back toward its original spawn point, keeping speed, remaining lifetime and **unmodified base damage**; if interception is exactly at that point, reverse its accepted incoming velocity. No homing/pierce/splash; first enemy/wall/owning-room boundary ends it. Allies/souls cannot be hit; original shooter need not survive. Bound multiplies damage at eventual impact once.
- Overlapping wards choose earliest trajectory intersection (tie: area ID). A projectile reflects only once across all wards; friendly shots ignore subsequent wards. Credit kill to ward owner with `origin=item`. Reflection can grant normal XP/streak/drop rewards but never any new relic/talent proc and never Q/E/LMB/RMB attribution. If owner leaves, an already reflected shot expires harmlessly on contact.
- Reflection mutates the accepted projectile in place, creating no extra projectile. Retain its original **hostile-budget allocation** until contact/expiry, independently of its now-friendly collision and secondary item provenance. Thus a return cannot take one of the 64 hostile-reserved slots away by recategorizing it into an additional player allocation. It consumes no relic-projectile entitlement; the global projectile count and original remaining lifetime still apply.

## 2. Relics and authored combos

### 2.1 Collection and loadout

- Twelve profile-bound relics, two equipped per character, no duplicates; swaps only in Lobby/Confessional. Eight chest/bargain choices, four deeds. Claim grants **ownership**, not auto-equipping. Pawned relics may be equipped at Confessional but are saved only on victory. A bargain must be accepted before boss death; victory closes unpaid bargain offers and releases their reservations, including offers previewed earlier. A late accept cannot spend a harmless post-victory HP price or create a pawn after its settlement event. Existing earned chest offers remain claimable after victory. Deed awards arrive after combat and can be equipped next run.
- Weights **60/30/10 per eligible Common/Rare/Legendary relic**. From unowned, unpawned, unreserved chest IDs, draw card 1 weighted from **presently usable** IDs if any; otherwise from the full list. Draw card 2 weighted from the remaining full list. One remaining ID → one card. If no unowned/unpawned ID exists, the Reliquary offers a Rite with its normal cap/no-node fallback; the **bargain offers only a legal Rite with a free Rite slot**, otherwise no bargain (never 30% max HP for six fallback halos). If IDs merely remain reserved by another pending offer, show `Finish your other offer first` and grant nothing. Compatibility uses §2.1.1 at first creation and never rerolls after respec/rejoin/reopen. Label an unusable collection card `For another build or character`. Stored offers reserve their shown IDs across that player's other offers; game.md's offer transaction owns resolution/decline.
- **Free chest refusal:** a created free relic offer has an explicit `Pass`, settling that chest entitlement without ownership, halos or a Rite, releasing only that offer's reservations and removing its exit hold. It is terminal for that source; repeat Pass/claim requests cannot reopen it or redraw cards. Esc only closes the view and preserves the same cards/reservations. A dormant collection card remains claimable; passing is a voluntary refusal, never a no-node fallback. The paid bargain keeps its separate terminal decline, and an exhausted chest's Rite uses game.md §9.10's free Rite Pass.
- **Pending paid Rite:** if another earned source fills the fourth Rite slot after a bargain was previewed, or every fixed bargain card becomes illegal, a later bargain accept is refused without HP cost or reward. It never takes the free-page six-halo fallback; the player can restore the original card's legal setup or decline. Sundial's separate fallback is settled only at a qualifying boss seal, never just for viewing an exhausted offer at the Advocate.
- **Choice timing:** earned Rite/relic choices, unpaid pre-victory bargains and valid Advocate loadout edits may commit in a valley or an encounter countdown, while their existing living-player, interaction-range and room checks still pass. The actual combat seal closes overlays and rejects later commits without discarding retained offers. Countdown allowance applies to these choice transactions; item pickup routing still uses §1.1's narrower valley definition, as do the core regeneration and teleport rules.
- Class-agnostic means identical effects/no class restrictions, **not that every kit meets every trigger**. States may come from teammates. Q/E-kill relics need a producible native enemy-damaging slot source, including actual retained native delayed damage; healing, pure shielding, pure dash and pure buff do not become damage through a relic. Equip cards show `Dormant: assigned Q/E cannot kill` where applicable, with a separate `Pending native source: <name>, until <expiry>` note if an actually retained original-slot source can still trigger it. State cards likewise distinguish currently producible setup from an expiring retained taunt/incense source. An assigned damaging alternative/augment can awaken a seal for future casts; a pending older action never implies that the new ability damages. Binder has no direct-damage Q/E and Betrayer has no direct-damage Q in this slice, so their respective seals honestly remain collection for another character.
- Relics add bounded power versus empty slots. Sidegrades are **between choices**, or between a combo and its separate effects. Border rarity controls discovery, not a damage multiplier. Edicts, fresh classes and Rites extend challenge; twelve collection entries alone cannot make one authored dungeon inexhaustible.
- Every relic has one trigger and visible effect. `CD` means internal cooldown shown on its icon. Successful creation starts CD; failed target search does not. Allocation-cap rejection is the explicit exception: discard that autonomous activation and start CD as §2.4.5 says. At most one activation per relic per sim tick; aggregate that root's eligible events on its resolving tick, never one proc per fatal pellet/victim. A later native pulse may trigger that relic after CD expires; talent kill-discharge nodes instead retain their stronger once-per-root flags. Cooldown events are discarded, never queued. Convert seconds to ticks at 30 Hz; expiration precedes that tick's new events.

### 2.1.1 Offer compatibility (deterministic metadata)

Evaluate with the owner's effective loadout, living connected party members' effective kits (including active bots), equipped relics, usable inventory charges and currently active same-room areas/hazards. Unseen, inactive or merely authored future hazards are not present sources, and compatibility never reveals unseen room contents. A dead/disconnected ally's uncast kit is not a producible source; an already emitted area counts only while its authored lifetime remains active. Each attack/ability/item metadata record exposes applied states, required state/source predicates and `canDamageEnemies`, after active Delivery/Payload augments and any Rite substitution are resolved. Suppressed learned augments are owned for gates only; they supply no future attack effect or state-source metadata. Existing areas retain only the cleanup/lifetime permitted by game.md's substitution transaction, never renewed attribution. A damaging projectile/area reflected by another effect does not become an ability source.

**Retained native setup:** an actually surviving same-room accepted source may qualify the corresponding trigger from its retained owner, original input slot, `depth=0`, state/damage metadata and remaining authored lifetime, even after a current assignment changes. Count existing taunt/incense tokens for Usher/Thurible, and original-slot native pending damage for the Seals, only if that accepted source actually retains those capabilities after cleanup. Comb may additionally read a retained native attack's **unconsumed** first hit-count window when its accepted hit set plus remaining authored native budget can reach five; an already consumed window or later cloud pulses cannot qualify it. Expired, canceled, removed or secondary sources supply nothing. This rule neither promises that a particular old Shroud survives a change nor changes its accepted behavior: use its actual retained metadata. Show current assigned dormancy and the expiring pending source separately. Revalidation never extends the source, casts another action or redraws a fixed offer.

**Producible source closure:** seed the source set with effective unconditional native attacks/abilities, usable carried items and actual active source tokens, including only the retained native capabilities above. Then repeatedly add an equipped passive/relic's output states only when its own trigger/setup is producible from the states and actions already in that set. Stop when no state/source is added; the catalog is finite and adding a fact never removes another fact. Conditional effects cannot qualify one another through an unseeded cycle. `usher` needs a producible taunt and its owner's direct enemy damage before contributing slow/pool; `link` needs producible bind and its owner's direct damage before contributing launch; `tile` needs producible pool and its owner's direct damage before contributing bind; First Plea needs its owner's producible native Q-kill source before contributing slow/pool; Comb needs that owner's qualifying native five-enemy attack/window before contributing slow/pool. Apply a combo's effective output/setup in place of its replaced relic effect. A presently active contributed area may seed the set independently for its remaining lifetime. Keep source facts typed by owner, origin/depth/input slot, floor/volumetric reach, eligible target kinds and whether a launch emits the direct landing bundle. Relic/combo `airborne` cannot license As We Fell/Where They Fall; a floor-only item bind cannot license Brought Low's flyer binding; a `pooled` floor is not automatically own incense/fire. This same closure evaluates state-dependent talent offer predicates on the proposed effective kit; raw `States applies` text alone is never evidence that a dormant effect can supply a state. It predicts an available setup rather than an enemy currently in range, and does not grant actual casts, free item charges or an additional proc.

### 2.2 The twelve relics

All radii obey room/wall rules. Relic damage is `origin=relic`; proc rules §2.4. A ring is one wave, never repeated DPS. State rings affect at most **24 nearest eligible enemies**, ties by stable entity ID. Damage effects specify their cap; no cap is imposed on consumable area coverage or class horde attacks.

**Geometry and valid targets:** Usher's circle, First Plea's rune, Comb's rectangle and the Link/Tile/Last Plea/Quill control rings are floor effects. Project a death/impact origin vertically onto the nearest legal supporting floor beneath it in that room; if that cell is invalid, choose the nearest reachable floor cell within 2 m, tie by canonical cell order. No legal origin → no creation/CD. Their radius/nearest tests use horizontal distance and connected-floor/1 m membership from §1.1. Control rings skip bound or already-airborne enemies for launches, and skip immune bodies for binds; no eligible affected enemy → no ring/CD. A floor pool may still be created into an empty spot to support the next wave. Choral Fragment and Broken Chorus are **3D damage spheres** about the owner's body center and the selected airborne enemy's body center respectively, with body-center range and geometry line of sight; they can damage flyers. Counterseal is an aimed volumetric burst as below. All autonomous relic/combo target selection excludes Watchers/Seals; their accepted incidental damage takes the boss-part area modifier, but their hits/deaths never satisfy Comb, kill/state relics or streak triggers (bosses.md §3.7). No effect reaches a different room.

**Comb's attack:** one native weapon shot, Scourge swing, stationary ability impact, accepted native swept movement leg, or native ability cloud's **first** pulse is one eligible attack. Count distinct enemies taking positive native enemy damage; five pellets or five ticks against one enemy count one. A movement leg accumulates its distinct positive hit set across its accepted swept segments and emits its five-hit event once, on the first resolving tick that reaches five; it does not create one new attack per segment or re-emit after five more victims. A cloud counts its first pulse only, never its lifetime. Item hits, heals, revives, props, boss parts and proc damage do not count. For a shot/swing/impact, the first enemy is first along its trajectory or nearest its impact center, ties by ID. For movement, retain the first eligible positive victim's accepted hit point (tick, swept intersection order, then incarnation), even if it later dies; the comb stays at that recorded point rather than following the victim. Finish that resolving tick's original damage before placing the comb, so it cannot retroactively slow those kills.

**Native censer hit-count window:** combine that shot's positive direct impact and its first native cloud pulse into one hit set, deduplicating victim incarnations; delivered Scapegoat clouds contribute their first-pulse group to that same window. Ploughshares uses its direct hit plus the furrow's first authored native damage-pulse group instead of incense; later entering victims/periodic fire pulses never accumulate toward this threshold. One killed impact victim plus four different positively damaged first-pulse victims qualifies as five. Retain the first positive hit point/order even if that victim dies before the pulse. Emit the five-hit threshold at most once for that window; an impact that already reached five cannot produce another threshold from its area. Later pulses never increase this count or start another `hits 5+` attack, although their direct native kills retain their original slot and may satisfy separate kill relics after their cooldowns. Passive fire/proc damage never contributes to the window.

**Comb's heading:** each root retains immutable `castPlanarHeading`: normalized horizontal accepted aim, or accepted cast yaw if the horizontal length is ≤0.000001. A stationary/delayed impact uses that captured heading. A native damage leg may retain its own immutable `planarHeading` on the **same original root**: a straight Shadowstep/Way Back cut captures its accepted path direction when movement begins; steered Gatebreaker captures the accepted segment direction at its first eligible positive hit. A zero-length horizontal path falls back to `castPlanarHeading`. Way Back's second press therefore uses the real return-to-marker path, never first-press placement yaw or current camera aim. No new root, cooldown or used-node allowance is created. The comb's long axis is perpendicular to its resolved heading; Harrow travels along it. Store the first-hit point and resolved heading with the comb through synchronization. A vertical censer shot has a legal cast-yaw lane and cannot rotate it by turning before its first pulse; a steered charge cannot rotate the first-hit lane by turning after that hit.

**Counterseal flight:** capture the host's accepted aim and owning room at successful `itemUsed`, including a pickup refresh. An owner whose accepted feet have no room (`roomOf = 255`, e.g. a vestibule) creates no stamp and starts no relic CD; the successful item use itself still settles normally. Travel at 20 m/s along that 3D ray to its endpoint, at most 20 m from the owner's eye; solid terrain/closed leaves or the first owning-room boundary shorten the endpoint to **0.01 m before the blocking surface/boundary**, so the burst origin is in free space in the original room. The stamp has no direct hit damage, ignores bodies in flight, and bursts once at that endpoint in a **radius 3 m 3D sphere**, with geometry line of sight and the nearest-12 damage cap. It does not search/home toward an enemy or project its burst through a wall onto the floor. This is the one visible stamp burst, never an additional impact hit.

Starting/stopping a combo removes its outgoing persistent areas/projectiles even when b remains equipped, and preserves both relic timers. Discovery does not award another upgrade. ### 2.4 Ownership, procs and lifetime

These content constraints use game.md's authoritative pipeline.

1. Kill triggers belong to the credited killer; sample victim states **immediately before killing damage**, before removing enemy/areas. Ally state application can enable your direct kill but does not steal an ally kill; assists follow game.md §10.
2. Q/E provenance is the source slot **when cast**, retained through an ability's native delayed area, pulse, landing or burst and later assignment swaps. Rain of Fire, Trial by Fire, Witnesses and native Shroud break damage stay depth 0 at their original slot. An augment changing that root's shape/damage stays depth 0; a separately generated passive on-hit/on-kill/landing discharge is depth 1 and cannot start another proc. Shroud replacement/expiry is never a kill. Items, reflection, barrels, relics/combos and hazards never acquire source-slot kills.
3. Direct item damage can start kill/state relics, **except reflection**. Relic/combo/reflected, prop and world/secondary damage cannot start any damage/kill/hit-count relic or talent proc. Player-owned secondary damage may yield ordinary kill credit, XP, streak and drops permitted by enemy reward flags; world damage settles enemy reward entitlements and recent real assists but has no invented player killer. State applications can be read by later direct attacks; this is the intended synergy. A tier crossed by a world/secondary death cannot start another relic, including through an ordinary assist. Later real pickup events remain eligible.
   - A direct owned **item** launch dispatches one landing-resolution bundle: each applicable owned class landing passive responds once per owner/node, respecting its authored ownership, source and position predicates. Allied Binder's Where They Fall may respond where its row permits. Resolve control before damage, then canonical owner ID/node ID order within each group. Outputs are depth 1 with no Q/E slot or capstone bonus, retain each class's per-tick/secondary caps, and cannot trigger relics or another landing event. A relic/combo launch never dispatches this bundle. Independent later player attacks may read all these states normally.
4. Each death emits once. Finish one direct root's damage/deaths for the resolving tick, produce immutable events, evaluate equipped relics in stable relic-ID order with victims in accepted hit order (ties: entity incarnation). Take that relic's first eligible successful effect for the tick; a no-target attempt may inspect the next original event but never manufactures a new event. Effects cannot rewrite an earlier event's state snapshot; a combo resolves in b's place. No `pooled → bind → death → pool` same-event cascade or slot-order exploit. A delayed native Q cloud can legitimately create another First Plea rune after its 5 s CD if it produces a later qualifying primary kill; the cloud's once-per-root talent-discharge flags are unchanged.
5. Per owner maximum **4 persistent item/relic area entitlements combined**, including **area slots reserved by accepted thrown flights**, and **4 relic/combo projectiles**; row one-instance limits are stricter. Check/reserve both projectile and landing-area capacity before consuming a thrown charge; on landing convert its reservation into its area, never request a fifth slot. A successful same-type replacement reuses its old area entitlement. At area/global-projectile capacity a manual item is refused without charge; world pickup follows store-or-remain. An autonomous proc may skip its creation and starts its usual CD to bound repeated allocation attempts; no delayed proc queue. Instant rings finish that tick. Four humans permit at most 16 persistent item/relic area entitlements and 16 relic projectiles inside the global budget; traces are batched visuals, not permanent entities.
6. Use strongest slow, never multiply; bind sources keep independent expiry as above. Bound blocks launch. Relics cannot root/slow/launch Gatekeeper, stationary boss parts or souls. Boss summons retain their normal enemy immunities and can trigger state/kill relics but grant no XP/halos/items. Ranged boss adds may roll ordinary motes only within the boss fight's shared eight-mote cap; no Bread or passive harvest from adds, preventing an endless sustain farm. Empty target search does not start CD; entity-cap rejection is the explicit capacity exception in rule 5.
7. Death removes persistent relic areas/projectiles and self shields/buffs, retaining cooldowns through revival; owned item areas keep their normal expiry as §1.1 specifies. Unequip/swap removes outgoing effects, retaining **per-relic-ID run cooldowns**; re-equip or changing ordinary/combo mode cannot reset. Disconnect removes effects and keeps timers in run record; simulation time advances them while away. Clear/victory removes offensive relic effects. Seal/Wax shields otherwise expire normally.
8. Only living humans activate relics. Cleared-room trickle cannot activate relics/combos or contribute streak/deed records. Props are not enemies. Returning to a cleared room grants no activation.
9. Halo event means **world pickup**, not each credited wallet: Quill belongs to actual human collector; bot collection never triggers human Quill. Mote event belongs to collector. Rite fallback wallet credits are not pickups. Catalog creates no currency/motes/refunds/extra stock/XP multipliers.
10. Single-target projectiles choose nearest target from origin with room line of sight, tie by ID, then travel toward the recorded position; never reacquire/home around corners. First living enemy intercepted takes the one-target hit even if different from selection. No target → no projectile. Wall/door/lifetime ends it. Sounds batch by effect kind, maximum one per kind per 0.25 s; weapon and boss-warning audio takes priority.

## 4. Mental walkthroughs and completed checks

### 4.1 Build and abuse walkthroughs (design review, not executed tests)

- **Fallen:** Balm/Feather fill two slots. A combat Bitter Water drop stays on the floor while inventory is full. Spending the Feather frees its slot for the Water; picking it up stores it, and pressing its key throws it deliberately. Taunted kills create Usher ash. Quill later rewards a pickup dive with escape; Expulsion instead rewards taunting a remote crowd and sacrifices the collector's local escape.
- **Heretic:** Thurible reads the base censer's kills. Vial keeps enemies in incense without stealing taunt/fire-rate jobs. Mercy Wax never adds to Shroud; its combo trades safety for a censed finisher. First Plea visibly sleeps with healing Q; a damaging Q Rite can awaken it.
- **Binder:** Link reads bound kills but launches only unbound neighbors, keeping the main pile intact. Comb/Wheel reads slowing the Binder already supplies. Harrow needs a lane and a still-active comb; unrelated relics can be more reliable. Repeated bind never multiplies ×2 more than once.
- **Betrayer:** Field of Blood supplies pooled kills for Tile; no relic adds fire rate. Mirror reflections remain item damage. Counterseal works with Balm/Feather; Appeal instead chooses area items and sacrifices immediate self-item reliability. Neither Field nor Judas Coin can trigger First Plea. Shadowstep and The Way Back are damaging E alternatives that **do** trigger Last Plea while preserving Field in Q; replacing their cut with a pure launch makes Last Plea dormant. Reversal is a different character's combination in this slice because Betrayer cannot create the required Q rune.
- **Four humans:** shared state setups reward shooting into teammates' effects, while XP remains shared. One halo pickup makes at most one Quill ring, not four. Mote collectors never distribute four shields. One world item is one use. Overlapping slows remain factor 0.5. Every player buys their own pedestal once.
- **Exhausted account:** one chest ID yields one card, zero yields Rite; four-Rite fallback retains Sundial timed-run qualification. Valuable full-inventory valley drops remain recoverable. First carried win cannot grant all deeds. Boss adds sustain control procs and at most eight ordinary ranged-kill motes per fight, never an endless resource/XP farm; trickle cannot farm deed streaks.
- **Proc loop:** Bitter Water kills pooled target → Tile binds once; later direct shot can read bound. Counterseal damage cannot start Tile/Seals/Comb or talent death procs. Wheel-driven tier crossing cannot activate Fragment. Confessional re-equip never resets cooldown.

### 4.2 Catalog accounting

- Eight items; weights **20+12+14+8+14+14+10+8=100**. One heal ≤40, one shield ≤50, one mobility effect, five placed/thrown, three extra-state items.
- Twelve relics: chest 4 Common+3 Rare+1 Legendary; deeds 2 Common+1 Rare+1 Legendary; total 6/4/2. Six disjoint pairs; every relic in one pair, every deed paired with chest, replacement b explicit.
- Nine relics read/apply states: Usher, Thurible, Link, Tile, both Seals, Comb, Wheel, Quill. Base class coverage taunted/censed/bound/pooled; two Q/E-kill relics with explicit dormant-kit behavior.
- No fire-rate/taunt/silence/censed application, ally heal/shield, shield stacking, currency creation or item-to-ability kill attribution. No bound above 1 s from this content, and no recursive procs.
- Six combos have a lost benefit and never add a third passive effect. Unrelated pairs remain useful. Caps bound proc entity count while preserving large class/consumable horde moments.
- Lore retains condemned heroes, Heaven's orderly certainty, appeal and danger to Earth. Flavor states origins without a punchline.

### 4.3 Fourth-pass edge walkthroughs (design review, not executed tests)

- **Shared pool cadence:** two humans land Bitter Water a quarter-second apart. The second landing cannot immediately damage a victim already pulsed by the first. The newest covering instance owns later permitted pulses; leaving/re-entering cannot reset that enemy's half-second budget. Four players cannot turn one poison effect into four simultaneous DPS sources.
- **Shield and control refusal:** at full 100 shield, a touched Seal stores or stays; it does not consume itself only to extend time. At 30 remaining shield, a legal manual use restores 100/8 s. Plume's first coverage of a bound enemy spends that instance's attempt; it cannot surprise-launch the same body when bind ends, and a second ring cannot relaunch a body already in its arc.
- **Geometry:** Bitter Water at the boss's lip covers adds on the floor and cannot pool/damage the elevated Gatekeeper. A launched survivor is still eligible as a Chorus center under its actual authoritative hit cylinder; the visual arc never moves the damage cylinder by itself. Watchers cannot be selected by autonomous relic aiming or manufactured into hit-count/kill procs; an incidental volumetric burst respects their half-area-damage rule.
- **Combo change and item timing:** a Wheel activation leaves a three-second timer; equipping Comb at the Advocate transforms it into Harrow with the same remaining timer. Removing Comb also removes an outgoing Harrow projectile, without refreshing Wheel. The first Bitter Water throw cannot feed Appeal before landing; a later Balm or Feather use may stamp the actual landed pool, while a freshly placed Vial/Ward works immediately. These are alternative item timings, not a hidden delayed activation.
- **Collection and recovery:** an exhausted account with four Rites sees no HP-priced bargain; the chest still settles its fallback once. Victory closes every unaccepted bargain, so a previously previewed offer cannot purchase a relic or Rite with an irrelevant post-win HP cost; already accepted pawns settle normally. A prior-winner who temporarily loses the connection retains the same admitted record and receives its eligible deed settlement on supported rejoin. Voluntary pre-win leave earns no deed. Carrying/idle friends are permitted by the trusted co-op policy; this spec does not pretend to authenticate personal effort.

### 4.4 Seventh-pass edge walkthroughs (design review, not executed tests)

- **A refused chest is finished:** a player sees two dormant but valid collection cards and chooses Pass. The chest entitlement closes with no ownership, Rite or halo payment; only those card reservations release, enabling a later bargain draw from the released pool. Esc, duplicate requests and reconnect cannot redraw the passed chest or restore its exit hold. Claiming a dormant card remains a different valid choice.
- **Vertical native attack:** a Saint's exactly downward censer shot has no horizontal aim component. Its first pulse positively damages five distinct enemies, so Comb records the accepted cast yaw as its planar heading. Turning before that pulse, later menu edits and reconnect cannot rotate it. A later slowed kill's Harrow follows that stored heading, perpendicular to the visible comb's long axis.
- **Native movement leg:** the Betrayer places a Way Back marker while facing north, then returns west toward it while looking south. Positive native cuts count once across that one accepted return leg; Comb uses its westward path and retained first-hit point. A steered Gatebreaker records the segment of its first positive hit, then reaches five victims after turning: the resulting comb uses the saved lane, not the later camera/segment. These records remain on their original cast roots, preserving all discharge flags and cooldowns.
- **Flight order differs from area order:** Bitter Water is thrown at tick 0 and lands at tick 30. A Ward placed at tick 15 was the newest real area until tick 30; afterward Water is newest. The throw's use at tick 0 could only stamp an already-landed area, and landing at tick 30 emits no second use. A later Balm use stamps Water if it meets Appeal's existing range/room conditions. Area synchronization restores the same activation times, rather than making an older Ward newer.
- **A finite collection remains finite:** the eight chest claims and four deeds do not imply ten or thirty compulsory runs. Several eligible deeds may settle together on one later victory; trusted friends may carry each other. After completion the useful replay decisions are effective Rite substitutions, alternative assignments, item stock, route, Bell risk and Sentence. Neither duplicate ownership nor hidden rarity damage is introduced to extend that count.
- **An actual retained source is temporary setup:** if authored cleanup permits an accepted native original-Q source to remain after a healing Q is assigned, its later positive Q kill can still trigger First Plea until that source expires. The offer distinguishes that pending source from the currently dormant assignment. Actual surviving taunt/incense tokens likewise support Usher/Thurible. Comb can read four retained distinct hits plus remaining native first-window budget capable of a fifth; consumed windows and later pulses cannot qualify. Removed, expired and secondary effects supply nothing, and this does not assume that any particular outgoing Shroud survives an edit.
