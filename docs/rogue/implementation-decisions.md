# Implementation decisions

2026-10-13 playtest follow-up (explicit user feedback): all ground enemies share dry navigation; lamps/chalices recharge in 15 s with authoritative deadlines; ordinary halo chunks become one-halo drops with unchanged room budgets; ranged mote chance is 35% within the existing caps. Hedge-end stairs and retained legal steering fix safe perches. Supplied prop/pickup/channel art and a Blender Dominion replace canvas fallbacks. Asset directories follow the original game layout; duplicate originals are recoverably archived. Protocol/rules 6, current full-pool hash ad5d1609. See [the follow-up report](r02-playtest-report.md) for evidence and remaining human acceptance.

The register of decisions made while implementing the rogue slice, each beside the spec clause it touches. The AI adds a row whenever implementation settles something the spec leaves open, or changes how a clause is carried out; a change to a rule itself also updates its owning clause in [game.md](game.md) or the catalog. **Your approval** names who approved it and when; `routine` marks an implementation choice the workflow lets the AI make on its own ([development-workflow.md](development-workflow.md), step 3).

Started in R0 (2026-10-12) on branch `rogue`.

R03 (2026-10-13; authorized by Tomas's “Implement R03”): schema 1 is the first real profile format, so schema 0 is refused instead of inventing a migration. Profiles use exclusive Web Locks and a mandatory durable stake before host placement; later accepted progress remains in memory on write failure, with export/retry. A retained profile-owned host record and sixteen-run terminal cache resolve reconnects without reconstructing a crashed host. XP uses exact generated wave weights, absolute monotonic checkpoints and saturating saves. Personal item prices and Chart provisionally scale by four with the R02 halo budgets; controlled collection verifies accounting, with human pickup/purchase balance still open. Accepted consumable actions receive unique run-scoped IDs; areas keep immutable flight/landing records and incarnation-scoped controls. Boss death secures one victory, and post-win inventory revisions retain that settlement. R03 implements the bounded Stair against the inherited boss fixture; R7 replaces the encounter and adds its own rewards, without duplicating settlement. Rite pages pay the documented six-halo fixture; R4/R6 rewards remain unimplemented. Protocol/rules 11/14. These routine implementation choices do not claim Gate B, asset or human play acceptance.

R2 predictive pressure follow-up (2026-10-13): Tomas accepted the latest flight feel and requested movement-leading slam aim plus slower backward running. Flying slams now predict one second of recent accepted ordinary motion, shorten unsafe leads, then lock the marker. Standing/stale/teleported/forced movement cannot supply an old or oversized lead. Ordinary backward speed is 80%, with proportional backward-diagonal slowdown, shared by client movement and host speed allowance. Ability/forced movement retains its speed. Protocol/rules 8/11; no floor repin. New feel acceptance is pending.

R2 Dominion pacing/leap follow-up (2026-10-13): Tomas replaces first-wave commanders with a 0/1/2/3/... wave-index ramp before party scaling, retaining authored minions and the two-alive cap. He requests a distant targeted leap, nonlethal enemy throws from both slams, and ordinary melee between casts. After his range correction the leap selects a random valid player 16–48 m away, locks the ground target, charges 0.5 s and flies for 1 s to the existing 1.5 s ring deadline, with an 8 s post-impact/cancel cooldown. Both slams still deal 80 to players but zero to enemies, with physical launch; melee is 12 damage/2 m using the Blessed cadence and no overlap with casts/recovery. Geometry/control cancellation and replicated immutable leap metadata are part of this change. Protocol/rules 8/10, full-pool hash 81713798. These are implemented tuning choices awaiting human play; R03 must count the revised per-wave commander group for XP.

R2 Dominion impact follow-up (2026-10-13): at Tomas's request, trigger/impact radii become 5/6 m and player damage becomes 80. Surviving nonimmune victims launch outward at 12 m/s for 0.5 s and upward at 9 m/s through ordinary collision/gravity; shields do not block the displacement. Tell/recovery/cooldown and enemy friendly damage stay unchanged. The host authorizes victim/direction through the existing acknowledged, revisioned correction; client movement receives a bounded speed allowance, not a speed-check bypass. Protocol/rules 7/9. These are initial tuning values pending human play, not Gate B acceptance.

R2 pressure follow-up (2026-10-13): Tomas found too little reason to enter crowds and too few masters accompanying the minions. Generated stamping now adds one Dominion outside each wave's authored TP mix, including the opening and crest; explicit elite-room additions remain extra. Native party HP scaling, first safe placement, the two-alive cap, wave completion and per-room ledger accounting apply. Room halo budgets rise fourfold; ranged weights become 3 rather than 10, and elites reserve at most one quarter of the budget. This is initial tuning, not a claim of accepted fight feel. Candles/chalices shrink to 1.1/1 m and candle player damage rises from the already implemented 15 to 40, retaining mitigation, shields, range, LOS and recharge. Tomas explicitly kept the chalice purely healing and requested proposals, not implementation, for Cherub/Chorister masters. Protocol 6/rules 8; geometry and seed selections stay fixed while wave data changes both pinned hashes. R03 must revisit shop prices and count the new commanders in XP entitlements.

R2 pickup follow-up (2026-10-13): Tomas requested more frequent enemy drops and quieter, nonflickering halos, with the existing cleanup committed together. Wave Blessed now roll one consumable at 2%, ranged enemies at 12%, Dominions at 50%; only accepted eligible damage deaths roll, and currency budgets, healing caps and pickup capacity remain unchanged. Currency uses soft-alpha normal blending without depth writes or additive glow; stable small offsets separate shared anchors, gentler bobbing replaces the large oscillation, and the last three seconds shrink instead of blink. Only motes retain a smaller, dimmer glow separated from their sprite plane. Protocol remains 6, rules advance to 7. Rates are the implementation's initial tuning under the requested fix; human feel acceptance remains open.

R2.5 (2026-10-13): players have accepted feet but no grounded bit, so floor contact uses the existing reported feet and 0.05 m tolerance; enemy contact additionally requires grounded state. Native mitigation/shield/immunity and world-drop entry points remain shared. A single reverse-ground cache projects fire targets and displaced avoiders to legal dry anchors with distance/row-major ties; four extra fields reuse the ordinary cadence and clamp. Escape takes precedence over cast holds while in fire, then holds resume on dry ground; root/stun retain authority and deadlines are unchanged. Finite ambient plays use normal admitted voices rather than the existing uncapped loop helper. Coded moving fire/gold lips remain explicit asset fallbacks. The scoped Gridiron dev wave includes Choristers for coverage; it does not replace authored full-pool waves. Routine choices under the prepared R2 brief; no performance threshold or benchmark scope changed.

R2.4 client (2026-10-13): keep reliable cue admission independent of complete-snapshot state, retain only bounded cast identities, resume tell playback at its elapsed buffer offset and stop its admitted voice on cancellation/omission. Sixteen short collision probes give bots same-room ring escape without a new map search. A labeled coded mace-bearing atlas is the prepared fallback while model/art approval remains absent. Load it only for authored elite waves. The scoped `dominion` lab uses authored Lampstands geometry and short explicit dev waves; normal pool adoption remains R2.6. Explicit dev menus and a one-shot held-ready hook exercise the real bounded PeerJS/FloorSync handshake without mutating host state. These are routine R2 implementation choices. Larger-session browser admission/disconnection failures remain unresolved and must be reported/retested; no timeout, upload or simulation threshold was relaxed, and the failed optional small-view experiment was reverted.

R2.2 (2026-10-13): `Floor.props` feeds identical `SimOptions.props` and client definitions through `propDefs`, instead of adding duplicate sockets to `RoomDef`. Canonical socket IDs/loot ordinal survive orientation and selection; map geometry and the accepted R1 pinned hash stay unchanged. Props are transparent to native ray penetration budgets, like existing breakable globes, but respect the accepted terrain/enemy stopping point. These are routine implementation choices under R2's authorized scope. B1 source images remain preserved; the runtime uses labeled coded sprites while green-background source preparation and human visual acceptance remain pending.

R2.3 (2026-10-13): recorded normalization has a maximum 4x boost (+12 dB), rejecting silent/nonfinite takes. Families keep their successfully decoded takes or their existing synth when none survives. Cosmetic Fisher-Yates bags advance only after voice admission; toggle-off and rejected playback preserve the next take. Existing 24 total / 4 per-sound voices, 30-ms gap, priorities, attenuation, pitch, limiter and mass-kill aggregation stay intact. This is a routine bounded-loader choice under the prepared brief; human ear acceptance and missing optional recordings remain separate.

R2.4 authority/wire (2026-10-13): sparse accepted Dominion records/deadlines live in a host controller alongside the existing enemy arrays. Evaluate the bounded caster list after ordinary enemy movement so world kills cannot invalidate that movement loop. Recheck caster life after a prior slam can trigger owned Shroud damage. Normal zero-elite stamped waves omit the optional `dominions` field, preserving the R1 geometry hash. Protocol 4/rules 3 reserve cast metadata in part0 and validate incarnation/windup against the assembled complete entity set. Routine component choices; client presentation, cue admission and bots remain required before the stage handoff.

R2.6 (2026-10-13): the full pool keeps the historical R1 pool/pin as regression fixtures and deliberately repins runtime content at seed317434499/hash43dfb2f9. One-second accepted warnings cover close/floor placements and anti-softlock relocation; relocation preserves enemy identity, HP, statuses and reward entitlement. Fallback searches reuse bounded static candidates and existing reachable fields, including pending first placements. These are routine choices within the prepared R2 stage.

R2.6 transport (2026-10-13): protocol5 losslessly transposes record columns before native deflate, with declared/output bounds, serial bounded work and whole-tick sender coalescing. This preserves the 10Hz cadence, 16,000-byte packet bound and 437KB/s limit at the enlarged crowd payload. Unsupported compression APIs retain raw fallback, whose peak bandwidth has not been established. Optional `playwright.hardware.config.ts` changes only Chromium's renderer to Windows D3D11; tests, deadlines and limits remain inherited. Same-check hardware successes do not erase recorded SwiftShader failures or establish their cause.
