## Ownership and effective effects

The core reviewer integrated the substitution transaction. Learned-only allocation and its gates remain valid on their own. Distinct held learned/Rite IDs count once for combined lower-tier gates, including temporarily dormant ownership; no node counts itself or a higher dependent node. Effective resolution dispatches at most one augment per implicit named-slot group. Suppressed nodes emit no future effects, source metadata or duplicate passive callbacks. Removing their outgoing transient effects cannot reset stored cooldowns/meters. The effective source calculation follows that suppression before deciding whether the candidate is useful.

The catalog prose that says two nodes conflict because they share LMB/RMB/Q/E documents an implicit group. It is not an additional authored `Excludes` rule. A genuine explicit exclusion is never bypassed. A second Rite in the same group is never accepted even if the first is temporarily dormant through assignment. Capstone ownership remains singular. Respec and run-end restoration cannot sell/refund the temporary overlay or learn an already-held Rite ID.

Free offers can be explicitly passed with no node/halos; Esc only keeps them pending. Sampled Rite card identities are fixed. Changing party life state, assignment, relics or draft talents may disable/re-enable those cards and update their opportunity-cost preview, but cannot redraw them or manufacture a fallback. This closes the free-respec reroll path reported to the coordinator. A genuinely empty initial eligible pool and the four-Rite cap retain their existing once-only fallback. The bargain's decline remains terminal, with no health payment.

### Initial-choice existence, without promising four Rites

At the start of a living solo Pearly run, before any Rite is held, every legal learned allocation has at least one useful effect-changing candidate. This includes any legal nine-point allocation. The reasoning uses this catalog's absence of additional explicit exclusions, permanent LMB/RMB availability and the native Pearly ground swarm; it does not apply to future catalogs with new exclusions or an enemy-free floor.

- **Fallen:** Fire and Brimstone and Heave are both tier-1 LMB augments. A learned allocation cannot own both, so at least one remains available, either ordinarily or as a substitution for a learned LMB augment. Both act on the native ground swarm without an external source.
- **Betrayer:** Glancing Silver, Recoil and The Nail are tier-1 RMB augments. At most one is learned, so at least two remain eligible; walls, native crowd lanes and normal player movement make their effects producible.
- **Binder:** if Whirl is unlearned, it is a tier-1 RMB candidate. If learned, either Barbed Rounds/Frost is an unlearned tier-1 candidate, or both C tier-1 nodes are learned and license the unlearned Whip at tier 2. Whip cannot already be learned beside Whirl, and can substitute for it.
- **Heretic:** if Ploughshares is unlearned, it is a tier-1 LMB candidate. If learned and Communion is assigned, either Exorcism is unlearned (tier 1), or Exorcism is learned and Ploughshares's held B tier-1 node licenses unlearned Wall of Fire (tier 2). If Witnesses is assigned instead, it supplies own incense: either Walk in the Smoke is unlearned (tier 1), or its learned C tier-1 ownership licenses Altar/Scapegoat (tier 2), which cannot be learned beside Ploughshares. Thus one of these LMB substitutions is available.

This establishes a nonempty initial pool and prevents the old all-halos veteran failure. It does not guarantee a specific two-card offer, four useful accepted Rites, parity between effects, or a player preferring any offered substitution to the current kit. Pass remains an informed refusal with no replacement reward.

### One source replacement through respec and run end

Take a Saint with learned C `1a 1b 2a 3a 3b cap`, B `1b`, A `1a 2b` (nine), assigning Communion/Shroud; the learned Witnesses alternative is initially unassigned. Altar is the only ordinary incense shape and Kindling supplies fire. A Ploughshares Rite substitutes for Altar: remove only Altar's outgoing contribution, preserve unrelated Kindling contributions and meters/cooldowns, recompute LMB as direct hit plus fire. Without own incense, Walk in the Smoke, Common Cause and Pillar are now dormant; the preview names that loss. Owned C IDs still meet C's gates and the single capstone remains owned. Equipped Thurible loses its setup. A previously pending fixed Scapegoat card becomes disabled by the held LMB Rite, rather than changing identity or paying halos.

At the Confessional the player may replace learned Altar with learned Scapegoat in one valid whole draft and assign the already-owned Witnesses to Q. C retains three tier-1/2 and five lower nodes; A still has Cunning Folk/Lazarus and its own valid lower gate; total learned spend stays nine. Carry the settled existing Q deadline. Witnesses's native moving incense restores the smoke/Pillar readers and Thurible's setup, while Kindling supplies the pool for Common Cause. Ploughshares now suppresses learned Scapegoat in the same LMB group, so respec cannot spawn three extra native clouds while the overlay is active. No point refund or second Rite is created. At actual run end remove Rites/temporary effects and the substitution overlay; the resulting persistent allocation and Witnesses assignment stay valid and Scapegoat becomes the learned effective LMB shape. A legal edit underneath the overlay cannot revive outgoing areas or change fixed offer identities.

## Event and reward pass after the repair

- Weight of Sin replacing Blasphemer's Ring makes Q nondamaging; First Plea and Reversal previews must become dormant unless a different actual Q source exists. No expired fire contribution inherits a new Q lifetime. Re-enabling an assignment retains its timer.
- Saint fire replacing attached incense can make smoke movement/Pillar/Common Cause dormant. Assigned Witnesses legitimately preserves incense in the Martyr example. Dormant owned effects neither dispatch nor preserve stale source metadata.
- Four humans using staggered Bitter Water still share one enemy damage cadence; floor tools use actual feet/support surfaces and never reach an elevated boss through the dais. A vertical throw and an ordinary throw settle one reserved landing entitlement, not a later second item event.
- Plume's direct landing bundle resolves effective owned applicable control readers before secondary damage, once per owner/node/source; dormant ownership contributes no callback. Relic/combo launches do not manufacture that bundle. Native delayed Q/E keeps cast-slot provenance; secondary sparks, shards, fire and reflection do not become new primary kills.
- Ordinary/combo swaps share each relic ID's timer. Seal/Wax remains one shield channel, never a new Shroud burst. Boss adds keep their one shared ordinary-mote cap and do not feed bonus Bread/secondary harvest. Collection/deed settlement stays once-only for admitted records; voluntary departure cannot recreate eligibility.
- Fixed offers remain fixed when a required ally dies or is revived. The disabled source card can become legal again without rerolling. Restoring a compatible kit is an optional way to use the existing offer; passing it is an explicit lost reward, not six guaranteed halos.
- Inventory capacity refuses before charge/currency consumption; accepted world-use/refresh emits one item event, storage emits none. A thrown item's one-second lifetime does not reset another item's global use cooldown or a Counterseal/Appeal timer.

## Research checked during this pass

McMillen's own 2012 postmortem ties Isaac's run variation to randomized resources and physical character growth; it also records rare combination failures and runtime constraints. The design inference here is to keep actual run-changing choices available to collected veterans while bounding their interaction rules. Random rooms and a completed persistent tree cannot substitute for those choices. This is an adaptation of the original design, not a claim that one Pearly dungeon offers Isaac's expanding authored chapters. [The Binding of Isaac postmortem](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/postmortem-mcmillen-and-himsl-s-i-the-binding-of-isaac-i-).

Blizzard's talent-design explanation prioritizes agency, clear dependencies, class foundations and paths that can produce functional builds. The inference is to count persistent and effective ownership explicitly and show the tool surrendered by a temporary alternative; a hidden exclusion dead end undermines the stated agency goal. These are 2022 design principles, not current WoW numerical rules. [Dragonflight talent preview](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/23797209/world-of-warcraft-dragonflight-talent-preview).

2K's 2020 skill-tree announcement describes mixing new passives with existing abilities and replacing a ranged setup with a closer control playstyle. The inference is to judge a new run option by how aim, route, position or timing changes, rather than only its damage ceiling. Our narrow substitution keeps four combat inputs and explicit opportunity costs. [Borderlands 3 skill-tree announcement](https://newsroom.2k.com/news/even-more-borderlandsr-3-skill-trees-revealed-ahead-of-designers-cut-launch-on-november-10).

## Limits that remain empirical

This pass repairs concrete rules rather than proving unlimited fun. The pool is finite; a few strong shapes could still dominate the 84-node system. Observe whether Heave/As We Fell, Whirl/Ring or coin lines become automatic best choices; whether losing the healer's escape/shield remains a felt cost; whether max-level players alter their route for a specific Rite source; and whether the four-Rite cap arrives too soon after collection completion. No tests, gameplay sessions or validation scripts were performed. No further definite catalog contradiction was found in the final reread, while the implementation, balance, frame cost, audio readability and repeat-run enjoyment still require real evidence.

## Final cross-review reconciliation: countdown choices

The independent core review found the countdown/valley transaction contradiction after this catalog pass. All four class catalogs and the item/relic catalog now mirror the authorized resolution: existing Rite/relic choices, pre-victory bargain acceptance and valid Advocate edits can commit during a valley or a countdown, subject to the same living-player, range and room checks. At the actual seal, overlays close and a queued later commit is refused without applying/spending anything or discarding its retained offer. Fixed identities, ownership gates, substitute-effect preview and the no-post-victory bargain rule still apply.

This makes Appendix A.3's learning-during-countdown scene executable without changing the valley definition. A placed/thrown world drop touched during countdown still takes its original item-routing path rather than acquiring valley storage behavior; passive regeneration and Pilgrim's Return keep their existing narrower phase requirements. No tests, builds, scripts or game code were used for this reconciliation.
