# Milestone 9 Specification: Classes

M8 made the game readable and punchy. The first playtest and the design work since ([classes.md](classes.md)) showed that the classes themselves need work: the Betrayer is weak against the swarm, Kiss of Betrayal is useless outside the boss fight, and every class has only one way to shoot. This milestone reworks the four classes. It adds no new dungeons, enemies or progression.

All numbers are **initial tuning values**, as in the MVP.

## 1. Goals and design rules

**Goals:**
1. The Betrayer feels strong against the swarm, not only against the boss.
2. Every class has two attacks, and both are worth using.
3. The Fallen's leap and the Heretic's heal are visibly big moments.

**Design rules** (from `classes.md`):
- **Two attacks per class.** Left mouse fires the primary, right mouse the secondary. Each is clearly the better one in some situation, so neither is worth holding all the time.
- **Every ability rewards timing.** No ability is worth pressing the moment it's ready.
- **One effect per ability, with no hidden riders.** A second effect is allowed only if it's visible on its own.
- M8's rules still hold: a clean screen, visual feedback rather than text, hellfire colors for players' effects (heals stay green, shields blue).

## 2. Controls and attacks

### 2.1 Controls

| Input | Action |
|---|---|
| Left mouse (hold) | Primary attack |
| Right mouse (hold) | Secondary attack |

- **One attack at a time.** While both buttons are held, the one pressed **last** is used. Releasing it switches to the other if that's still held.
- The client resolves this and sends one value, `fire` (§10): none, primary or secondary.
- The canvas suppresses the browser's context menu (`contextmenu` is prevented), in game and in the dev modes.
- Both buttons are released and ignored in the same cases as fire today (focus lost, Pause, chat line open, dead).

### 2.2 The shared fire timer

Each player has **one fire timer** for both attacks, replacing the per-weapon timer of MVP §6. Each tick, in this order:
1. If an attack is held, it can fire (§2.4, §2.5), and the timer is 0 or less: the attack fires once and **its own** time between shots is added to the timer.
2. The timer decreases by 1/30 s. While no attack is held, it doesn't go below 0.

So firing a slow attack delays the next shot of either attack. A Silver Bullet costs 1.5 s of revolver fire, which makes the choice between the two a real one. The client runs the same timer locally for its cosmetic shot feedback, as now.

### 2.3 Attacks

| Class | Attack | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Fallen | Primary: Brimstone Shotgun | As now: 8 hitscan pellets × 12 dmg, spread ±8° yaw and ±4° pitch, range 20 m, 0.8 s. Adds the knockback of §3.1. |
| Fallen | Secondary: Brimstone Slug | Hitscan, 60 dmg to the first enemy hit, no spread, range 50 m, 1.0 s. |
| Heretic | Primary: Censer Launcher | As now. |
| Heretic | Secondary: Sacrament | Heals the ally target 15 HP, 0.5 s (§2.5). |
| Binder | Primary: Chain Gun | As now. |
| Binder | Secondary: Scourge | Melee sweep, 25 dmg, 0.8 s (§2.6). |
| Betrayer | Primary: Silver Revolver | Hitscan, 30 dmg to the first enemy hit (no pierce), no spread, range 60 m, 0.15 s. |
| Betrayer | Secondary: Silver Bullet | Hitscan, 240 dmg carried through the line (§2.4), no spread, range 60 m, 1.5 s. |

**Reviving** (M8 §4.2): every hitscan attack revives souls as now, with `interval ÷ 3` per hit from its own interval. Censers revive as now. The Scourge and Sacrament don't revive.

### 2.4 Silver Bullet: damage carried through the line

- The ray finds every enemy along it within 60 m, nearest first, up to the terrain.
- The bullet starts with `remaining = 240`. For each enemy in order:
  - Let *m* be the product of the enemy-side multipliers of the damage pipeline (§3.6: Bound and Field of Blood).
  - If `remaining × m` is at least the enemy's HP, the enemy takes damage equal to its HP and dies, and `remaining` drops by `HP ÷ m`.
  - Otherwise the enemy takes `remaining × m` and the bullet stops.
- **The Gatekeeper** takes whatever is left, so it stops the bullet.
- **Where the ray stops** (for souls, M8 §4.2, and the tracer): at the enemy that stopped the bullet; otherwise at the terrain or 60 m.
- **Examples:** 12 Blessed (20 HP each) in a line; 24 bound Blessed; a Chorister (60 HP) and then 9 Blessed.

### 2.5 Sacrament

- **Uses the Heretic's ally target** (M8 §3.5), the same one as Martyr's Shroud, with the same range: 40 m.
- The client sends the ally target in every input, as now. The host uses the latest input's `allyTargetId`. The ally must be alive and within 40 m; the host doesn't recheck line of sight.
- **With no valid ally target, Sacrament doesn't fire**: nothing happens and the fire timer isn't touched. It never heals the Heretic.
- Each firing heals the ally 15 HP, capped at max HP. It fires even at full HP, so the beam (§5.1) stays steady.
- **The `beam` field** (§10) of the Heretic's player record is the healed ally's ID from a firing until 0.6 s pass without one, then 255.

### 2.6 Scourge

- Hits the **6 nearest** living enemies within 3 m of the Binder's body center (distance to the enemy's cylinder, MVP §5.3) whose horizontal direction from the Binder is within **60°** of the horizontal aim direction (a 120° arc).
- Each takes 25 dmg and, if it survives, is slowed for 1 s, like a Chain Gun hit.
- No line of sight is needed, like other area effects.
- It fires whether it hits anything or not.

### 2.7 Speeds

| Class | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| The Fallen | 7 m/s | 5.5 m/s |
| The Binder | 8 m/s | 6 m/s |

The host's speed check (MVP §9.3) uses the new class speeds. This replaces the decision about outrunning the Blessed "comfortably" (`docs/decisions.md`, §7.1).

## 3. Abilities

### 3.1 Shotgun knockback

- After all 8 pellets of a shot are resolved, each enemy that a pellet hit, that is still alive, and whose cylinder is within **6 m** of the Fallen's body center is **knocked back 2 m** horizontally, directly away from the Fallen, over **0.2 s**.
- It's knocked back once per shot, however many pellets hit it.
- **Not knocked back:** rooted enemies, including during a pull, so the Binder's piles hold; and the Gatekeeper (immune).
- Knockback works as in MVP §5.6 (normal collision, no steering, can fall off ledges). A new knockback replaces one in progress.

### 3.2 Falling Star

- **The landing** is unchanged: 40 dmg within 5 m, then the survivors are knocked back 4 m away from the landing point. The knockback now takes **0.4 s** instead of 0.2 s.
- **New event `starLanded`** (§10), sent on the landing tick:
  - `launched`: the slots of the knocked-back survivors;
  - `killed`: the slots of the enemies the landing killed.
- **On every client**, after the render delay, like other events:
  - **Launched enemies** fly an arc: their billboard is lifted by `4 × 1.0 m × u × (1 − u)`, where *u* goes from 0 to 1 over the 0.4 s. The peak is 1 m, at the middle. Their ground position comes from the snapshots as usual.
  - **Killed enemies** are thrown too: instead of their death animation, the corpse moves 2 m away from the landing point while rising to 1 m over 0.2 s (the first half of the arc), then bursts into its feathers and sparks at the top.
- The arc is only drawn. In the simulation enemies move along the ground as in any knockback.

### 3.3 Martyr's Shroud burst

- **The host remembers who cast each shield.**
- When **damage** takes a shield to 0, it bursts: 50 dmg to every enemy within 5 m of the shielded player's body center, credited to the caster.
- **No burst** when the shield expires, when a new shield replaces it, or when its player dies or leaves while it's up.
- **New event `shroudBurst`** (§10) for the effect and sound.

### 3.4 Field of Blood (Betrayer, Q)

Replaces Kiss of Betrayal. Cooldown **30 s**.

- **Where:** a ray from the eye along the aim, **ignoring enemies**, up to 30 m. It stops at the first wall, closed door or terrain, or at 30 m.
  - The **field's center** is the floor point under where it stopped (a cell's floor height).
  - If it stopped in a wall or door, the center is in the last open cell before it.
- **It always has a place**, so it always goes on cooldown.
- **Effect:** for **8 s**, every living player within **6 m** of the center (distance to the player's cylinder, MVP §5.3) is **in the field**.
  - Damage credited to a player in the field is doubled (§3.6).
  - Being in the field is checked at each hit, from the credited player's latest accepted position. A censer that explodes after its Heretic walked out of the field isn't doubled.
- **One field exists at a time.** The cooldown is longer than the duration, so this only matters for a Betrayer who leaves and rejoins. A new field replaces the old one.
- **Event:** `abilityUsed` with `slot: 'Q'`, at the field's center.

### 3.5 Kiss of Betrayal is removed

Remove it everywhere:
- the ability and its constants;
- the marked status and its damage step;
- the snapshot flag bit 1, which stays unused (§10);
- the mark sprite in the world atlas and its red glow;
- the mark beam;
- the *Kiss* sound;
- its texts and hints;
- the debug and test references.

`icon-kiss.png` and `mark.png` stay in `assets/art-src/` but are no longer built into the game.

### 3.6 Damage pipeline

Replaces MVP §5.5 as amended by M8 §8. Every hit is resolved in this order:

1. **Bound:** if the target enemy is rooted (pull included), damage ×2.
2. **Field of Blood:** if the player credited with the hit is in the field (§3.4), damage ×2.
3. **Brimstone Hide:** if the target is the Fallen, damage ×0.6.
4. **Invulnerability:** if the target is invulnerable, damage is 0.
5. **Shield:** the shield absorbs damage first; the remainder goes to HP. A shield taken to 0 bursts (§3.3).
6. **Death:** at HP 0 or below, the target dies. The credited player gets the kill.

- Bound and Field multiply (×4).
- Damage-based thresholds (the Judgment interrupt) count damage after step 2.

## 4. The Gatekeeper

Kiss tripled the party's damage on the boss for 6 s of every 10 s, about ×2.2 on average. Field of Blood gives about ×1.3 when the whole party stands in it, and the Betrayer's single-target damage rose from about 170 to 200 per second. In total, party damage on the boss falls to about 64% of M8's.

| Item | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| HP | 45 000 | **29 000** |
| Judgment interrupt threshold | 2 000 dmg | **1 300 dmg** |

Both are still multiplied by the party-size scaling (M8 §6.3). Everything else is unchanged.

## 8. Bot

Changes to the bot (MVP §2.5, M8 §6.4). Each frame, after choosing its aim:
- **Fallen:** holds the secondary when its aim enemy is farther than 20 m from its eye, otherwise the primary.
- **Heretic:** when no living enemy is within 6 m and a living teammate within 40 m with line of sight has less than 60% HP, it aims at that teammate's body center (the lowest HP fraction first) and holds the secondary. Reviving (M8 §6.4) comes first.
- **Binder:** holds the secondary when at least 3 living enemies are within 3 m and within 60° of its aim, otherwise the primary.
- **Betrayer:** holds the secondary when at least 4 living enemies' cylinders lie along its aim ray within 60 m, checked at most 4 times per second, otherwise the primary.
- Q and E: as now, whenever ready.

## 10. Protocol changes

Changes to MVP §9 and M8 §10. `version` still rejects mismatched builds.

**Input:** byte 24, `fireHeld`, becomes **`fire`**: 0 none, 1 primary, 2 secondary. The size stays 30 bytes.

**Events**

| Change | Type | Fields |
|---|---|---|
| changed | `abilityUsed` | the Betrayer's Q is at the field's center (Kiss is gone) |
| new | `starLanded` | `playerId`, `x`, `y`, `z`, `launched: number[]`, `killed: number[]` (enemy slots) |
| new | `shroudBurst` | `playerId` (the shielded player), `x`, `y`, `z` |
| new | `silverBullet` | `playerId`, `x`, `y`, `z` (the eye), `ex`, `ey`, `ez` (where the ray stopped) |

**Snapshots**
- **Players:** two new fields after `shots`:
  - `shots2` u8: a wrapping counter of secondary attacks fired;
  - `beam` u8: the ally being healed by Sacrament (§2.5), 255 for none.
  - `shots` now counts primary attacks only.
  - A record grows to **32 bytes**.
- **Enemy flags:** bit 1 (`marked`) is unused.
- **Largest snapshot:** 15 763 bytes, still under the 16 000-byte split limit.

**Debug object:** each player gets `secondaryShots`: the host's count of secondary attacks fired, not wrapped.
