# Revision 6: room and Gatekeeper contract review

Reviewed 2026-10-11. Scope: `docs/rogue/rooms.md`, `docs/rogue/bosses.md`, the core contracts they depend on, `docs/lore.md`, all four recorded playtests and the horde-fun report. The coordinator attempted the requested `origin/master` comparison; that ref is absent and the rogue specification is untracked, so no exact master-relative review is available. Existing revision-5 notes and geometry numbers were treated as history, not proof of this revision.

No game code, UI, test files or executable validators were created, changed or run. The scenario review below is reasoning about authored contracts. Pacing, expanded geometry, deterministic output and frame budgets remain implementation/playtest acceptance work.

## Findings fixed in the owned catalogs

1. **A rotated Gate could be one cell inside its wall.** Existing `GateDef` coordinates are continuous wall-plane/row-line positions, while the floor's D4 formula transforms integer cells. Applying `W−1−x` to plane x=0 would produce x=67 rather than the east face x=68; center line 34 would become 33. The catalogs now transform continuous endpoints/center with `W−x` and `H−y`, retain integer transforms for cells, use a half-open transverse extent and sample foot height from the adjacent interior floor. Rendering, terrain skip and decorative hitboxes must share that transform.
2. **World deaths had rewards but no implementable provenance.** Refiner's Fire and the Dominion's friendly slam now use `owner=null`, `origin=world`, `inputSlot=null`, `depth=1`. The victim's existing XP/type-specific drop entitlement and encounter quota settle once. Actual recent player damage/owned-state applications retain the ordinary 1.5-second assist; no killer, button kill, damage/kill proc or derived streak-tier proc is invented. A later hazard tick cannot repay the same dead incarnation.
3. **Spawn preference could not express a special-only marker.** Added the coordinated `spawnTags` schema, allowing Chorister-only, Dominion-only or joint restrictions on existing markers. Type filtering precedes affinity/proximity. Unrestricted markers alone satisfy 8/12/24 minima and crest capacity; special-only markers cannot be borrowed by fodder, flyers, a trickle or a Bell mix. Current authored points remain unrestricted, preserving the existing pool. A future marked extension has a precise format and rejection rules.
4. **Fire avoidance claimed a second field without its cost/lifetime.** The catalogs now require at most four additional room-clamped dry ground fields, one per living-player target slot, only while an avoider and active authored hazard coexist. Existing rebuild cadence, a dry chase anchor and cached ground-to-dry escape routing replace per-enemy/full-map searches. An avoider displaced into fire may cross fire to leave it; a player standing on fire never becomes an unreachable chase target merely because the avoider declines entry.
5. **The whole lip was credited with ideal shotgun damage.** The boss contract now states its inherited radius-2/height-6 cylinder and distinguishes central eye LOS from a ray to its exposed near upper surface. A concrete east-lip geometric example shows how a legal shot and blocked Judgment LOS can coexist. Terrain/per-pellet range checks still apply; 178 DPS is an ideal ceiling, not a promise for the north/west lip or every aim. The existing 100-DPS Fallen timing input remains an estimate.
6. **The three-minute boss target could be read as applying to every class subset or transformed build.** The reference is now explicitly one of each base class. Smaller class subsets, assisted parties and replacement-ability parties retain a legal ordinary-aim/movement solution and no enrage, time limit, forced damage interrupt or mandatory respec. The revised Way Back in E retains Field in Q; Judas Coin deliberately trades Field. A missing Field changes output and positioning rather than boss completion legality. The lobby still permits each class once.
7. **A final lethal shield-breaking hit could race its own boss-winning consequence.** The integrated contract resolves invulnerability before shield depletion, then the accepted hit's actual shield/HP/life state, then a previously cast native break and any part tether synchronously before all-dead. A lethal Shroud hit can therefore produce a legitimate final victory that revives the new soul; a rejected/invulnerable hit cannot fabricate a Shroud burst. Secondary damage cannot recurse, and each shield identity emits once.
8. **A one-metre dais was described as immunity to Blessed melee.** The inherited attack reaches 1.2 m horizontally across a 1 m feet-height difference. Hall of Dominions now describes route advantage rather than an invulnerable perch; the general geometry contract explains that a player must move inward or keep moving after hopping the ledge. Dominion's larger ring also remains a threat.
9. **Final-eye completion and recovery cleanup had ambiguous settlement order.** A Watcher death updates its remaining count/completion before its current tether, so a last eye can legally open the gate and deliver the final body hit. Previously deferred damage never finishes automatically. Ordinary anti-softlock removal settles unused room XP only, rather than the scenario's overbroad claim of no reward; no kill, proc, streak or drop is manufactured.
10. **A shot-down globe's blast had credit without a proc boundary.** Its inherited 40 damage in a 3 m 3D/LOS volume now carries the shooting root, shooter owner, globe origin, null input and secondary depth. It retains legitimate ordinary kill/drop entitlement without manufacturing weapon/Q/E, hit-count or damage/kill/streak-tier discharges. Native hostile shatters and secondary direct reflections remain separate behaviors; incidental part damage still takes its modifier and can produce its authored once-only tether.

## Research and the fun criterion

Valve's original [Replayable Cooperative Game Design talk](https://cdn.fastly.steamstatic.com/apps/valve/2009/GDC2009_ReplayableCooperativeGameDesign_Left4Dead.pdf), pages 19–21 and 28–38, ties replayability to enemies that force a change in tactic, readable anticipation, constrained unpredictability and alternating pressure/relief. The design inference here is to preserve distinct answers: bait the Dominion rather than always kill it immediately; prioritize exposed ranged threats; choose a dry bridge or lure through fire; answer the boss's ground ring, visible Seal and elevated eyes with different verbs. This inference does not establish that these authored numbers are enjoyable in an implemented build.

The fixed boss schedule is intentionally more learnable than the procedural floor. Route, template, build, party composition and optional risk change the run around that learnable encounter; randomizing the required dodge timing or adding invisible enemy strength would reduce informed choice. Twenty combat templates are a bounded slice, so neither this review nor the catalogs claim unlimited authored variety or proven ultimate replayability.
