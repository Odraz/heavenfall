# V2 Architecture

**Status: planned** (drafted 2026-10-25). Dependencies: V1 accepted. Rules and budgets: [spec.md](spec.md). Tomas's side: [guide.md](guide.md#v2).

## Playable outcome

The flat painted walls become modeled architecture like the concept's: walls of recessed pointed bays between clustered columns with carved gold capitals, tracery windows, molded bases and cornices; free-standing pillars as clustered columns; molded doorways; open edges as modeled arcades with balustrades; vaulted ceilings over corridors; here and there an arch closed by a locked wrought-iron gate with an aisle glimpsed beyond it, as on the left of the concept (D2: nothing new to enter); worn marble floors with gold inlays and borders. Every room template, corridor and special room uses the kit.

No wall reads as a flat boundary any more, yet no room gains a branch or an opening: the floor plan, heights and every line of fire are exactly as before. Before/after: the `arena` view matches the concept's architecture.

Out of scope: characters (V3), lava and effects (V4), props (V5). The HUD stays (D7).

## Frame-time share

Architecture: High ≤ +15%, Medium ≤ +7% of the baseline; Low must stay at or below the baseline using LOD1 and V1's culling.

## The kit

Pieces fit the level's grid: cells are 1 m, textures tile every 4 m (`TILE`), wall segments and arcade bays are 4 m (`BAY_CELLS`). Wall faces rise 8–11 m above their floor (M10 §3.2); zone tops are 5.3 m above the highest floor of the zone (`HEADROOM`).

| Piece | Where | Geometry rule (spec §2) |
|---|---|---|
| **Wall bay** (4 m): a recessed pointed arch on two engaged clustered half-columns with capitals, a tracery window or blind tracery in the recess, base molding, a frieze band, stacked to the wall's height | every *Wall* face run, replacing the window and pilaster segments of `looks.ts` | columns and moldings within 0.12 m in front of the face; the arch's recess up to 0.9 m behind it (inside the wall cell); never within 1 m of a convex corner |
| **Corner and end pieces** | inner and outer corners, steps between runs of different heights | as the wall bay; an outer corner gets a plain chamfered pier |
| **Cornice** | top of every wall run | at or above the zone top (as today's crown) |
| **Clustered pillar** | every *Pillar* group (M10 §3.2) | strictly inside the group's footprint: nothing in front of a pillar face (M10's relief rule excludes pillars); the capital fits the footprint and the shaft is recessed under it |
| **Doorway** | every door | jambs as recess; the molded arch and tympanum above `HEADROOM` (M10 §6.2) |
| **Open-edge arcade** | every open edge (M10 §3.3), replacing the painted cut-out bays | piers and arches above the parapet; balustrade modeled, same `PARAPET` height as the simulation |
| **Gated bay** (D2): the wall bay's arch closed by a locked wrought-iron gate (from `kit-gate`), with a glimpse behind | a few bays per room, chosen from the seed, never two side by side; the aisle version only where at least 3 m of void or wall-of-no-other-zone lies behind the run, otherwise a shallow gated recess | spec §3: gate in the face's plane within `RELIEF`, alpha-tested; a shallow recess inside the wall cell, or an aisle entirely over void or wall cells of no other zone with a closed back wall; impacts drawn on the gate's plane |
| **Vault** (D3) | over corridors and gated aisles | springs at or above the zone top; ribs and a keystone boss |
| **Floor trims** | along wall feet, around medallions | flat, in the floor mesh; no height |

Two styles of the same kit by room purpose: **cloister** (lighter, more windows: combat rooms) and **nave** (taller, darker, more carving: the Gate arena and special rooms). Variants come from data (which window, which capital), not new models. Along each run the bay varies (tracery window, blind arcade, empty niche, gated bay) by a seeded pattern, so no wall reads as one panel repeated.

## Ordered stages

### 1. Kit pipeline

- `scripts/blender/kit/`: a shared module with the grid, the bake (high poly → normal, occlusion with crease/cavity ink lines into one RGB map), LOD0/LOD1 export and naming; one script per piece family. Headless, deterministic, rerunnable (`blender -b --factory-startup -P scripts/blender/kit/<family>.py -- export assets/kit/`).
- `@gltf-transform/cli` for meshopt compression; KTX2 for the baked maps (V1's tools).
- Runtime: `src/render/kit.ts` loads the GLB set once, and the level build merges placed pieces into the zone chunks of V1 stage 3 (position, normal, tangent, two UV sets, layer index), one merged mesh per chunk and material family.
- First piece: a plain test wall bay, placed along one wall run of the pilot room behind `?kit=1`.

Tests: placement determinism (identical buffers for the same seed); merged chunk counts; the GLB set loads with the fallback textures.

### 2. Kit material

- One shader for kit pieces: surface layer from the texture array (2 048 px on Medium/High), tangent-space normal map, the piece's baked occlusion and ink, the level's baked light (sun visibility per vertex from the wall faces' shadow lines in `lightmap.ts`; floor-adjacent pieces sample the lightmap), one directional sun term N·L with the normal map so carving reads, the M10 sky fog, and the V4 dynamic light hook (unused until V4).
- Surfaces from Tomas's textures (guide step 2.1): pilot `tex-ashlar` and `tex-marble-floor` first.
- Inverted-hull outlines for LOD0 columns, ribs and arch edges in the ink color.

Tests: shader compiles in warm-up (`compileAsync`); the darkest-surface rule; the calibration views.

### 3. The pilot room

- Wall bays, corners, ends and cornice in the `arena` room from the modeled references (`kit-wall-bay`, `kit-capital` via Meshy, guide 2.2–2.3).
- Before/after screenshots beside `look-concept-arena.png`; Tomas reviews. **Iterate here until Tomas approves the pilot**; the rollout copies it.
- **Recommended benchmark point** (ask Tomas): pilot room, High and Low.

Tests: every placed piece passes the geometry rules (an automated check over all pieces of all templates, extended as pieces are added): nothing in front of a face beyond 0.12 m, nothing on a pillar face, nothing within 1 m of a convex corner, nothing below a zone top outside a wall cell except relief; M10 §8.1's line tests still pass.

### 4. Gated bays (D2: pilot first)

- The gate from `kit-gate` (guide 2.3): black wrought iron with gold, both leaves closed and locked, alpha-tested bars in the face's plane, LOD1 as a flat alpha-tested card.
- In the pilot room only: pick gated bays from the seed; where a run has ≥ 3 m of void or wall-of-no-other-zone behind it for the bay's width, build the glimpsed aisle (floor, back wall with a window, vault, baked light so it reads darker than the room); elsewhere a shallow gated recess with a dark back.
- Impacts: a hitscan or projectile stopping at a gated bay's face draws sparks on the gate's plane through the existing impact path, never a mark on stone behind it.
- **Stop for Tomas's verdict** with screenshots and a playable build: gated bays everywhere they fit, shallow gated recesses only, or none (then the stage's code is removed and the bays keep windows).

Tests: every aisle cell is void or wall of no other zone; no gate or aisle geometry in front of the face beyond `RELIEF`; a shot into a gated bay stops at the face (simulation unchanged) and its impact is drawn on the gate's plane; placement deterministic per seed and never two gated bays side by side.

### 5. Pillars, doorways, open edges

- Clustered pillars (from `kit-pillar`), doorways (`kit-doorway`), open-edge arcades (`kit-arcade`) replacing `arches.ts`'s painted bays; the cliffs below get a rock texture.
- The Gate (`gate.ts`) keeps its painted leaves; its posts and arch move to the kit's nave style.

Tests: geometry rules; the boss arena's pillars still hide players from Judgment exactly as before (M10 §8.1 cover test).

### 6. Corridors and vaults (only if D3 is yes)

- Vaults over corridors from `kit-vault`, springing at the zone top; included in the light bake (corridors under a vault are shaded except where a window or door lets the sun in).
- A shot that passes above a vault's springing line draws its impact on the vault (client-side clip), not in the sky beyond.

Tests: bake includes ceilings; impact clip.

### 7. Floors and trims

- New floor surfaces (`tex-marble-floor`, `tex-marble-border`, `tex-marble-medallion`) on the heightfield floors; borders along wall feet; medallions where `looks.ts` puts them today.
- Decals merged into the floor mesh: worn gold, cracks near pillars, a few per room from the seed. Floors stay mid-to-light and low in contrast.

### 8. Rollout

- All 20 combat templates, the four special templates, the fallback floor, corridors; cloister/nave styles assigned by room purpose; gated bays as Tomas decided in stage 4.
- Remove the painted wall path and its textures from the download once nothing uses them (Low uses LOD1 kit pieces).
- Budget test over every template at fixed seeds; LOD distances tuned.
- **Recommended benchmark point** (ask Tomas): full floor, all tiers.

## Play checklist

1. Pilot room on High, then Low: does it read as the concept's cloister? Do the columns and capitals look carved, not flat?
2. Look through a gated arch: does it read as a locked aisle beyond the room, not as a way you should be able to go? Shoot the gate: does the hit land on the gate?
3. Hide behind a pillar from a Chorister and from the Gatekeeper's Judgment: still exactly as safe as before?
4. Look for z-fighting, gaps at corners, floating pieces, light leaks in corridors.
5. In a swarm: is the busier architecture still behind the enemies, or does it compete with them?
