# Bosses

**Purpose:** the Pearly Gates boss (the Gatekeeper, reworked for the roguelite) and the mini-boss framework. Filled in by the boss designer; the rules come from [game.md](game.md) §11, which wins over this file. All numbers are initial values.

**Status:** complete encounter contract, revised 2026-10-11. The manual scenario audit is in §6. All timing estimates are design estimates, not measured playtests. Geometry is canonical in rooms.md; fight behavior is canonical here, subject to game.md.

---

## 1. Requirements (from game.md §11)

**The room**
- A 2×2 room, **68 × 68 m**, the boss room's only door leading from the Confessional. It seals like any fight (20 s countdown, 3 s when everyone's in, the multiplayer holds of game.md §4.3). Designed with [rooms.md](rooms.md) as the `boss` template: `entrySocket`, `points.boss`, the `gate` (`GateDef` with a `facing`, a 90° transform of the x-plane layout). The Gate stands on the **gate wall**, the side opposite the entry, which the generator guarantees is an outer face (its railings are see-through). The template **walls in 9 cells at each end of the gate side**, so the Gate stands on a straight 50-cell face with a post cell at each end (M10's geometry; on the full 68 m wall `gateLayout` would put the posts mid-wall).
- The base floor is at 2.0 m (height `8`) like every template; the dais and terraces are obstacles above it.
- Recognizable before entry: its door in the Confessional is the tall gold gate leaf with a trumpet.

**Structure**
- **2 phases**, threshold **50%**; at the threshold **1.5 s** invulnerable with a full-arena signal (a shockwave ring or feather burst). The second phase adds **one** new move or rule.
- **Fight length:** 3:00 ± 0:30 for the reference four-human party with one of each base class. Different class subsets, assisted parties and alternative abilities have different damage and sustain; their clears remain legal without a time limit or mandatory respec. Solo pacing depends on the selected class: approximately 1:15–2:30 Betrayer, 3:00–4:30 Fallen/Binder, and 4:30–5:30 Heretic with the inherited base weapons. These are tuning ranges, not a promise of equal damage roles. Every class can finish without a required relic or a required talent; the longest case is a playtest priority (§3.4.2).
- **HP by effective party size `P`** (humans + 0.5 × bots): the 4-player HP × interpolation through **(1, 0.30), (2, 0.55), (3, 0.78), (4, 1.0)**, fixed at the seal (3 humans + a bot: 0.89). The reasoning is game.md §11.2 (one player deals about a fifth of a 4-party's damage because of Field of Blood). **Damage to players never scales.** Tune the 4-player HP for **base kits with no Rites**; parties with Rites and talents will be faster, and the Edicts are their answer.
- **Anything counted in boss damage scales with the HP:** the Judgment interrupt is **4.5% of the boss's max HP at the seal** during the cast (today 1 300 of 29 000), not the old `bossMultiplier`.
- **Adds:** at most **250 × mult(P)** alive; they give no XP and no halos. Fewer or no adds in a damage window is encouraged.
- **Telegraphs:** wind-up 1.0 s single-target, 1.5 s area, 2–3 s arena-wide; "about to hit" = a ground decal filling gold, white flash at impact; "vulnerable now" = red; "can't be damaged" = pale grey; a wind-up sound per move.

**What the boss must test**
1. **Movement:** at least one move answered by jumping, dodging, stopping or hiding. Every class answers it with plain movement (a plain jump clears a ring); Falling Star and Shadowstep may answer it better.
2. **Aiming:** at least one phase or move with targets that aren't the body. Count **3 + humans + bots** (4 solo, 7 at four), low HP each; area damage deals half to them.
3. **Teamwork:** at least one mechanic where helping each other is best, stating its **solo rule** (one player can do it alone), **bot rule** (what a bot does) and **fail cost** (never a wipe: the boss heals 10% or an elite wave comes). Counts capture connected living players (e.g. plates needed = living players).
4. **One identity rule** in a sentence. The Gatekeeper's is **"Judgment hits everyone who can see it: hide."** Keep it.
5. **Class-neutral:** no class is required; Blasphemy may draw the boss, but the fight works without a Fallen.

**Edicts that touch the boss** (game.md §2.5): the Edict of the Trumpet makes the phase-2 move active from the start of the fight (design phase 2's move so it's fair on its own at 100% HP); the Edict of Swift Hymns makes its projectiles 30% faster.

**States on the boss** (game.md §4.11): the Gatekeeper can be `taunted` (Blasphemy may draw it), `silenced` (Discord interrupts Judgment), `censed` and `pooled`; never `bound`, `slowed` or `airborne`. Say the same for each aiming target (the generic "boss part" type): default `censed` and `pooled` only.

**Players at the seal:** the Sundial's Rites apply at the seal if it comes before 14:00 (game.md §6.8); a player who took the Advocate's bargain fights at 70% max HP (the Betrayer at 84). Judgment still kills at any max HP (hide); no other single hit may take more than 60% of the Betrayer's bargained 84 HP.

**On the boss's death (game.md §11.5–§11.6):** every enemy bursts without credit; every soul revives at 50%; the living heal 30% unless Penance; **from then on nobody takes damage and defeat is off**; connected eligible humans get 1 500 XP with the Sentence multiplier. Every admitted eligible unclosed human record, including a temporarily disconnected one, receives its victory/clear/unlock/pawn/deed settlement under the core's admission rules; being offline grants no missed XP or automatic choice. Progression-disabled developer runs change only their disposable in-memory records. The Stair of light rises at the room's center with each player's unbought Pardoner pedestals beside it. Both ordinary exit conditions (all living humans in the light for 3 s, or 30 s after the first enters) respect the pending-choice hold of §3.8; the hard limit is 120 s after boss death.

**Lines** ([lore.md](../lore.md)): before the fight, *"Your appeal is denied."*; at victory, *"The first trumpet is silent. Six to go."*

**Bots** play the base kit and follow the bot rules here; they must not be needed to win, and a solo player must be able to do every mechanic.

## 2. The Gatekeeper today

From the shipped game (MVP §7.4, M12 follow-up, codebase_audit.md §3.7): 29 000 HP at 4 players, on a 3 m dais it never leaves (speed 0); **Orb Volley** every 4 s (8 orbs, 15 dmg); **Judgment** every 25 s (a 3 s cast; hits everyone whose eye sees its eye for 100% of max HP; interrupted by Discord or 1 300 damage during the cast); **summons** every 30 s (150 Blessed and 10 Cherubs). It has no movement or aiming test of its own and no second phase; the rework adds them.

**The fight in one paragraph** (what players tell their friends). The Gatekeeper never moves; the hall does the talking. Every 28 s he raises his hand and the drone of Judgment rises: three seconds to get out of his sight, behind a pillar, under the lip of his own dais, or up in the gallery's shadow, or to break the cast before it ends: the Binder's shout, or the whole party pouring fire into him inside the Betrayer's Field and ducking at the last half-second if the red bar isn't full. Between Judgments the Host floods in through the Gate's bars toward wherever you hide, he strikes the dais with the keys and gold rings roll across the floor to be jumped, and he reaches out and **calls one of you by name**: a chain of light lifts you and drags you toward the Gate while your friends shoot the padlock on it, or he takes his due and heals. At half health he raises the silent first trumpet and the Gate's **Watchers** open: eyes that see behind your pillar. Shooting their tethers turns his own judgment against him and visibly tears a share of his health away; then the party hides.

**Decisions it asks for** (beyond "hide and shoot"): where to stand (the lip: full damage and hidden, but in the Host's path, the rings are born there and every Watcher sees it; the near pillars: the censer's and Discord's reach; the gallery: no rings and delayed Host pressure, but only the slug and the revolver reach him); hide or **break Judgment by force**; the Binder keeps Discord for Judgment or spends it on the Cherubs; leave cover to free a called friend or trust them; the Fallen taunts the Gatekeeper just before a call so it takes the call itself; in phase 2, which eye first (the gold threads show the ones that see you).

#### 3.2.1 The cycle

The fight uses **28 s scheduled cycles**, driven by a host tick counter. The seal sets `cycleStart`; `c = (tick − cycleStart) / 30`. Completing or interrupting a cast never advances this counter. At c 28, resolve the scheduled gaze and advance `cycleStart` by 840 ticks. The phase transition is the sole reset: at its end set `cycleStart = tick − 510`, so the next scheduled event is c 17. Each scheduled event is marked consumed once; a delayed cast cannot run twice.

**A fast build still sees the encounter.** Called starts in the first cycle. Until a Called episode has resolved (seal broken, timer expired, target gone, canceled cast or valid suppression by its deadline) and the first phase-2 aiming episode has resolved, lethal body damage leaves 1 HP. The aiming episode resolves when all its eyes have been destroyed and at least one scheduled Judgment has already resolved in the fight, or when its c 28 gaze deadline resolves. Body damage continues normally above 1 HP; this only defers the last hit, never adds a full idle cycle. Show `Break the seals of Judgment` on the boss bar while the final hit is deferred. If an early transition happened before the first Call's scheduled attempt, start one immediately after transition end (1 s tell; hold ≤6 s); it completes before the c 25 Judgment. A first Call already canceled by the threshold counts as resolved and is not replaced. Boss death after the gate opens follows §3.8; the deferred damage does not kill automatically.

**Casts and silence.** Volley, Toll, Called and Judgment share one cast slot. Priority at the same tick is Judgment, Called, Toll, Volley. Called may wait until c 10; the first Toll until c 10, the second until c 17. An event missing its deadline is consumed and skipped; a skipped/silence-suppressed Call also resolves its episode for the completion gate, so sustained valid interrupts cannot leave an immortal 1-HP body. Volleys have scheduled attempts c 3, 8, 13, 18, 23; a busy or silenced attempt is skipped, not queued. Judgment starts at c 25 and cancels another wind-up. If silenced at c 25 it is suppressed for this cycle; silence arriving during its cast interrupts it immediately. Body damage taken before interruption is kept; interruption never heals it. Blasphemy's stun does not stun the Gatekeeper; its taunt still applies.

#### 3.3.3 The identity: Judgment

Not a test with a soft fail: it's the one rule the boss is (game.md §11.3 item 4), and the first thing players learn about it. **Solo rule:** the same, at every party size (only the break threshold scales: 4.5% of max HP). **Bot rule:** hide (§3.7). **Fail cost:** death of whoever stays in his sight (a soul, revivable; the boss room is revivable). It wipes only a party in which nobody hides, after three seconds of the loudest tell in the game, every 28 s; a solo player dies only by staying in sight. One rule kept from today on purpose: a broken Judgment costs the party nothing and gives nothing but the seconds they didn't spend hiding.

**The Toll's hit test** (host, per tick, at most four swept checks): the origin is the Gatekeeper's accepted horizontal body center, canonical (10.5, 34.5), transformed and translated with him. On birth check the disk from radius 0 to 3.5 m, so entering its initial footprint cannot bypass the ring. The authored dais lip is not one fixed radius: its front/body contacts range from impact to about 0.4 s later. Subsequently sweep the annulus between the previous and current radii against each player's previous/current horizontal body position, including player radius. Check feet against the local floor at the crossing point: a floor ≤1 m above base and feet <0.5 m above it is hit. Each ring records its four-player hit bitset; moving back through a front never causes a second hit. Held players and the +2 m gallery are above the front. A ring lives at most seven seconds; a delayed second Toll impacts no sooner than seven seconds after the previous impact, otherwise it is skipped before its deadline. This keeps a single active ring/snapshot field sufficient.

**Volley safety:** each globe carries its volley ID and the volley keeps one accepted-player-hit bitset for its entire projectile lifetime. A player takes damage from at most one globe of that volley, including **direct contacts and inherited 1.5 m native shatter blasts** from terrain/range/player impacts. A later globe still bursts normally but does no additional damage to an already-hit player; another player may receive its own first hit. This holds at point-blank range and against a wall, including Swift Hymns. Shoot-down explosions cannot hurt players, and break credit comes only from damage actually accepted by the body during a Judgment cast.

**Shoot-down provenance:** a player ray/censer that shatters a hostile globe creates the inherited **40-damage, radius-3 m 3D blast**, with geometry LOS and the owning-room boundary. Eligible ordinary victims retain that shooter's normal kill/XP/drop entitlement; boss-add exceptions still apply. The blast supplies no Q/E/weapon kill, hit-count or damage/kill/derived-streak-tier talent/relic/combo proc. Native hostile impact shatters retain hostile collision/damage; a Mirror-reflected shot uses items-relics.md's direct secondary reflection and has no former globe blast.

The body bar flashes red at that accepted hit. ### 3.7 Bots, invalid targets and recovery

- **Cover:** at c 22, every bot computes an enemy-reachable hiding cell with no geometry LOS to the Gatekeeper's eye, preferably the nearest of the six pillar shadows. Exclude the dais, decorative plinths, active ring fronts and cells already reserved by another bot. Path toward it immediately, giving Judgment priority over damage and revives. If an eye threatens that cell, shoot the eye from cover. Recompute when a path is obstructed; never assume a remembered world coordinate remains cover after rotation.
- **Action priority:** avoid an imminent damaging front; reach Judgment cover; shoot its own Seal or a visible ally's Seal; clear eyes threatening a human; revive a soul when at least four seconds remain before Judgment; then ordinary add/body targeting. Bots use the same live range and LOS checks as humans. A bot under a hold can still aim and shoot. No mechanic needs a bot to interact with F.
- **Watcher membership and access:** capture connected living player IDs when the eyes open. Spawn one nearest unused marker per ID, then three random unused markers. Every selected point must be within **20 m horizontal distance and geometry LOS of at least one living human's eye** at opening. If all humans are souls and a bot is still alive, use living bots as the access anchors until a human revives. If an authored marker fails that check, move its eye toward the nearest living human along the marker-to-human direction to a clear hover position over a player-reachable cell between 8 and 20 m away; ties use canonical cell order. If no such cell exists, choose the closest clear hover point at least 2 m from that human, avoiding the entry strips and other eyes by 2 m. This changes an eye's hover location, never the room geometry. In solo every eye therefore starts visible and within censer range. The entire part cylinder must fit clear of solid geometry at the final hover point; LOS to its center alone is insufficient placement validation. The gold opening ray shows the final location for its two-second harmless tell. Random draws use simulation RNG; IDs retain canonical marker identity despite relocation. Later deaths/departures do not add replacement eyes; revived/joined players do not trigger a new pack. Gaze reads living targets at impact. Boss parts never award kill XP, halos, motes, items, streak credit or kill-trigger procs; the visible tether damage is the specified encounter reward.
- **Called targeting:** capture living IDs at cast start. Taunt wins if its owner is connected and living; otherwise choose uniformly, excluding the previous target if another exists. If that target dies/leaves during the tell, cancel this Call and mark it resolved. Do not retarget without a new tell. Silence or transition canceling a Call also counts as resolving it for the fast-build completion gate; skillful interrupts cannot softlock victory. If the transition's replacement Call is silenced, it is consumed as suppressed after its one-second tell.
- **No living target:** normal all-dead defeat takes priority. A last survivor can free its own Seal and revive the others. Body HP and add counts stay fixed at the seal after departures, as game.md §12.2 requires; parts use their captured/current membership rules, and a departed held target incurs no heal. A known rejoin restores the host-retained alive/HP or soul state on dry entry cells, with no free heal, invulnerability, cooldown or charge refresh. Disconnect already canceled an owner's hold; rejoin never recreates it or grows an existing Seal's scaling headcount. First-time boss admissions are closed at seal; ordinary first-time mid-combat admissions are souls under the core rule. A host departure ends the run by the core settlement rules; no local client invents a victory.
- **Add placement:** use gate markers for Host and field markers for seal adds, then the core safe placement fallback; never place inside a living body or geometry. A blocked point retains its queued summons until another point becomes safe or that batch expires (§3.4). An arrival's visible warning and grace period follow game.md §4.3. Boss parts use reserved capacity even when enemies or pickups have filled their normal lists; a damage mechanic must not silently fail to spawn.
- **Host processing order:** accepted incoming damage/part deaths, body threshold or boss death, participant removals and safe releases, scheduled casts/spawns, player hits, then all-dead defeat. Boss death disables further incoming damage and defeat before later effects in the tick; threshold changes cancel the old phase's damaging events before they resolve. Every cast/part has a unique encounter sequence ID so delayed packets cannot replay it.

During the player-hit stage, an accepted native shield-break transaction may itself cause new part/body damage. Finish that transaction under §3.5 and apply its threshold/victory immediately before continuing scheduled hostile hits or checking defeat. An expired/replaced shield and a rejected/invulnerable hit cannot enter this branch. Every break still has one identity and the originating cast's immutable provenance.

### 3.8 Boss death and the Stair of light

The first accepted death of the body latches victory. Cancel casts, rings, held states, all hostile projectiles and every queued summon; release held players safely; remove adds and parts without credit. Capture life-state groups **at that victory latch**, after any originating native shield-break transaction: souls, including retained disconnected souls, become alive at exactly 50%; only connected players already living in that captured group heal 30% unless Penance. Newly revived players do not also receive the living heal; absent living records receive none. Disable all damage and defeat. Grant the core's 1,500 boss XP with Sentence only to connected eligible humans; once per eligible admitted unclosed record, settle victory/clears/unlocks/deeds/pawns, retaining a disconnected record's settlement for supported rejoin without offline XP or automatic reward selection. A voluntarily closed record is ineligible. When `progressionEnabled=false`, all of these progression/inventory/allocation/claim changes stay within the disposable run; no profile writes or carried-item stakes occur, including subsequent shopping and exit. Reject all later combat/Bell countdowns, new encounters and Advocate bargains; only existing earned claims, shopping and unbroken props remain during the Stair valley. Show `The first trumpet is silent. Six to go.` The Gatekeeper and his unplayed trumpet burst into feathers and gold sparks; never blood.

Place the Stair at the **continuous center of the actual transformed reward square**, `rewardSpot+(2,2)`, after rooms.md §2.2 has transformed its occupied 4×4 rect and recovered the minimum-cell anchor. The canonical Gate center is `(34,34)` before stamping; transforming only the old top-left cannot define it. Transform the four authored pedestal cell points from §3.1.1 with their retained player-ID assignments; their shop view exposes that human's remaining personal stock, including the map if unsold, rather than creating four shared inventories. Purchases debit each wallet and save the resulting victory inventory once. Preserve existing unclaimed relic and Rite entitlements according to game.md; leaving after this point settles as victory.

Normal exit is eligible when all living connected humans have remained in the light for three continuous seconds, or thirty seconds after the first human enters. **Both conditions** respect the same pending-choice hold: an eligible earned chest/page entitlement or pending Rite offer belonging to a connected human, **including a page not yet touched**, delays exit for at most **60 s total**, shows `A choice is waiting`, and permits returning to it. An unopened chest requires already-earned reachable key access; a key in a skipped fight cannot authorize a new encounter or hold after victory. Once that allowance is exhausted, exit can resolve with an explicit notice of unclaimed choices. Independently, **120 s after boss death** always ends the run, even if no human entered the light, with the same pending-choice notice. Bots and disconnected records never hold either rule; no connected human left after victory ends the encounter immediately under core settlement. Clear time ends at boss death; post-win shopping and choices cannot change the Sundial or best clear time. The Stair is a valley with no respawning trickles and no hazard damage.
