# The Fallen: talent trees and new abilities

**Purpose:** the Fallen's three talent trees and its three new abilities for the roguelite. [game.md](game.md) §0 fixes precedence: its rules win over this catalog, [classes.md](../classes.md) supplies class identity/design intent, and [lore.md](../lore.md) supplies lore. Core §9.3–§9.4's explicit composition rules supersede the older one-effect wording. All numbers are initial values.

**Status:** completed design baseline (2026-10-11), reviewed through solo/co-op scenarios. Numbers are playtest baselines; scenario claims describe intended play, not measured results.

---

## 1. Requirements (from game.md)

**Structure (game.md §9.3–§9.4)**
- **3 trees**, each a playstyle domain (e.g. ranged, melee, area, damage over time, slowing, hazards, traps, or a combination). One tree deepens the Fallen's verb, **intercept**; the other two bend it. Each must be fun in a pure swarm of Blessed and playable solo.
- **7 nodes per tree**, 1 point each, one rank:

  | Tier | Nodes | Opens with distinct strictly lower-tier nodes in the tree (core §9.3) |
  |---|---|---|
  | 1 | 2 | 0 points |
  | 2 | 2 | 1 point |
  | 3 | 2: exactly **one ability node** and one other | 3 points |
  | 4 | 1 capstone | 5 points |

- **One capstone per player** (taking one, learned or as a Rite, locks the other two). The Pearly Gates allows **9 points** (one capstone build of 6 plus 3, or a spread) plus up to **4 Rites** per run; later heavens at most 14 points.
- **Each tree's ability node** teaches one new ability for **Q or E**, replacing a named base ability (Blasphemy or Falling Star) when assigned. LMB and RMB are never replaced; no new keys.

**What a node may do (game.md §9.3)**
- Change a **visible count or shape** (+1 pellet, a wider cone with a visibly larger effect); **replace** an attack's or ability's effect with a different visible one; or **add one visible effect** to an attack or ability that has only one.
- **Never:** an invisible percentage, a hidden or conditional rider, a "press when ready" buff, a stat node, a multi-rank node.
- Augments declare a **Delivery** channel (count, trajectory, placement or footprint of an existing effect) or a **Payload** channel (adds/replaces one emitted effect). One active augment per channel and resolved named input is allowed; different channels compose in Delivery-then-Payload order, with at most two visible effects. Same-channel alternatives exclude; an ability unlock occupies neither channel. A Rite may substitute for a learned augment only in its own channel, never a capstone/another Rite/explicit exclusion. Passive/movement nodes have no implicit channel exclusions. Explicit target sets below define alternative-ability inheritance; no other node silently inherits. Every six-point capstone path and nine-point extension obeys these rules and the two kill-discharge-node limit.
- Applying a state (game.md §4.11), or one more visible effect on enemies in a state, counts as the node's one visible effect.

**The class's rules (classes.md)**
- **Taunting belongs to Blasphemy only.** A new Q that replaces Blasphemy can't taunt; a Fallen who swaps it out has no taunt that run. No E alternative taunts.
- **Weakness stays:** the Fallen can't heal itself; no node gives it healing or a self-shield.
- Bound enemies aren't knocked back (the Binder's piles hold); keep that for any new knockback.
- Don't propose again: Shattered ground after Falling Star; a War Stomp (stun plus knockback); overheal turning into a shield; a double jump. A **charge** is the idea classes.md reserves for a new ability.
- Movement abilities run on the client first and the host accepts them (MVP §9.3); say how a new one splits.
- **Bots play the base kit** (game.md §12.4): no tree may be needed for a bot to work.

**Enemy states (game.md §4.11): the bridge to relics and teammates**
- The Fallen's base kit **applies** `airborne` (Falling Star's launch) and `taunted` (Blasphemy, which only it and no talent may apply); it **reads** Falling Star's landing and the shotgun skip knockback on `bound` enemies (the Binder's piles hold; bound enemies take double).
- **Each tree** has at least one node that makes an attack or ability **apply a state the base kit doesn't**: `bound` (at most as long as Chains' 2.5 s), `slowed` or `pooled` (an area it leaves); not `taunted` again, not `silenced`, not `censed`.
- **The class** has at least **two nodes that read a state another class's base kit applies**: `bound`, `slowed`, `silenced` (the Binder), `censed` (the Heretic), `pooled` (the Betrayer's Field of Blood) (e.g. "kills of `censed` enemies by the slug burst into a 2 m ring").
- Fill the `States` column (`applies slowed`, `reads bound`); a relic or a teammate's kit can then be named that pays off on it.

**Rites (game.md §9.10): every node is also a run-only pick**
- A node is offered as a Rite only with its lower-tier gate, exclusions, current ability and usefulness conditions satisfied (game.md §9.10). Its card explains one visible rule; conditional readers require a producible state source. Learned-only gates never borrow temporary Rite points.
- **Source closure:** use game.md §9.9 and items-relics.md §2.1.1's finite producibility closure after resolving channels and substitutions. Seed only effective native kit, actually available carried items, active same-room areas and usable same-room hazards; admit a triggered emitter only when its trigger is reachable from those seeds. An equipped but untriggerable relic, an absent ally, a surrendered payload or an unseeded cycle cannot qualify a state reader. `own` predicates require the owner's producible source, not an ally's flag. Timed/source prerequisites are checked when offering/taking, then ordinary loss of a source makes the held effect dormant without erasing ownership.
- **Temporary substitution:** a Rite augment may suppress one conflicting learned augment in the same Delivery/Payload channel of its implicit named-input group; explicit exclusions, another Rite and capstones are never displaced. Suppressed ownership remains for gates, but only effective nodes produce effects, metadata or passive dispatch. Evaluate usefulness after suppression; show the sacrificed effect and any effects losing their sole state source. Table annotations explaining same-input/same-channel conflicts are implicit-group documentation, not extra `Excludes` prerequisites. No point refund or cooldown/source refresh is granted; the learned effect returns at the run's end.
- An augment of Q or E is offered only while that ability is assigned, so **each tree has at least 2 tier-1/tier-2 nodes on `LMB`, `RMB` or `passive`** to support early offers. These still obey gates, exclusions and state-source usefulness checks.
- An ability node taken as a Rite goes straight into its slot (Q or E) with the replaced ability's remaining cooldown; it must be playable without any of its tree's other nodes.
- Rite choices and valid Confessional loadout edits may commit during a valley or an encounter countdown, with the same living-player, range and room checks. At the actual combat seal, close overlays and reject later commits without discarding their pending offers. This does not widen the valley rules for items, regeneration or teleporting.
- Bots never get Rites; nothing here may be needed for a bot.

**Quantities:** 21 nodes, 3 new abilities, 3 capstones.

Origins (for card flavor, stated plainly): the Fallen kept the red-hot iron walls of Dis, the city of lower Hell (A); in Dante's seventh circle the blasphemers lie on burning sand under a slow rain of fire (B); in the war in Heaven the rebel angels were cast down, a third of the stars of heaven with them (C).

**Shared rules for the nodes below** (so each card stays one line):

- **Launch.** To *launch* an enemy is to knock it back with `airborne`: Falling Star's landing (the base kit) and, from nodes, Heave, Wormwood, Cast Out and Gatebreaker's fling. Every launch uses Falling Star's arc: **0.4 s, at most 1 m high**, drawn only; the sim moves the enemy along the ground as any knockback (classes.md), so its hit cylinder stays where the shots are aimed. Only ground enemies (Blessed, Choristers, the elite) are launched; Cherubs are knocked back as today but never "land"; **bound enemies and the Gatekeeper are never launched or shoved** (piles hold). A launched enemy **lands** at the end of its 0.4 s, if it's still alive. Launched enemies don't count for wading (M12 §3.1), as after Falling Star today: each launch is listed in an event as `starLanded` lists its `launched`.
- **Hellfire** (tree B). The Fallen's fires are **burning floor cells** (the 1 m map cells), each with an end tick (a cell lit again keeps the later end). An enemy whose actual feet contact that burning floor surface takes **5 dmg (10 if bound) on the tick it first stands in fire and every 0.5 s after while it stays in any**, at most once per 0.5 s however many fires cover it (a new `eFireTick`, like `eIncenseTick`); it's `pooled` while it stands there. A Blessed that stays in fire dies at its 4th pulse (1.5 s), like one in incense. Incense and fire are different sources: an enemy in both takes both. Fire never hurts players, never breaks props and doesn't reach hovering Cherubs; a Brought Low flyer can burn during actual floor contact. It can reach the Gatekeeper only if a valid fire patch occupies its actual dais-foot surface; ordinary arena/lip fire never hits or pools the elevated boss. No enemy avoids class fire (it isn't in the flow fields, so Choristers and the elite walk through it too; authored environmental hazard avoidance is separate). At most **600 burning cells** per Fallen; evict oldest first-lit tick, ties owning-room row-major cell ID. Refreshing a still-lit cell does not reset that age, and evicting a cell removes all its fire contributors. Look: low red-orange flames with rising embers on each cell (game.md §4.10's damage color, with motion), fading over its last 0.5 s.
- **Button kills.** Native weapon/equipped-ability damage and its direct shape augments retain that slot's root provenance (`depth=0`): shotgun/slug hits, Falling Star landing, Gatebreaker lane, Cast Out blast and **Rain of Fire's delayed native fire, Brand's directly placed RMB fire and Blasphemer's Ring's directly cast Q fire**. Passive-generated footstep/kill fire, Pyre pulses, Red Iron, Underfoot, As We Fell, Wrath and capstone crashes are secondary (`depth=1`), never Q/E kills and cannot start a new hit/kill/damage proc. The Burning Plain reads eligible deaths in its fire and spreads area only; its own spread does no immediate damage. A direct owned item launch dispatches one landing bundle to each applicable owned landing passive once, with controls before damage, at depth1 and no Q/E slot; a relic/combo launch dispatches no authored landing bundle. New independent primary actions can read every still-active state. Relic trigger 3 uses this same vocabulary.
- **States applied here:** `slowed` 3 s (Weight of Sin), `bound` 1 s (Broken Wings), `airborne` 0.4 s (every launch), `pooled` while in fire. Re-applying refreshes (game.md §4.11).
- **New ability ids:** `gatebreaker` (E), `rainOfFire` (E), `castOut` (Q). Ability-unlock nodes do not occupy augmentation exclusion groups (game.md §9.3). Son of the Morning explicitly shapes every authored alternative in its target set; other augments remain tied to their named input. Alternatives can still make nonmatching base augments dormant; only applicable nodes are eligible Rite cards. Learned gates use learned lower-tier nodes alone; Rites only help the gates of later Rites.
- **Resolution and lifetime.** Follow game.md's same-room LOS, floor clipping, source-token ownership, root-action proc bounds and canonical movement envelopes. Native ability impact damage resolves before its landing bundle. Broken Wings applies its bind before As We Fell's impact; the remaining bind sources determine a single ×2 multiplier. Passive landing discharges and crashes are secondary damage and cannot start on-kill damage chains; the native Falling Star impact retains E provenance. A corpse's scheduled crash may draw and damage at its last accepted landing point, but never reads a reused enemy slot. Clear/transition deletes outstanding combat effects. All Fallen fires share one 5-per-0.5-s fire budget per enemy. An actual covering cell supplies pulse provenance (newest currently active source, then source ID); only outside actual fire may Pyre supply that pulse, using the newest eligible Pyre source. A merged cell retains each contribution's original expiry and provenance: a spread/footstep refresh may keep the cell lit but never prolong Brand/Rain/Q primary attribution. Pyre uses the same budget, while Heretic incense retains its separate budget. Floor contact uses actual feet: a Cherub grounded by Brought Low can burn until it leaves the surface; an ordinary hovering Cherub cannot.
- **Native slot damage metadata:** Blasphemy Q **false** at base or with Weight of Sin, **true** with Blasphemer's Ring; Cast Out Q **true**. Falling Star/Gatebreaker/Rain of Fire E **true**. Passive Red Iron, Underfoot, Pyre, Wrath and landing/crash discharges never turn a nondamaging equipped Q into a Q-kill source. This metadata determines whether a seal relic can activate on the current kit.

**Composition data (game.md §9.3).** All nodes of Kind `augment` appear exactly once here. Passive/capstone rules do not consume either channel; they still obey source predicates, effect rules and discharge limits.

| Channel | Node IDs |
|---|---|
| Delivery | `fallen.a.2a`, `fallen.c.1b` |
| Payload | `fallen.a.1b`, `fallen.b.1b`, `fallen.b.2a`, `fallen.b.3b`, `fallen.c.1a`, `fallen.c.2b` |

**Enemy event eligibility.** Trickle kills/hits never dispatch enemy-triggered talent/relic/combo discharges or advance kill meters. Ordinary native attacks, their direct shape/state rules and defensive passives still work. Watchers/Seals never supply kill-listener or hit-count events; authored incidental damage uses the boss-part area modifier. Ordinary boss summons permit their explicitly bounded ordinary combat procs, combat streak credit and Armageddon eligibility, but never Bread/extra passive mote harvest or item/halo/XP rewards. Boss parts and trickle never build deed streaks. World damage supplies the core death entitlement and recent setup assist only, never a fabricated player/button kill. Area-only propagation keeps its explicit core exception.

**Kill-discharge data.** `fallen.b.1b` Fire and Brimstone is this class's kill-triggered talent discharge node. At most **two** are effective in one build, counting learned nodes, Rites and capstones together. A listener still counts when its read state or target is temporarily absent; only actual named-input dormancy or suppression removes its active count. Use real remaining source/phase predicates for offers, not this limit as a dynamic target counter. Each root collects an active node once at that root's **first eligible resolving tick**, bundling all qualifying deaths of that root/tick after native damage; the row's origin/victim caps apply. Later native pulses retain the dispatch flag and cannot re-dispatch that node. Never collect separately per pellet, branch or fatal victim. Eligibility and ordinary damage happen before the secondary bundle; secondary kills cannot seed another bundle. Eligibility still uses per-victim pre-fatal state; the flag and any cooldown remain through death/reassignment/reconnect and the root's delayed lifetime. The Burning Plain/The Sixth Circle's existing area-only propagation exception, native ability damage, Bread's bounded harvest, landing effects, motes and revival listeners are outside this kill-discharge category. There is no banked third proc or retroactive state qualification.

**Combos.** Every launch supplies `airborne` for a subsequent primary hit or an independent teammate's state-reading attack. Falling Star and Cast Out can satisfy "one attack hits 5+" by damaging five distinct enemies; Wormwood's damage-free surrounding launches do not count, and its slug normally damages only one. Broken Wings turns the thrower into a `bound` source: what you throw lands rooted at double damage for the Betrayer's Silver Bullet (half cost), its Broken Vessel (bound kills shatter), the Heretic's incense (10 per pulse) and any "kill a `bound` enemy" relic. As We Fell's own landing kill is secondary and therefore cannot start those kill relics. In a spread build, Heave with B's Brand throws the crowd with the shotgun and burns the floor it lands on with the slug, and B's Rain of Fire takes Falling Star's place for a C + B Fallen who throws the crowd with Cast Out into its own fire. Cast Out from the side throws a crowd off a swarmed ally, and into hazard cells in a hazard room (game.md §4.10: luring and knockback pay, and the Blessed don't avoid them).

## 6. Playtest-in-head notes

**Paper walkthroughs** (each imagined in a pure swarm, a solo 2×1 horde room of about 265 Blessed over 4 waves, and a ranged room, a solo 1×1 of about 110 Blessed and 6 Choristers behind cover; then the same with a Binder and a Heretic):

- **A, capstone + 3:** Red Iron, Weight of Sin, Unbowed, Gatebreaker, Underfoot, Wrath of Dis + C's Heave, As We Fell, Wormwood (Through the Ranks skipped to fit nine points; it now composes with Heave).
  - *Swarm:* walk into the wave unslowed, Blasphemy: the crowd trudges in slowed and Underfoot bursts each one as it presses on you; once the 3 s slow ends, strikers take Red Iron's 10 and Wrath fills at about 30 HP/s, erupting every 3–4 s. Gatebreaker rakes an 18 m lane whose flung edges die on landing (As We Fell); Heave makes the shotgun kill out to 12 m. The intended anvil feel comes from alternating these moments. HP is the limit: unhealed, about 15–25 s in a full surround, so Gatebreaker doubles as the way out (it isn't invulnerable, but nothing keeps up with 15 m/s).
  - *Ranged:* Gatebreaker reaches Choristers on the same floor, dealing 40 in its central lane or 10 plus As We Fell's 20 on the flung edge. The central hit does not launch; neither route kills a fresh 60-HP Chorister alone, so finish with the slug. Red Iron and Wrath do little against globes (7 per globe after the Hide). Weaker than in the swarm, as intended; it can't reach a ledge without Falling Star.
  - *Found:* Underfoot and Wrath of Dis pull slightly against each other (trampled enemies never strike, so the ring fills slower while the crowd is slowed). Kept: the two alternate inside one Blasphemy cycle (3 s of trampling, then 9 s of striking and erupting), and both say "stand in the crowd".
- **B, full tree + 2:** all seven B nodes + A's Red Iron and Unbowed.
  - *Swarm:* Rain of Fire on the side the wave arrives from, Blasphemer's Ring at your feet, then hold: the taunted crowd crosses 10 m of rain and 4 m of ring; Fire and Brimstone keeps the ring lit as you shotgun. Pyre carries that same pulse for 2 s after a survivor leaves the burning lane, letting the kiter finish edge-clipping enemies; it adds coverage, never a second overlapping pulse. The Burning Plain spreads floor cells from those deaths. Between cooldowns, Brimstone Tread supplies the chase lane. All seven B nodes plus Red Iron and Unbowed are active solo; the 600-cell cap bounds the floor spread.
  - *Ranged:* the fire is slow against Choristers (Brand under one: 40 in 4 s; the slug kills it anyway) and never touches Cherubs. Weakest tree in a flyers room; the slug carries it, as the base kit's does.
  - *Tradeoff:* without Falling Star and the stun, this Fallen has no movement escape and no stunned walk-out for a swarmed ally. Unbowed is the natural two-point dip. Pyre is useful through own fire even without a Heretic; with neither a censed source nor own fire it is filtered from Rite offers.
- **C, capstone + 3:** Heave, Son of the Morning, As We Fell, Wormwood, Cast Out, A Third of the Stars + A's Red Iron, Unbowed, B's Brimstone Tread.
  - *Swarm:* Falling Star into the crowd: the crater dies, the 3.5–8 m ring flies 4 m, dies on landing and crashes onto the crowd 7.5–12 m out. Cast Out does the same to a 10 m cone every 10 s; Heave and As We Fell make every shotgun pellet a kill out to 12 m; Wormwood pops clusters at up to 50 m. The most spectacular clear of the three and the biggest "rain of angels" moment.
  - *Ranged:* Wormwood and Cast Out handle Chorister groups (each Chorister still needs the slug: 10 + 20 leaves it at 30). Good.
  - *Found:* the strongest swarm clear: Falling Star with A Third of the Stars empties about a 12 m disk every 10 s in a dense crowd. Kept for the first playtest, as its capstone moment; **the first tuning knobs** are the crash radius (1.2 m) and Son of the Morning's 8 m. Its cost is real in co-op: no taunt, so the party is unprotected. Heave also makes the shotgun launch like Falling Star, which blurs classes.md's "the shotgun's knockback stays a flat shove so the landing stands out"; acceptable because it's a choice, and the landing still bursts and crashes where the shotgun's launch only flies.
- **Spread 3 + 3 + 3:** A's Red Iron, Weight of Sin, Unbowed; B's Brimstone Tread, Fire and Brimstone, Pyre; C's Son of the Morning, As We Fell, Wormwood (base Q and E).
  - *Swarm:* Blasphemy slows the crowd, you wade in unslowed, the shotgun's kills light the floor, Falling Star's 8 m disk dies on landing, the slug pops clusters. Fun, a bit of everything, no new ability.
  - *Tradeoff:* `LMB`, `RMB` and `Q:blasphemy` are shared across trees, so this 3 + 3 + 3 uses Pyre as its third B point. Own fire supplies its carry even solo; the Heretic's incense adds its party payoff. The three learned dips satisfy their gates independently.
- **Base kit + 2 random Rites** (a level-1 character; only tier-1 nodes are eligible until a tree holds a Rite):
  - *Heave + Red Iron:* the shotgun bounces the crowd off at 12 m without killing more; it reads as crowd control, and the second Rite works at once. Fine.
  - *Weight of Sin + Fire and Brimstone:* Q supplies taunt plus slow, replacing its stun; LMB supplies damage plus kill-fire, replacing its survivor shove. These are separate buttons with two effects each. A later Through the Ranks Delivery lengthens the same shotgun attack without adding another rider. The slowed crowd presses into the cells of its dead, but neither augment keeps the base immediate escape effect. An unplanned combo with a visible cost, the kind Rites should make.
  - *Heave, then As We Fell:* the first C Rite opens C's tier 2, so the second offer can hold As We Fell; together they change the run's kit for good (the shotgun kills out to 12 m, Falling Star's whole 6 m disk dies). Brimstone Tread first would open only B's tier 2.
  - *Gate:* an ability Rite needs three lower-tier points in its tree, so a level-1 character sees one only after three Rites in that tree. A level-4+ character may see one on the first clear if its three learned points meet that gate; a spread allocation does not automatically qualify.

**Other checks.** Bots use the base kit (game.md §12.4). Bound enemies are not launched/shoved by Heave, Wormwood, Cast Out, Gatebreaker or Unbowed. Fire uses per-cell expiry and a shared pulse timer; landing records use stable source/entity IDs. Crash damage merges per-root victim sets rather than summing every overlapping corpse disc. Gatebreaker checks swept accepted movement. Synchronize active fires, landing records, ring fill and source/end ticks in events plus floorSync; an event-only client cannot recover still-active effects after rejoin. Performance targets need authorized measurement; no unmeasured claim of one scan per tick is made.

**Rules that were hard to satisfy:**
- **Composition:** Through the Ranks shapes the existing pellet path; Heave replaces its survivor shove. Their combination is valid, while Heave and kill-fire remain alternatives for that same payload. Son of the Morning has explicit alternative numbers rather than undefined inheritance.
- **Ability unlocks and augments are separate.** game.md §9.3 exempts unlock nodes from augment exclusion. The authored alternatives need no specific augmentation to function as a Rite.
- **The stun on Blasphemy** counts as its second effect, so a Blasphemy node can only replace it (Weight of Sin, Blasphemer's Ring), never add; both trees that keep Blasphemy want to change it, hence the cross-tree pair.

No tests were run or created for this design review. First observed tuning should compare A's surviving-damage eruption rate, B's floor coverage and C's merged crash reach; hypothetical density kill rates above are estimates, not acceptance evidence.

### 6.1 Collected veteran: two different firewalker runs

Take the legal nine learned nodes **all seven B nodes + A Red Iron/Unbowed**, assigning Blasphemy and Rain of Fire. This veteran now has both same-Payload substitutions and compatible Delivery upgrades. Son of the Morning can widen the assigned Rain immediately; Through the Ranks can add a piercing Delivery beside learned Fire and Brimstone because A already has Red Iron as its tier-1 gate. Choosing those upgrades or a different payload changes the same completed character's next attempt.

- **Held burning lane:** take A Weight of Sin as a Rite, suppressing learned B Blasphemer's Ring. A now holds Red Iron, Unbowed and Weight of Sin: three lower nodes, so A Underfoot is a legal next Rite and has the new slow source. Rain, Brand and footprints still supply the burning lane and B capstone; Q loses its immediate fire circle in return for slower arrivals and close trampling. No A capstone can be added beside the learned B capstone.
- **Launch and land:** take C Heave as a Rite, suppressing learned B Fire and Brimstone. C's tier-2 gate opens with that one held tier-1 node; As We Fell is a legal second Rite with the owner's Heave launches as its source. Shotgun survivors fly and land for damage; the shotgun loses its kill-fire patches and survivor shove. B's own Rain/Brand/footprints still sustain its capstone. Wormwood may later substitute for learned Brand; a compatible LMB Delivery Rite may coexist, but a second LMB Payload Rite cannot replace Heave.

The held learned and Rite IDs each count once for gates, including suppressed ownership; learned-only gates never borrow the Rites. These are alternative tactical identities, not an entitlement to four useful offers or proven balance. Fixed cards can be passed or left pending under game.md; changing a build never redraws them. A C capstone card specifically requires an assigned own Q/E launch: a Wormwood-only launcher with Q Blasphemy and E Rain cannot claim that capstone's usefulness.

### 6.2 Composed and alternative kits

**Piercing thrower:** a.1a Red Iron + a.2a Through the Ranks (a tier 2 has its own tier-1 gate), c.1a Heave + c.2a As We Fell is a legal four-node spread. The Delivery gives each pellet one extra direct victim if it killed the first; the Payload replaces surviving victims' shoves with launches. One root carries the native pellet hit set and one launch per acquired survivor, followed by secondary landing damage; it never creates another shot or on-kill chain. A Fire and Brimstone Payload Rite can substitute for Heave while keeping the piercing Delivery, visibly changing thrown survivors into burning kill cells.

**Improved alternatives:** c.1b Son of the Morning is eligible with Gatebreaker, Rain or Cast Out assigned as well as the base leap. Its exact side-band/radius/cone data appears in the row; Gatebreaker central damage never becomes a second hit on widened side victims. Rain plus Son of the Morning covers a radius-7 patch, but the common fire-cell and pulse limits remain. Gatebreaker plus As We Fell or Broken Wings still uses one landing bundle per accepted launch. Thus learning an alternative keeps real upgrade paths and source-reading opportunities without an undefined inherited base effect.
