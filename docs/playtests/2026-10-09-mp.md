# Playtest 4 (multiplayer with bots) — 2026-10-09

Tomas and Marek, online, with bots filling the party, on the M12 build with the follow-up (stages 6–10). Fixes in commit a52f2fd; the rest moved to [m12-followup-2.md](../m12-followup-2.md).

## Tomas's notes

- The run took almost 8 minutes, without bigger troubles.
- The boss fight's length felt right, but there were few enemies in the room: one or two big waves, then mostly the boss.
- The bots played well, which made it hard to tell whether the game is too easy.
- Bots died a few times and were revived, as expected.

**Issues**
- After the game, back in the Lobby, the bots stayed on the Results screen, so we couldn't start again with them.
- In the Lobby a picked class could only be changed by picking another one. Once every class was taken, nobody could change. Clicking your class again should clear it.
- The default master volume (80%) was too loud during a voice call; 50% would be better.

**Nice to have** (Marek's ideas)
- A score sheet during the game, to see how the others are doing: Tab could show the table in the top-left corner, like the hints panel.
- "Monster kill" and "ultra kill" call-outs, Diablo 3 style: kills in quick succession build a streak, a pause of about 2 s ends it and announces it. The score sheet could show each player's best streak.
- Slower movement when running backwards.

## Decisions

- **Fixed now:** clicking your own class clears it; auto-joined bots press `Back to lobby` 2 s after Results; master volume 50%.
- **Difficulty:** bots don't panic and revive reliably, so a run with bots doesn't measure difficulty. The next playtest records the bot count, and one run is humans only.
- **Few enemies at the boss:** expected. M12 left the boss arena as it was (one wave and the summons); its rework stays deferred.
- **Score sheet, streaks, backpedal:** specified in [m12-followup-2.md](../m12-followup-2.md). The streak's gap is 1 s rather than 2 s, since in a horde something dies every 2 s and the streak would only end with the wave. Backpedalling is the one that matters most: it works against playtest 2's "back up and hold the trigger".
