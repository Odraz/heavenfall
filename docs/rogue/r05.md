## As implemented (2026-10-18)

Implemented under Tomas's direct "I have provided art of remaining talents for R05. Process them and Implement R05" request, without a separate preparation pass. The contracts below record what was built; [r05-report.md](r05-report.md) has the checks, quick builds and play checklist.

### Shared capabilities (stage 1–2)

- **Catalogs and resolver** (`src/data/talents.ts`, `src/sim/build.ts`). 84 nodes, 12 alternatives, 12 capstones in one registry. Son of the Morning's mixed targets (`targets` on E plus `qTargets: ['castOut']`) resolve to whichever matching ability is assigned and occupy Delivery there. Capstones that need their base ability (`needs`: Resurrection → Communion, All of Tartarus → Chains, The Wind → Discord) are dormant without it and refused as Rites with `<ability> is not assigned`.
- **Producibility closure** (`producedStates`): base kit states, then active nodes' `applies`; a node with `reads` contributes only once that state is produced. Ability nodes contribute only while assigned. Rite needs added: `pooledSource`, `airborneSource`, `ownLaunch`, `ownQELaunch`, `ownFire`, `ownFireOrCensed`, `ownIncense`, `ownIncensePooled`, `pitch`, `irons`, `twoAllies`, `flyers`. The Rite context carries allies' active talents, live R5 areas and fires in the room, and whether the floor still holds survivors (Watchmen, Dominions, Cantors) and flyers.
- **Launches** (`src/sim/launches.ts`). A launch is a 0.4 s ground knockback (drawn as Falling Star's arc) with a landing record `{owner, kind: qe | weapon | item | other, cast}`. Bound enemies and the Gatekeeper are never launched; Cherubs slide and never land. Landings run once per tick after movement: controls (Broken Wings, Where They Fall), then damage (As We Fell), then A Third of the Stars' crash, merged per cast. Clients get `launched {playerId, slots}`.
- **Floor fire** (`src/sim/floorFire.ts`). Per owner, 1 m cells with end ticks; the Fallen holds 600, the Heretic 400; a new cell over the cap puts out the oldest first-lit one. Each contribution keeps its own end and root (pulse provenance). Contact is actual feet within 0.3 m of the cell floor; the Gatekeeper never burns. The Fallen pulses on its own per-enemy budget, the Heretic on the shared incense budget. Changes go out as `fire {playerId, cells, ends}` once per tick; `floorSync.fires` restores every burning cell.
- **Primary kills**: `Talents.primary` marks its damage as a root's native hit, so a death knows it was primary (Bread of Angels' harvest). Roots now cover the Fallen's shotgun, slug, Falling Star, Cast Out, Gatebreaker and Rain; every censer and its cloud or furrow, the Shroud burst, Trial by Fire, Wall of Fire and Witnesses; the Binder's Chain Gun bullets and Scourge swings.

### Per-class contracts

- **The Fallen** (`src/sim/talentsFallen.ts`). Gatebreaker: the E press starts a client-predicted charge (15 m/s, ≤180°/s turn toward the view, 0.3–1.2 s); the next press (the key's release) ends it no earlier than 0.3 s. The host cuts along the accepted positions each tick, at most 18 m: the 1 m lane takes 40, the 2.5 m band (3.5 m with Son of the Morning) takes 10 and is flung 4 m sideways; the band tests only what the path has passed, plus a terminal cap. Cooldown 10 s starts when the charge ends. Rain of Fire: hold E shows the 5 m (7 m) ring; the release press is cast at the host's current aim, must be ≤30 m, in the caster's room and in sight, else spends nothing; cells light 0.5 s later for 6 s. Cast Out resolves on the press like Discord. Wrath of Dis fills from HP and shield removed after the Hide and is sent as an owner `meter` event.
- **The Heretic Saint** (`src/sim/talentsHeretic.ts`). Incense clouds live here: radius, wearer (detached at the last point on death, leaving, teleport or another room), root, and a published `cloud` area (x1 radius, y1 wearer, z1 Pillar). At most 6 clouds and furrows per Heretic, oldest by creation tick then id. Incense keeps the M12 cadence; the Saint's fire, furrows and Pitch pulse an enemy when its shared budget allows. Bread of Angels harvests distinct primary kills within 8 m in sight (not the Gatekeeper or its summons), at most six motes per cast, 20 s each; a full pickup queue consumes nothing. Cunning Folk and Resurrection's rally steer motes through `Pickups.update`'s `steer` hook; only hurt players collect.
- **The Binder** (`src/sim/talentsBinder.ts`). Own bind sources per enemy: Binder, cast, incarnation, ordinary end and ceiling (cast start + 6 s); a pull records its source on arrival. The Chain Holds transfers the remaining time without refresh; Sunk in the Lake holds an own bind while pooled up to the ceiling. Brought Low lowers a bound flyer to its floor at 13.3 m/s (0.3 s from hover) and lets it climb back at 6 m/s. The Well and the Boundary Chain cast on the press from the host's aim (walked like Cast Down; refused without cooldown when the eye can't see the point).

### Client

`FireView` draws burning cells; talent areas draw the Rain circle, Well, frost, Boundary chain, furrow, Bread ring and cloud rims; incense puffs come from the host's cloud areas (worn clouds follow their wearer, pillars add tall puffs). Wading talents filter by snapshot flags (Unbowed: bound only; Iron Stride: not slowed; Walk in the Smoke: none inside the owner's incense; Gatebreaker's charge ignores wading). The R4 aimed-hold request was not needed: Rain of Fire reuses Falling Star's client hold state and sends its release as the press.
