# R2 Pressure and pickups

**Status: planned** (revision 9). Dependency: accepted R1. Prepare this brief against R1's code before implementing it ([development-workflow.md](development-workflow.md)).

## Playable outcome

Fights where staying in the safe ring loses and moving pays. Ivory amphorae break into coins, motes and the odd item; red sanctuary lamps explode in chains; gold chalices heal everyone near them. Halos drop inside the crowd and fade; heal motes come from ranged enemies, elites and vases. The Dominion walks in behind its crowd and raises its mace: step out and its slam bursts its own horde. Refiner's Fire burns the crowd that walks through it while the Choristers and the Dominion path around it. Every 2×2 room ends in the crest of 1 000 Blessed. The weapons, hits, deaths and breakables are recorded sounds with three takes each.

**Before:** clear the room by holding the trigger and backing up. **After:** a reason to go in, a reason to move, and a reason to keep an enemy alive.

**Fixture limits:** item pickups lie in the world but can only be used once R3 adds the inventory (until then, an item pickup is used on touch if usable, else stays); halos accumulate in a wallet shown on the HUD with nothing to buy until R3; the marked amphora drops its gold halo instead of the Chart until R6.
