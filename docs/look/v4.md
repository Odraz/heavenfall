# V4 Effects

**Status: planned** (drafted 2026-10-25). Dependencies: V1 (HDR, bloom) and V2 (kit material with the light hook) accepted. Rules: [spec.md](spec.md). Tomas's side: [guide.md](guide.md#v4).

## Playable outcome

Fights look and feel heavier without becoming harder to read: Refiner's Fire is a sunk channel of flowing lava with a dark crust, flames licking its edges, embers rising and a warm glow on the walls and enemies around it; muzzle flashes and explosions briefly light the room; hits throw sparks off armor and dust off stone; kills leave debris that catches the light; ability effects glow in HDR with soft edges where they meet the floor.

Every ability keeps **one visible effect** and its state color; no effect hides a hazard edge, a telegraph or an enemy.

Out of scope: new abilities, new hazards, sound.

## Frame-time share

Effects and lights: High ≤ +10%, Medium ≤ +5%, Low ≈ 0 (no lights, half particles).

## Ordered stages

### 1. Particle upgrade

- `particles.ts` keeps its caps (`MAX_PARTICLES` 4 000, `MAX_PIECES` 512) and one instanced draw per blend mode. New per-particle data: HDR intensity (for bloom), flipbook frame range, soft-particle flag.
- Soft particles: on Medium and High, smoke, dust and glows fade where they meet geometry, using the scene target's depth (a depth texture on the V1 target).
- Particle counts per effect scaled by the tier (spec §6).

Tests: caps hold under a crest (the existing mass-kill aggregation); tier scaling; no per-frame allocation.

### 2. Flipbooks

- Claude simulates and renders fire, smoke puff, dust burst, spark burst and ember in Blender (`scripts/blender/fx/`), as 8 × 8 flipbooks, black background for additive ones, encoded KTX2. Fallback: the current single images (`fx-flames`, `fx-smoke`, `ember.png`).
- Inked edges where the style needs them (smoke and dust get a thin dark rim like the concept's).

### 3. Refiner's Fire

- Lava shader for the channel surface (`hazards.ts`, `fireView.ts`): two scrolling flow-noise layers over `tex-lava`, a crust that cracks open where it's hottest, HDR emission for bloom. The hazard's edge stays crisp and the same color as today, so its extent reads exactly.
- Flames along the edges from the fire flipbook, embers rising, a heat shimmer above the channel on High (a small screen-space distortion limited to the channel's screen rectangle).
- Its warm glow is already baked (V1 stage 7); a slow flicker modulates it through one dynamic light per visible channel group, within the light cap.

Tests: the hazard's drawn extent equals its simulated extent (as today); shimmer only on High.

### 4. Dynamic glow lights

- Up to the tier's count (spec §6) of point lights, chosen each frame by priority and distance from: muzzle flashes, explosions and blasts (sanctuary lamps), ability bursts, lava flicker. Short lifetimes; no shadows.
- Kit and floor shaders evaluate them per pixel (V2's hook); billboards per vertex, so a muzzle flash lights the Blessed in front of it.
- One uniform block updated per frame; nothing else per frame.

Tests: priority and cap; lights expire; Low has none.

### 5. Hits, kills and debris

- Sparks on armored enemies, dust on stone, feathers and gold dust on angels, from the flipbooks; scaled by the crest's aggregation so a mass kill stays bounded.
- Torn halves and debris (V3's renders) take the colored light and the dynamic lights.
- Impact decals on stone (scorch, chips) as a small pooled set in the floor/kit chunk, oldest replaced first.

### 6. Abilities pass

- Each class's effects re-tuned for HDR and bloom, with soft particles where they touch the floor. One visible effect per ability; state colors unchanged; telegraphs and rings stay drawn by code as today.
- Tomas reviews each class in a short session.
- **Recommended benchmark point** (ask Tomas): a crest of 1 500 with all four classes' abilities.

## Play checklist

1. Refiner's Court: does the lava look dangerous and alive? Is its edge exactly where it hurts?
2. Fire the Chain Gun in a dim corridor: does the flash light the walls and enemies briefly, without flicker that tires the eyes?
3. A mass kill: satisfying debris, but can you still see the next wave?
4. Every ability of your class: still one clear effect, still the right color?
