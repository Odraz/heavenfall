# The Betrayer: talent trees and new abilities

**Purpose:** the Betrayer's three talent trees and its three new abilities for the roguelite. [game.md](game.md) §0 fixes precedence: its rules win over this catalog, [classes.md](../classes.md) supplies class identity/design intent, and [lore.md](../lore.md) supplies lore. Core §9.3–§9.4's explicit composition rules supersede the older one-effect wording. All numbers are initial values.

**R06 follow-up (2026-10-22):** the in-game card wording lives in `src/data/talents.ts` and was rewritten in plain words; the Rule column below keeps the design wording. Glancing Silver and Behind His Back now combine. See [r06-followup.md](r06-followup.md) §1.

**Status:** completed design baseline (2026-10-11), reviewed through solo/co-op scenarios. Numbers remain playtest baselines; scenario clear rates are analytical estimates.

## 1. Requirements (from game.md)

**Structure (game.md §9.3–§9.4)**
- **3 trees**, each a playstyle domain (e.g. ranged, melee, area, damage over time, slowing, hazards, traps, or a combination). One tree deepens the Betrayer's verb, **shred**; the other two bend it. Each must be fun in a pure swarm of Blessed and playable solo.
- **7 nodes per tree**, 1 point each, one rank:

  | Tier | Nodes | Opens with distinct strictly lower-tier nodes in the tree (core §9.3) |
  |---|---|---|
  | 1 | 2 | 0 points |
  | 2 | 2 | 1 point |
  | 3 | 2: exactly **one ability node** and one other | 3 points |
  | 4 | 1 capstone | 5 points |

- **One capstone per player** (taking one, learned or as a Rite, locks the other two). The Pearly Gates allows **9 points** (one capstone build of 6 plus 3, or a spread) plus up to **4 Rites** per run; later heavens at most 14 points.
- **Each tree's ability node** teaches one new ability for **Q or E**, replacing a named base ability (Field of Blood or Shadowstep) when assigned. LMB and RMB are never replaced; no new keys.

**What a node may do (game.md §9.3)**
- Change a **visible count or shape** (+1 pierce shown on the tracer, a longer dash streak); **replace** an attack's or ability's effect with a different visible one; or **add one visible effect** to an attack or ability that has only one.
- **Never:** an invisible percentage, a hidden or conditional rider, a "press when ready" buff, a stat node, a multi-rank node.
- Augments declare a **Delivery** channel (count, trajectory, placement or footprint of an existing effect) or a **Payload** channel (adds/replaces one emitted effect). One active augment per channel and resolved named input is allowed; different channels compose in Delivery-then-Payload order, with at most two visible effects. Same-channel alternatives exclude; an ability unlock occupies neither channel. A Rite may substitute for a learned augment only in its own channel, never a capstone/another Rite/explicit exclusion. Passive/movement nodes have no implicit channel exclusions. Explicit target sets below define alternative-ability inheritance; no other node silently inherits. Every six-point capstone path and nine-point extension obeys these rules and the two kill-discharge-node limit.
- Applying a state (game.md §4.11), or one more visible effect on enemies in a state, counts as the node's one visible effect.

**The class's rules (classes.md)**
- **Field of Blood is the only fire-rate multiplier** in the game. A node may change where or how the Field works, but nothing else may raise anyone's fire rate. Judas Coin is the one Q alternative and names the lost party multiplier on its card. The Way Back and Silver Wire replace Shadowstep on E, so the Sicarius and Akeldama alternatives retain Field of Blood for the boss.
- Its weakness stays: 120 HP; it needs Blasphemy, Falling Star and the Heretic to survive the swarm it shoots into. No self-heal, no self-shield.
- No taunt.
- Don't propose again: Kiss of Betrayal; Two Masters (a timed second revolver); Greed (fire rate rising with kills); a double jump; Shadowstep priming a critical shot; Kiss refunding on a kill; Field of Blood doubling damage. **Thirty Pieces of Silver** is reserved for the ultimate (M13).
- Movement abilities run on the client first and the host accepts them (MVP §9.3); say how a new one splits.
- **Bots play the base kit** (game.md §12.4): no tree may be needed for a bot to work.

**Enemy states (game.md §4.11): the bridge to relics and teammates**
- The Betrayer's base kit **applies** `pooled` (enemies within 6 m of its Field of Blood's center); it **reads** Silver Bullet: `bound` Blessed cost half as much.
- **Each tree** has at least one node that makes an attack or ability **apply a state the base kit doesn't**: `slowed`, `bound` (short) or `airborne`; never `taunted`, `silenced` or `censed`.
- **The class** has at least **two nodes that read a state another class's base kit applies**: `taunted`, `airborne` (the Fallen), `censed` (the Heretic), `bound`, `slowed`, `silenced` (the Binder) (e.g. "kills of `censed` enemies by the slug burst into a 2 m ring").
- Fill the `States` column (`applies slowed`, `reads bound`); a relic or a teammate's kit can then be named that pays off on it.

**Rites (game.md §9.10): every node is also a run-only pick**
- A node is offered as a Rite only with its lower-tier gate, exclusions, current ability and usefulness conditions satisfied (game.md §9.10). Its card explains one visible rule; conditional readers require a producible state source. Learned-only gates never borrow temporary Rite points.
- **Source closure:** use game.md §9.9 and items-relics.md §2.1.1's finite producibility closure after resolving channels and substitutions. Seed only effective native kit, actually available carried items, active same-room areas and usable same-room hazards; admit a triggered emitter only when its trigger is reachable from those seeds. An equipped but untriggerable relic, an absent ally, a surrendered payload or an unseeded cycle cannot qualify a state reader. `own` predicates require the owner's producible source, not an ally's flag. Timed/source prerequisites are checked when offering/taking, then ordinary loss of a source makes the held effect dormant without erasing ownership.
- **Temporary substitution:** a Rite augment may suppress one conflicting learned augment in the same Delivery/Payload channel of its implicit named-input group; explicit exclusions, another Rite and capstones are never displaced. Suppressed ownership remains for gates, but only effective nodes produce effects, metadata or passive dispatch. Evaluate usefulness after suppression; show the sacrificed effect and any effects losing their sole state source. Table annotations explaining same-input/same-channel conflicts are implicit-group documentation, not extra `Excludes` prerequisites. No point refund or cooldown/source refresh is granted; the learned effect returns at the run's end.
- An augment of Q or E is offered only while that ability is assigned, so **each tree has at least 2 tier-1/tier-2 nodes on `LMB`, `RMB` or `passive`** to support early offers. These still obey gates, exclusions and state-source usefulness checks.
- An ability node taken as a Rite goes straight into its slot (Q or E) with the replaced ability's remaining cooldown; it must be playable without any of its tree's other nodes.
- Rite choices and valid Confessional loadout edits may commit during a valley or an encounter countdown, with the same living-player, range and room checks. At the actual combat seal, close overlays and reject later commits without discarding their pending offers. This does not widen the valley rules for items, regeneration or teleporting.
- Bots never get Rites; nothing here may be needed for a bot.

**Quantities:** 21 nodes, 3 new abilities, 3 capstones.

**Shared rules for the nodes below** (so each card stays one line):

- **Button kills.** Native equipped ability/weapon damage and its direct shape augmentation retain their root slot (`depth=0`), including a pierced/glanced/coin-split bullet, Shadowstep or Way Back cut. Sparks, clay rings, touch cuts and other passive discharges are secondary (`depth=1`), not button kills and cannot start another hit/kill/damage proc. Judecca frost/Blood Money coins carry secondary source tokens but do not deal damage. Delayed ability damage retains its cast's original slot across assignment swaps. Respect the canonical proc limits in game.md §14.11.
- **Lore rendering.** Field of Blood is a ritual name: the thirty silver pieces cast a pool of blood-red light, with no literal blood or flesh. All kills remain feathers and gold sparks (lore.md).
- **Shadowstep already has two effects** (the dash and the M12 cut), so its nodes only change the cut's shape or replace the cut. Silver Bullet, the revolver and Field of Blood have one effect each, so a node may add one.
- **Durations of states** applied here: `slowed` 1.5 s, `bound` 1 s, `airborne` 0.4 s (Falling Star's arc). A state applied again refreshes (game.md §4.11). The Gatekeeper is never `bound`, `slowed` or `airborne`, and no node targets it with sparks or cuts it by touch.
- **New ability ids:** `judasCoin` (Q), `wayBack` (E), `silverWire` (E). Ability-unlock nodes do not occupy augmentation exclusion groups (§9.3). Learned lower-tier nodes alone must license learned gates; Rites can open higher Rite tiers only. Same-room LOS, accepted floor clipping and source lifetimes follow game.md; no wire, coin, spark or shard reaches another room through a wall. Native enemy-damaging slot metadata: base Q Field **false**, base E Shadowstep **true** (false with Scattered Sheep); Judas Coin Q **false** (split kills remain LMB/RMB), Way Back E **true** (false with Scattered Sheep), Silver Wire E **false**. This metadata drives honest seal-relic offers.
- **Split/ricochet accounting.** A single root weapon shot owns a set of hit entity IDs shared by its coin branches and ricochets. Each enemy takes that shot's native damage once, cannot collect three hits from converging branches, and generates at most one root kill event. A revisited entity consumes no second Silver Bullet damage budget or revolver pierce; the ray continues. Branches inherit only the parent's remaining range, damage budget, bounce allowance and revolver pierce allowance; a split never resets a spent pass. An unspent Halt and the Lame pass can be used once per branch only after that branch kills an already-slowed victim. Delivery is applied to the ray before Payload: Recoil grants one envelope per accepted root, never per coin branch; Nail emits a bind at each terminal spent/enemy/wall point (at most three after one coin split), deduplicating target IDs per root. A bounce is not terminal and cannot nail its wall; max-range exhaustion creates no nail. Blood Money uses only distinct primary revolver victims in its bounded first-tick kill bundle. Resolve branches in center/left/right order and intersections by distance then ID. Sparks use a separate once-per-root victim set; a spark's secondary kill never starts more sparks, frost, coins or clay rings.

**Composition data (game.md §9.3).** All nodes of Kind `augment` appear exactly once here. Passive/capstone rules do not consume either channel; they still obey source predicates, effect rules and discharge limits.

| Channel | Node IDs |
|---|---|
| Delivery | `betrayer.a.1a`, `betrayer.a.2a`, `betrayer.a.3b`, `betrayer.b.2a`, `betrayer.c.1b` |
| Payload | `betrayer.b.1b`, `betrayer.b.3b`, `betrayer.c.1a`, `betrayer.c.2a` |

**Enemy event eligibility.** Trickle kills/hits never dispatch enemy-triggered talent/relic/combo discharges or advance kill meters. Ordinary native attacks, their direct shape/state rules and defensive passives still work. Watchers/Seals never supply kill-listener or hit-count events; authored incidental damage uses the boss-part area modifier. Ordinary boss summons permit their explicitly bounded ordinary combat procs, combat streak credit and Armageddon eligibility, but never Bread/extra passive mote harvest or item/halo/XP rewards. Boss parts and trickle never build deed streaks. World damage supplies the core death entitlement and recent setup assist only, never a fabricated player/button kill. Area-only propagation keeps its explicit core exception.

**Kill-discharge data.** `betrayer.a.1b` Judecca, `betrayer.a.cap` From Hand to Hand, `betrayer.c.2a` Blood Money and `betrayer.c.2b` Broken Vessel are this class's kill-triggered talent discharge nodes. At most **two** are effective in one build, counting learned nodes, Rites and capstones together. A listener still counts when its read state or target is temporarily absent; only actual named-input dormancy or suppression removes its active count. Use real remaining source/phase predicates for offers, not this limit as a dynamic target counter. Each root collects an active node once at that root's **first eligible resolving tick**, bundling all qualifying deaths of that root/tick after native damage; the row's origin/victim caps apply. Later native pulses retain the dispatch flag and cannot re-dispatch that node. Never collect separately per pellet, branch or fatal victim. Eligibility and ordinary damage happen before the secondary bundle; secondary kills cannot seed another bundle. Eligibility still uses per-victim pre-fatal state; the flag and any cooldown remain through death/reassignment/reconnect and the root's delayed lifetime. The Burning Plain/The Sixth Circle's existing area-only propagation exception, native ability damage, Bread's bounded harvest, landing effects, motes and revival listeners are outside this kill-discharge category. There is no banked third proc or retroactive state qualification.

## 6. Playtest-in-head notes

Assumed: the crest's density near the party about 1 Blessed per m²; Blessed strike 5 per 1 s after a 0.25 s wind-up within 1.2 m (`sim.ts:352-357`); base Betrayer about 6.7 kills/s in a good lane.

**A6, The Long Watch** (Judecca, The Halt and the Lame, The Informer, Judas Coin, Glancing Silver or Behind His Back, From Hand to Hand).
- *Swarm, solo:* kite at 9 m/s; frost from each kill slows nearby chasers to 2.8 m/s, so a well-lined revolver can take 2 kills + 2 sparks (ceiling ≈ 13 kills/s); the Silver Bullet wins when a denser lane supplies ten or more targets. Glancing Silver lines run along walls; every 10 s a coin can split a bullet into three lines (up to 60 with sparks if every branch and spark has a distinct victim). Aiming at the coin divides attention while movement remains available; the shared attack timer prevents firing both attacks together, and does not force a stop. If caught, base Shadowstep supplies an escape.
- *Swarm, party:* Behind His Back over Glancing: the Fallen's taunted ring is a 20-deep line. No Field: the party loses ×2 in the swarm, which it barely needs there.
- *Ranged room:* the best tree here: The Informer outlines the Choristers behind cover for everyone, Glancing banks around cover, a coin-split revolver shot drops three Cherubs. Sparks (20) don't kill Choristers (60), as intended.
- *Boss:* Coin cannot multiply hits on the Gatekeeper body; it can spread aimed rays through add groups and separately hittable targets. Field is an optional coordinated body-DPS choice at the Confessional, while Coin remains a viable complete-run loadout. The encounter's required reach and completion gates are alternative-neutral.

**B6, Sicarius** (Through the Throng, Recoil, One of Them, The Way Back, The Long Knife or Scattered Sheep, A Thousand Years Among Them). The six-point paths choose damage width or a launch replacement to fit six points; those nodes do not exclude each other. A seventh B point composes Long Knife Delivery with Scattered Sheep Payload into the wider launch return, while Q Field remains assigned.
- *Swarm, solo:* runs through the crowd at full speed, with its narrow dagger lane yielding an ideal ≈ 5.4 new kills/s at the assumed density; Long Knife dashes may take 30–45 in a packed route, and The Way Back cuts a 20 m column. Recoil fires point-blank and moves out when no other envelope or Held state is active. The dagger's 0.3 m center-path radius leaves enemies between that lane and their 1.2 m melee reach alive, so turning, clipping walls or failing to choose a gap remains dangerous. A straight high-speed pass can avoid some 0.25 s wind-ups, but safety is a playtest question, not guaranteed immunity. **Fix made:** the earlier body-to-body phrase could imply a much wider effective kill radius; the exact narrow path now matches the intended defensive tradeoff.
- *Ranged room:* One of Them: it hides in the Blessed packs while the casters fire at others, then dashes to them. Scattered Sheep is the safer pick if elites are about.
- *Party:* the Fallen's taunted crowd ignores it, so it runs laps through it. Watch in the real playtest whether B6 is too safe solo; the knobs are the reach and the 1 s per-enemy limit.

**C6, Akeldama** (The Nail, Thrown Silver, Blood Money, Broken Vessel, Silver Wire, The Potter's Field; the hazard path substitutes Known Ground for Thrown Silver).
- *Swarm, solo:* during the Field (8 of 30 s) the crowd binds at its edge and every Silver Bullet runs 20 deep and shatters; outside it, the wire holds a row, the nail pins the end of each line, revolver coins bind the next arrival. A held, turret-like style; without Shadowstep it must keep its distance by speed. Fun, and the strongest tree on a stacked crowd. **Fixes made:** Broken Vessel's ring cut from 2 m to 1.5 m (a 2 m ring along 20 bound bodies cleared a 4 m strip), and the wire lasts 6 s, not 8 (at 8 of every 10 s it was always up, so its placement stopped being a decision).
- *Ranged room:* the weakest tree here: the wire and coins don't touch flyers, Choristers stay back. The base revolver does the work; Known Ground pays only in hazard rooms.
- *Party:* the Field becomes a ×4 kill zone (×2 fire, bound double) at its rim; the Fallen landing on the wire's row kills it whole.

**3 + 3 + 3** (A Judecca, Informer, Glancing Silver; B Through the Throng, One of Them, The Long Knife; C The Nail, Thrown Silver, Broken Vessel). Keeps Field (now thrown) and Shadowstep (wide). Frost, endpoint binds and clay rings in the swarm, wide dashes, walking through crowds; Field thrown onto the Binder's pile from 25 m. Glancing Delivery plus Nail Payload produces a banked line that pins its endpoint. Judecca and Broken Vessel are the two effective kill discharges; adding Blood Money is illegal until one is removed. No new ability or capstone is required.

**Base kit + 2 random Rites.** Tier-1 offers on a fresh character: Glancing Silver, Judecca, Through the Throng, Recoil, The Nail, Thrown Silver. Every one reads in a line and works alone (none reads a teammate's state). E.g. Judecca + The Nail: frost along the line and a pinned knot at its end, solid. Recoil Payload + Glancing Delivery can both come; the accepted shot banks once and its one validated recoil moves the shooter. Recoil + Nail cannot coexist, because both occupy RMB Payload. The Halt and the Lame requires a producible slow and its tier-2 gate; Behind His Back requires a present Blasphemy and its tier-3 gate. Unsupported readers are filtered from the offer.

**Fixes made before the tables above:** a "silver wake" Shadowstep node was dropped (Shadowstep already has two effects, so an extra slow would have been a third); the slow source moved to Judecca at tier 1 so The Halt and the Lame isn't dead solo; The Halt and the Lame passes through once, not three times (three made the revolver beat the Silver Bullet in any crowd); Behind His Back sits in A's excluding pair so a solo A6 can skip it.

**Resolved interaction rules.** Direct/passive origin and bounded victim sets allow frost, sparks and clay from one primary action without a recursive damage chain. State-reader Rite offers require a producible source; Behind His Back without a present Blasphemy is not an eligible card. Lower-tier gates prevent self-supporting learned allocations; the sequences above use only learned prerequisite points. The nine-point extensions are A6+B `1a→2b→2a`, B6+A `1b→2a→2b`, C6+A `1a→2b`+B `1a`. C6 takes Glancing Silver rather than Judecca to keep Blood Money/Broken Vessel within the two-discharge limit. C6 can now include thrown Field without excluding its capstone.

Judas Coin is the only new Q alternative giving up Field's party fire-rate benefit, and its card shows that loss. Way Back on E keeps Field while trading the instant Shadowstep for a prepared return. No other effect supplies fire rate. Known Ground applies to authored environmental hazards/prop blasts only, never enemy pools, boss rings or Judgment. One of Them changes only ordinary ranged-enemy targeting while physically surrounded; it never forces a target onto someone else and fails if no qualifying crowd remains. Potter's Field creates a visible one-second bind per entrant, never multiplies Field's actual attack damage.

**Outstanding playtest questions:** Can a Sicarius safely ignore melee wind-up too often while moving? Does aiming at a coin interrupt movement or simply add a rewarding shot? Does losing Shadowstep for a longitudinal wire create enough defensive choices? These require observed play. No tests were run or created for this specification pass.

### 6.1 Collected veteran: fixed talents still allow a different attempt

In the Akeldama nine-node build, learned Nail occupies RMB Payload, Blood Money LMB Payload, A Glancing/Informer and B Through the Throng are held, and Q Field/E Wire are assigned. Glancing Delivery already banks the line while retaining its endpoint nail. With an available slow item or a teammate's effective slow source, **The Halt and the Lame** is an eligible LMB Delivery Rite beside Blood Money; both direct revolver victims can seed coins in its one root bundle. A then owns three lower nodes (Glancing, Informer, Halt), licensing **Judas Coin** as a later ability Rite. Taking it exchanges Field's party cadence and Potter's active binding for three aimed lines. The card names that cost; Wire and Blood Money still provide solo binds, while every E alternative retains Field. Without a reachable slow source Halt is filtered, and this branch is unavailable; Judecca cannot be used as a third discharge beside Blood Money/Broken Vessel.

Alternatively, **Recoil** substitutes for the Nail; B's learned Through the Throng opens **One of Them** at tier 2. Those three B lower nodes then license **The Way Back** as the third Rite, exchanging the existing E Wire for a prepared return escape while retaining Field/capstone binding on Q. Recoil cannot displace another Rite, stack a movement envelope or evade a Held Seal. The affected slot's remaining cooldown and any outgoing action cleanup follow the same substitution/assignment transaction.

The A and B nine-node builds likewise permit compatible RMB Delivery/Payload additions or same-channel substitutions, and Blood Money/Halt composition where their tier, actual slow source and kill-discharge cap allow. A state supplied only by a suppressed node never licenses its replacement reader. The shared root victim set, secondary sparks/clay, two effective kill-discharge nodes and at-most-four Rites still bound the resulting build. These scenarios establish plausible alternative decisions after relic exhaustion, not a claim that every random offer creates an equally strong build.

### 6.2 Composed and alternative kits

**Bank and nail:** Glancing Silver Delivery + The Nail Payload is immediately legal with one tier-1 point in A and C. The native 200-damage ray reflects once with the remaining range/budget; at its terminal spent hit or wall, the single Payload binds surviving ground targets. A ricochet wall is not also a terminal nail. With Judas Coin, the three branches share native hit IDs, each retains only an unspent bounce and each has one terminal nail, with acquired bind targets deduplicated across the root. This creates a positioning choice rather than three hits on one boss.

**Wide return with Field:** B Through the Throng, Recoil and Long Knife meet the three-lower-node gate for Way Back. With E Way Back and Q Field, the return cuts a 1.5 m side band while the party's boss cadence remains available. Adding Scattered Sheep at tier 3 replaces that same cut with a 1.5 m launch footprint; the native E damage metadata becomes false. The return still requires a previously placed five-second marker and retains its cancellation/cooldown limits. Q Coin + E Way Back is also legal, but only the optional Q substitution surrenders Field.

**Bounded kill combinations:** Judecca + From Hand to Hand is a legal marksman pair. Blood Money + Broken Vessel is a legal saboteur pair. Judecca + Broken Vessel can form the generalist pair, using Nail/Field/Wire or an effective allied bind; adding Blood Money to that pair is refused. Halt and the Lame is native conditional Delivery, so it does not consume a discharge channel, and cannot use Judecca's post-root frost retroactively. None of these pairings authorizes a third talent proc merely because a coin branch killed more enemies.
