# Builds, items and persistence: talent trees, relics, consumables, shops, saved characters

Scope: how action games and roguelites design talent trees, abilities granted by talents, persistent relics, consumable inventories, shops with a shared currency, progression for mixed-progress co-op groups, and browser save systems. For each mechanism there is an inference on how it maps to Meatgrinder (docs/rogue/brief.md, docs/classes.md) and whether it conflicts with the class design rules: one verb per class; LMB/RMB each better in some situation; every ability rewards timing (no "press the moment it's ready" buffs); one visible effect per ability, no hidden or conditional riders; every class fun in a pure swarm; power from doing the class's job, not stats.

Source note: Fandom wikis (wowpedia, Isaac fandom) returned HTTP 402 and were not readable; wiki.gg mirrors were used instead. Many numbers come from fan wikis and guides, not developer statements, and are flagged where sources disagree.

## 1. Talent trees: structure, points, what makes a talent fun, respec

### Takeaway
The classic three-tree model (WoW Classic, Borderlands) gives identity and a "capstone" goal but fills up with +X% filler that players solve into one cookie-cutter build; Blizzard called it "fundamentally flawed and unfixable" in 2011, then brought trees back in 2022 with fewer, chunkier nodes and choice nodes. Modern co-op games (Darktide Patch 13, BL3) converge on: three branches per class, a budget too small to fill everything (roughly 1.5–2.7 full branches), each node "meaningful and impactful", and free or cheap respec to encourage experimenting.

### Cited Findings

**WoW Classic / TBC / Cataclysm (three trees, points per level)**
- Classic: first talent point at level 10, one per level to 60 = 51 points; the first 31-point talent is reachable at level 40 (31 points in one tree). — [Blizzard Watch / wowhead classic overview via search](https://blizzardwatch.com/2019/06/19/talents-talent-trees-wow-classic/); [Wowhead Classic talent overview](https://wowhead.com/classic/cn/guide/classic-wow-classes-and-talent-overview)
- Each class had three trees, points could go into any tree, tiers unlocked after 5 points in the tier below, and most talents had several ranks (often 5 points into one talent for its full effect). — [Warcraft Wiki: Talent](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Talent)
- Classic respec at a class trainer: 1 g, then 5 g, rising per reset to a 50 g cap; the fee falls 5 g per month without a respec. (One Blizzard Watch article says the first respec is 1 silver; most sources say 1 gold.) — [Blizzard Watch](https://blizzardwatch.com/2019/06/19/talents-talent-trees-wow-classic/); [Blizzard forums](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/how-to-change-talents-in-classic/267545)
- Dual Talent Specialization cost 1000 g when introduced in patch 3.1.0; Cataclysm lowered it to 100 g at level 30. — [Warcraft Wiki: Talent](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Talent)
- Cataclysm shrank trees from 11 rows to 7 and points from 71 to 41, and forced 31 points into the primary tree before the others unlocked (the last 10 anywhere). (The same page also mentions 76 points elsewhere, an internal inconsistency.) — [Warcraft Wiki: Talent](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Talent)

**Why Blizzard removed the trees (Mists of Pandaria, 2011–2012)**
- Ghostcrawler (Greg Street): many picks were trivial (a 10% vs 5% damage talent); choosing among talents irrelevant to your spec was "a multiple choice test, and an easy one at that"; trees had "a ton of inconsequential talents" before the good ones; "40-50 fun, interesting and balanced talents in a tree" was not achievable; many combinations made "unbeatable synergies" likely; one point per level "works less well" at 90 levels; tiers offered a clear damage/tank/heal pick so hybrids had to give up one role. — [Engadget: Ghostcrawler on the MoP talent philosophy](https://www.engadget.com/2011-12-08-ghostcrawler-on-seeing-the-forest-for-the-talent-trees.html)
- "We tried the talent tree model for seven years. We think it's fundamentally flawed and unfixable." The new model: "fewer choices. But you will have more choices that matter." — [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/uk/blizzard-tackle-world-of-warcraft-talent-revamp-criticisms-say-old-model-is-unfixable); [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2011-12-08-ghostcrawler-on-seeing-the-forest-for-the-talent-trees.html)
- MoP model: six tiers of three talents, one pick per tier, unlocking at levels 15, 30, 45, 60, 75, 90; core rotation abilities (e.g. Raging Blow) granted automatically instead of costing a point; goal of "18 fun talents" per class; one of Bladestorm/Shockwave/Avatar per tier so synergies stay limited. — [Warcraft Wiki: Talent](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Talent); [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2011-12-08-ghostcrawler-on-seeing-the-forest-for-the-talent-trees.html)

**Dragonflight (2022): trees return**
- Two trees per character, class tree (utility, shared across specs) and spec tree (power in the role); first class point at level 10, first spec point at 11, alternating; at level 70, 31 class points + 30 spec points. Blizzard: "it would probably be a mistake if all 61 points were always heavily in play". Some basic abilities are granted free by spec. — [Blizzard: Dragonflight Talent Preview](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/23797209); [Warcraft Tavern summary](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/wow/news/world-of-warcraft-dragonflight-talent-preview/)
- Node shapes signal type: square = active ability, round = passive, octagon = choice node (pick one of two). Builds can be saved and switched. — [Wowhead: Dragonflight talent trees](https://wowhead.com/cn/guide/classes/dragonflight-talent-trees)
- Row gates are 8 points spent (row 5) and 20 (row 8) instead of filling every tier. — [Warcraft Wiki: Talent](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Talent)

**Borderlands 2 / 3**
- BL2: one skill point per level from level 5 (46 at level 50; level cap 72 with both Ultimate Vault Hunter packs, ~68 points). Tiers need more points per tier; a calculator lists 5/10/15 for tiers 2–4 and 26 points to a capstone (low-quality source). — [Borderlands Wiki: Experience Points via search](https://borderlands.fandom.com/wiki/Experience_Points); [co-optimus: level cap 72](https://co-optimus.com/article/10733/borderlands-2-dlc-beefs-the-level-cap-to-72.html); [west-games BL2 calculator](https://west-games.com/borderlands-2-skill-tree-calculator/)
- BL3: three trees per Vault Hunter, each tree begins with its own action skill and the player equips one at a time; trees unlock passives, action-skill augments and new action skills; each ends in a capstone (e.g. Amara's Avatar: use the action skill once while on cooldown). — [Windows Central: BL3 skill trees](https://windowscentral.com/borderlands-3-every-skill-tree-and-how-they-work); [PC Gamer: BL3 skill trees](https://www.pcgamer.com/borderlands-3-skill-trees/)
- BL3 at level 72 = 70 skill points; maxing one tree takes 26 points, so a player gets two capstones plus change. Cap rises to 53 and 57 were sold on "three more points" and "two more capstones". — [progameguides](https://progameguides.com/borderlands/what-is-the-max-level-in-borderlands-3/); [mentalmars](https://mentalmars.com/game-news/what-is-the-max-level-cap-in-borderlands-3/)
- BL3/BL4 respec (Quick Change station) costs a percentage of carried money; BL4 about 10%. — [PowerPyx](https://powerpyx.com/borderlands-3-how-to-reset-skill-points); [esports.gg BL4](https://esports.gg/guides/gaming/how-to-respec-skill-trees-in-borderlands-4)

**Diablo 2 / 3 / 4**
- Jay Wilson (D3) on dropping skill points: skill points "are really commitments before you even know what you're committing to"; a finite limit on how many skills you take forces the meaningful choice; D2 "seemed like a lot of opportunities to make mistakes, and there's no respec". — [Blizzplanet: IGN on ditching skill points](https://www.blizzplanet.com/blog/comments/ign-blizzard-on-ditching-skill-points-in-diablo-iii)
- D3 (Sirlin's analysis): exactly 6 ability slots from ~24 abilities per class, 5 runes per ability (one active) that often change how the skill plays; free swaps outside combat with a 3 s cooldown; low switching cost encourages exploration, high costs (D2, WoW) pushed players to cookie-cutter builds; "over 29 BILLION possible builds per class". — [Game Developer: Diablo 3's ability system](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/diablo-3-s-ability-system)
- D2: respec was added only in v1.13 (2010): one free respec from Akara per difficulty (3 total) plus a craftable Token of Absolution from four Hell-only boss essences. — [Diablo Wiki: Respec](https://diablo2.diablowiki.net/Respec); [Warcraft Tavern D2](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/d2/how-to-respec/)
- D4: six active skill slots unlocked at levels 2, 4, 8; respec free early (sources say to level 15; beta said 7), then per-point gold cost rising with level (1 g at 7, 222 g at 25, tens of thousands per point near 70); Blizzard said the costs "are final". — [Prima Games: D4 slots](https://primagames.com/tips/how-to-unlock-hot-bar-skill-slots-diablo-4); [GamesRadar: D4 respec](https://www.gamesradar.com/diablo-4-respec-how-to); [Blizzard forum thread](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/d4/t/blizzard-says-diablo-4s-respec-costs-are-final/15938)

**Path of Exile, Grim Dawn, Torchlight 2**
- PoE passive tree: "over 1,300 nodes" (guide figure, no official count); up to 123 passive points; every point spent travelling away from your start is a point not spent on power; the official wiki says configurations are effectively infinite. — [Shacknews PoE guide](https://pkm.shacknews.com/path-of-exile); [PoE Wiki: Passive skill](https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Passive); [ExitLag guide](https://www.exitlag.com/blog/path-of-exile-skill-tree/)
- Grim Dawn: one mastery at level 2, a second at 10 (dual class, permanent); 26+ skills per mastery; 3 skill points per level to 50, then 2, then 1 after 90 (community figure); Devotion is a separate constellation tree fed by shrines; respec at the Spirit Guide starts cheap (25 Iron Bits) and rises with points changed. — [Grim Dawn feature list](https://www.grimdawn.com/guide/about/major-features); [Crate forum: New Player Compendium](https://forums.crateentertainment.com/t/new-player-knowledge-compendium/40322)
- Torchlight 2: respec only the last three points spent, for gold; players called it "atrocious" and used respec-potion mods. — [GameBanshee: TL2 post-beta changes](https://www.gamebanshee.com/news/108571-torchlight-ii-post-beta-changes-and-updates.html); [Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/200710/discussions/0/517142253863761232)

**Hades Mirror of Night (meta talents in a short-run roguelite)**
- Talents bought with Darkness at the Mirror in the hub; per-rank costs rise steeply (Shadow Presence 5 ranks 10/15/20/25/30; Death Defiance 3 ranks 30/500/1000); full red + green set ~35,365 Darkness. — [Prima Games: Mirror of Night](https://primagames.com/gaming/hades-guide-mirror-of-night-upgrades-fated-list-of-prophecies); [Hades wiki mirror: Darkness](https://breezewiki.discard.no/hades/wiki/Darkness)
- Each Mirror slot has two mutually exclusive alternatives (red vs green), swapped freely at the Mirror; each is bought separately; alternatives unlocked with Chthonic Keys; a full reset/refund costs 1 Chthonic Key. — [Prima Games](https://primagames.com/gaming/hades-guide-mirror-of-night-upgrades-fated-list-of-prophecies); [Hades wiki: Chthonic Key](https://hades.fandom.com/wiki/Chthonic_Key)

**Darktide Patch 13 (Oct 2023, co-op horde game)**
- Each class got a tree with three main branches (one = the old kit, two new), 30 points total, so specialising in one branch leaves points unspent; spending/removing points is free "to encourage greater experimentation"; trees are "domains" (Veteran "anything from a sharpshooter to a squad leader to more commando"); producer: in co-op, options multiply "by the synergies you create with other players". — [Shacknews: Patch 13](https://www.shacknews.com/article/137295/warhammer-40k-darktide-patch-13); [Level Infinite deep dive](https://www.levelinfinite.com/news/deep-dive-into-warhammer-40000-darktide-patch-13/); [GameSpot](https://gamespot.com/articles/warhammer-40000-darktide-getting-revamped-class-system-with-skill-trees-and-new-abilities/1100-6517003/)
- Later (Arbites dev blog, June 2025): they "purposely aimed to not overextend with the amount of nodes" and focused on "making each node meaningful and impactful". — [Darktide Arbites talent tree dev blog](https://darktide.gameslantern.com/news/introducing-dev-blog-arbites-talent-tree)

### Inferences
- **Budget ratio.** Total points ÷ points to fill one tree: WoW Classic 51/31 ≈ 1.6, TBC (61 points, 41-point capstone, standard WoW knowledge, not re-sourced here) ≈ 1.5, BL3 70/26 ≈ 2.7, Darktide "can't finish a branch". A ratio of ~1.5 forces a main tree plus a dip; ~2.7 lets hybrids take two capstones. For Meatgrinder (short runs, 3 trees × 4 classes), a cap around 1.5 trees keeps a clear identity per build (one capstone + a dip), which matches "three playstyles per class".
- **Small trees.** MoP's 18 talents per class and Darktide's "don't overextend" argue for small trees. Proposal for the spec: each tree 4 tiers × 2–3 nodes, tier gate every 3 points, capstone after ~9 points in the tree; lifetime cap ~14 points. This is ~25–30 nodes per class (×4 classes = ~100–120 nodes to design and balance), already a big content job.
- **No +X% nodes.** Ghostcrawler's 5%-vs-10% complaint and the class rule "power from doing the class's job, not stats" point the same way: every node must change a verb (a new ability, a new shape for an attack, a new visible interaction like "Falling Star landing leaves enemies bound"). Multi-rank "+4% per rank" nodes conflict with the design rules (invisible, no decision) and should be out of scope.
- **Choice nodes (Dragonflight octagons) fit the rule "LMB/RMB each situational".** Present two mutually exclusive ways to play a node (like Hades red/green), never "more vs. less".
- **Hybrid danger:** Ghostcrawler's "unbeatable synergies" risk grows when two capstones combine; keep capstones from multiplying each other (e.g. one capstone per player, enforced).
- **Respec:** the brief already limits respec to the leveling room. Darktide and D3 show free respec drives experimentation; WoW/D4 cost-based respec mostly creates frustration. Recommend free respec at the leveling room (the trip there is the cost), no gold or halo price.
- **XP from healing:** the brief awards XP for kills and healing. Not researched; risk is that the Heretic either outlevels (healing ticks every 0.5 s) or lags. Recommend party-shared XP (everyone gets each kill's XP), which also removes kill-stealing.

### Gaps
- No primary source for BL2/BL3 exact tier gates (5 points per tier) or the BL2 respec price; only calculator sites.
- No developer talk found on how many talent points suit a short-run game; the numbers above are cross-game ratios.
- TBC 41-point talents and 61 points at 70 are from general knowledge, not re-verified in this session.

## 2. Abilities granted by talents and keeping the button count small

### Takeaway
Games that add abilities through talents keep the number of active buttons fixed and make the talent change *which* ability sits in a slot (BL3 one action skill, D3 6 slots, D4 6 slots, Guild Wars 8 skills), or change the ability's behaviour in place (BL3 augments, D3 runes). Meatgrinder already has four buttons (LMB, RMB, Q, E); talents should swap or reshape Q/E rather than add keys.

### Cited Findings
- BL3: each tree starts with an action skill, but only one is equipped at a time; later tiers add augments that modify it (e.g. Blight Tiger adds toxic damage to Phaseslam; Zane's SNTNL gets rocket or missile augments); augments cost no skill points once unlocked and several can run at once. — [Windows Central](https://windowscentral.com/borderlands-3-every-skill-tree-and-how-they-work); [GameWith BL3](https://gamewith.net/borderlands-3/article/show/11106)
- BL3 anointments (gear rolls) mostly trigger on action-skill use; a patch stopped them triggering on "missed activations" (Fakegrasp, Gamma Burst at the sky) because players were firing skills at nothing to proc them. — [The Sixth Axis: BL3 November patch](https://www.thesixthaxis.com/?p=368830); [Windows Central: anointed gear](https://windowscentral.com/how-get-anointed-gear-and-what-it-does-borderlands-3)
- D3: 6 slots, ~24 abilities per class, runes "often completely changing the gameplay of each skill"; swaps free outside combat with a 3 s cooldown. — [Game Developer: D3 ability system](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/diablo-3-s-ability-system); [Shacknews: D3 rune system](https://www.shacknews.com/article/55258/diablo-3-lead-designer-reveals)
- D4: 6 skill slots, unlocked at levels 2, 4, 8 (a forum thread called the 6-slot limit "a dealbreaker"). — [Prima Games](https://primagames.com/tips/how-to-unlock-hot-bar-skill-slots-diablo-4); [Blizzard forums](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/d4/t/6-skill-slot-limit-might-be-a-dealbreaker/13318)
- Guild Wars 1: a bar of at most 8 skills, at most one elite; not every skill can be brought, so players choose which work together; GW2 instead ties half the bar to the weapon, with staggered unlocks to limit newcomer confusion. — [Guild Wars Wiki: Skill](https://wiki.guildwars.com/wiki/Skill); [GameBanshee GW2 interview](https://gamebanshee.com/wphd)
- MoP: core abilities granted automatically by level/spec, talents reserved for choices. — [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2011-12-08-ghostcrawler-on-seeing-the-forest-for-the-talent-trees.html)
- Darktide Patch 13: new branches "feature new abilities" (blitz, aura, combat ability chosen in the tree, one active per slot). — [GameSpot](https://gamespot.com/articles/warhammer-40000-darktide-getting-revamped-class-system-with-skill-trees-and-new-abilities/1100-6517003/)

### Inferences
- **Slot model for Meatgrinder:** keep LMB, RMB, Q, E. Each tree offers one alternative Q or E (for example a Fallen "charge" as an E alternative to Falling Star; classes.md already reserves "a charge may return as a new ability later"). At the leveling room the player assigns one ability per slot, like BL3's one action skill. Three trees × (Q or E) gives each class up to 3 new abilities + 2 base = 5 abilities, of which 2 are equipped.
- **Augments vs. "one effect per ability":** BL3 augments stack hidden riders on one skill, which conflicts with "no hidden riders". Allowed form: an augment that *replaces* the ability's single effect with a different visible one (D3 rune style: Chains of Tartarus that pulls into a line instead of a pile). Stacking several augments at once (BL3) should be out of scope.
- **Anointment lesson:** any relic/talent that triggers "on ability use" invites firing the ability at nothing; trigger on visible results instead ("when Silver Bullet kills 5"), or not at all. "On use" buffs also conflict with "no press-when-ready".
- **Secondary attack alternatives** (talent swaps RMB) are possible but risk breaking the carefully paired LMB/RMB situational split; recommend Q/E swaps only in the first spec.

### Gaps
- No source on how BL3 handles UI clutter with multiple augments; no data on how many abilities players actually use in co-op shooters.

## 3. Persistent relics/artifacts that combine (two equipped, combos)

### Takeaway
Roguelites split "stuff that persists" into (a) unlocks that add variety to the pool (Isaac, Codex of Power as a collection) and (b) a small number of equipped pre-run modifiers (Hades Keepsakes 1 slot, Dead Cells mutations 3 slots). Combos are made legible by naming them: Hades' Duo Boons are separate, named rewards that appear only when two prerequisite gods' boons are held. Stacking systems like RoR2 use hyperbolic stacking to prevent runaway power.

### Cited Findings
- **Hades Duo Boons:** combine two gods' powers; require specific boons from both (e.g. Sea Storm needs particular Zeus and Poseidon boons); among the strongest boons; never guaranteed even when requirements are met; builds in guides are organised around one Duo. — [Prima Games: Duo Boons](https://primagames.com/tips/hades-what-are-duo-boons-how-get-duo-boons); [Digital Trends: Hades builds](https://www.digitaltrends.com/gaming/hades-best-builds/)
- **Hades Keepsakes:** one equipped; a god's keepsake raises the odds of meeting that god and higher ranks raise Duo odds; in Hades 1 each keepsake upgrades twice; mid-run swapping only after a boss (via a House Contractor upgrade); in Hades II, swaps only between regions, a swapped-out keepsake can't be re-equipped that run, rank 2 after 25 encounters and rank 3 after 50. — [Prima Games: Keepsake list](https://primagames.com/tips/hades-keepsake-list); [Dot Esports](https://dotesports.com/hades/news/hades-all-keepsakes-nectar-rewards-and-how-to-get-them); [Rogue Ranker H2](https://rogueranker.com/hades-2-keepsakes/)
- **Dead Cells mutations:** one more mutation per cleared area, max 3 equipped; reset costs gold starting at 1,000, doubling per reset to a cap of 8,000; mutations unlocked permanently by bringing blueprints to the Collector and paying cells. — [Dead Cells wiki: Mutation](https://deadcells.wiki.gg/wiki/Mutation)
- **Diablo 4 Codex of Power:** aspects become account-wide once in the Codex; imprinting costs gold and materials; Codex versions are always the weakest roll, while drops can roll higher; seasonal realms reset it. — [Icy Veins: Codex guide](https://www.icy-veins.com/d4/guides/legendary-aspects-codex-of-power-guide/); [Maxroll](https://maxroll.gg/d4/getting-started/powering-up-in-diablo-4)
- **Isaac:** McMillen designed items so "your character would have some kind of physical growth" (visible changes on the sprite), and drops reward playing well; his aim was a game that seems "to have a mind of its own". (A claim that synergies were "mostly manually coded" is unsourced.) Isaac's meta progression unlocks variants, not power. — [Game Developer: Isaac postmortem](https://gamedeveloper.com/business/postmortem-mcmillen-and-himsl-s-i-the-binding-of-isaac-i-); [Steam discussion on meta progression](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1145350/discussions/0/4358999171576511867)
- **RoR2 stacking:** 232 items + 46 equipment by tier (36 white, 42 green, 36 red, 25 yellow boss, 20 lunar, 14 void…); stack types linear, hyperbolic, exponential; Tougher Times 15% per stack is hyperbolic (≈47% at 6 stacks, not 90%); Shaped Glass doubles damage per stack; players note linear capped items "feel weak" and higher tiers are less meant to be stacked. — [RoR2 wiki: Items](https://riskofrain2.wiki.gg/wiki/Items); [Steam: stacking analysis](https://steamcommunity.com/app/632360/discussions/0/599658601187019562/)
- **Slay the Spire:** 100+ items; PC Gamer: relics can matter more than cards, e.g. Unceasing Top with discard cards creates a loop build. — [Mega Crit press kit](https://megacrit.com/press-kits/slay-the-spire/); [PC Gamer: Best Design 2019](https://www.pcgamer.com/uk/best-design-2019-slay-the-spire)
- **BL3 anointments:** a very low chance roll on any rarity, mostly tied to action skills; bought for Eridium at a vending machine; 14 damage anointments later added "for all skill trees". — [Windows Central](https://windowscentral.com/how-get-anointed-gear-and-what-it-does-borderlands-3)

### Inferences
- **Two slots is in the genre's range** (Hades 1, Dead Cells 3). The combo should be a *named third thing* like a Duo Boon: when relics A and B are both equipped, a small "combo" badge with its own name appears in the loadout and on the HUD, and the combined effect is one visible effect. This answers "make combos readable" and avoids hidden riders.
- **Avoid one dominant combo:** (1) authored pairs only (Hades style), not every relic × every relic, so balance is per-pair; (2) combos key off a class verb (e.g. "Binder pile", "Silver Bullet line"), so the best pair differs per class and per tree; (3) no multiplicative stacking (RoR2 Shaped Glass shows how doubling runs away); relics don't stack with copies.
- **Account-bound power risk:** relics persist across characters, so a veteran's relics are permanent power. Codex-of-Power's "account-wide collection, weakest version" and Isaac's "unlock variety, not power" suggest relics be *sidegrades* that change how an attack or ability behaves (e.g. "Shotgun pellets that kill a Blessed pass through it": visible, one effect), with a drawback where possible.
- **Conflict check:** relics like "+10% fire rate" conflict with "power from the class job" and "Field of Blood is the fire-rate multiplier". Relics that add an "on cooldown ready" trigger conflict with "no press-when-ready". Relics that are class-agnostic must still make sense for all four classes; better: each relic names its effect per class or only references LMB/RMB/Q/E generically ("your E leaves a gold ring for 3 s that slows").
- **Swap rule:** Hades II "can't swap back" and Dead Cells' escalating reset show swap friction; the brief's "reallocate only at the leveling room or lobby" already supplies it. Free swaps there.
- **Chest pacing:** the brief has one treasure room per dungeon, so ~1 relic per dungeon. With a pool of e.g. 24 relics, a collection takes 24 dungeons; dupes should convert to halos or something visible.

### Gaps
- No Supergiant or Hopoo primary source (GDC talk) on duo-boon design or item stacking intent was found; only guides and player analysis.
- No source on how many authored pairs Hades has (Duo count) because the Hades fandom/breezewiki pages were blocked.

## 4. Consumable items and inventory (2 slots, stacking, use-on-pickup)

### Takeaway
Shooters keep consumables scarce and slotted by type (L4D one throwable, one med, one pill/adrenaline; Vermintide one per type), so each use is a decision. Isaac's pills are the best model for "try it to learn it": effects are randomized per run, unidentified until used, and once used every pill of that colour is known. Equipment in RoR2 is one slot with a long cooldown; Helldivers 2 stratagems are four slots with cooldowns of minutes.

### Cited Findings
- **Isaac pills:** 50 pill effects (24 good, 14 neutral, 12 negative per the wiki's list); 13 effects in a run's pool (9 before Afterbirth); 13 colours; effect-to-colour mapping is random per run; unidentified ("???") until used, then all pills of that colour are identified; expected per run ≈5.84 positive, 4.15 negative, 3.01 neutral; pills share the pocket slot with cards and runes; horse pills (1.43% of random pills) are stronger versions. — [Isaac wiki: Pills](https://bindingofisaacrebirth.wiki.gg/wiki/Pills)
- **Isaac co-op (Repentance+ True Co-Op):** coins, bombs and keys are one shared pool; health, items, trinkets and cards/pills are per player; boss rooms drop an extra item per extra player. — [Isaac wiki: Repentance Co-Op](https://bindingofisaacrebirth.wiki.gg/wiki/Repentance_Co-Op)
- **Left 4 Dead:** players argue the few slots make each item matter (adrenaline held "for emergencies"), common loadout 1 defib + 3 medkits; pills heal to a lower cap than kits (community testing). Community sources only; no Valve design statement found. — [Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/550/discussions/0/3005549744930146955); [Steam workshop discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/discussion/3452710213/598522590404821420)
- **Vermintide 2:** each consumable type has its own slot, one of each; Healing Draught heals 75 HP and can be given to someone; Medical Supplies heal 80% of missing health; potions: Strength (damage + armour pierce), Speed (move + attack speed), Concentration (career ability recharge). Durations not found. — [Ludo.guide: Vermintide 2 potions](https://www.ludo.guide/guide/warhammer-vermintide-2/items-equipment/consumables-potions); [MagicGameWorld healing items](https://www.magicgameworld.com/?p=17158)
- **RoR2 equipment:** 46 equipment items; Fuel Cell adds +1 equipment charge per stack and cuts equipment cooldown 15% per stack (cooldowns per item not retrieved). — [RoR2 wiki: Items](https://riskofrain2.wiki.gg/wiki/Items)
- **Helldivers 2:** support weapons take one of four stratagem slots; Machine Gun and Recoilless Rifle unlimited uses with 480 s cooldown; some stratagems have limited uses per mission; backpack slot competes with ammo packs (Quasar frees it). — [EIP Gaming: stratagems](https://eip.gg/guides/helldivers-2-stratagems/); [TechRaptor: stratagem costs](https://techraptor.net/gaming/guides/helldivers-2-stratagems-guide-every-strategem-cost)
- **Diablo-style potions:** not researched in this session (gap).

### Inferences
- **Slots:** the brief's 2 stacking slots fits the genre (L4D/Vermintide 3–4 single slots by type; Isaac 1 pocket slot). Recommend 2 slots, each holds one item type, stack cap 3, one key each (e.g. 1 and 2, or mouse wheel + one key) — note this adds 1–2 buttons to the 4 already used.
- **Use-on-pickup = Isaac identification.** Picking up a dropped item applies it immediately, which teaches what it does (like taking an unidentified pill), and makes the shop purchase an informed choice. Recommend: the first time a player sees an item effect, show its name and one-line description as a toast; the shop shows "tried" vs "untried".
- **"If not already active"** (brief): a pickup while the same effect runs should still do something visible (e.g. go to the inventory if a slot has room, or refresh duration) so pickups never feel wasted.
- **Conflict: heal and shield items vs. the Heretic.** classes.md: "healing and shielding stay separate" and the Heretic's job is sustain. A heal potion every player carries undercuts the Heretic's verb, exactly the healer problem L4D avoids by having no healer class. Keep heal items small (e.g. 40 HP, less than one Communion at 120) and rare, and do not make a shield item that looks like Martyr's Shroud (or make it a different effect, e.g. "next hit ignored").
- **Conflict: timed buffs vs. "no press-when-ready".** A speed or damage potion is a buff, but scarcity (stack cap 3, cost in halos) makes timing the decision, unlike a cooldown ability. Still prefer consumables with a place or target (a thrown venom flask that leaves a pool, a reflect ward placed on the ground) over self-buffs, because they reward *where* as well as *when*, matching the ability rule.
- **"Fire twice as fast" belongs to Field of Blood.** A fire-rate item would duplicate the Betrayer's role; avoid.
- **Fun item effects that fit the swarm and the rules (one visible effect each):** venom flask (pool, DoT), holy water/frost (slow pool), reflect ward (projectiles bounce back, aimed at the ranged-enemy problem from playtest 5), speed (escape when caught), small heal, map reveal (brief), key. Negative/neutral "pills" (Isaac) would add risk but likely annoy in co-op; out of scope initially.

### Gaps
- No Valve developer commentary found on L4D item slot design.
- No Vermintide potion durations or Diablo potion cooldown/charge numbers.
- No RoR2 per-equipment cooldowns retrieved.

## 5. Shops and shared currency in co-op

### Takeaway
Co-op games mostly pool or auto-share currency to avoid stealing and keep players equal: Isaac's True Co-Op shares one wallet, Killing Floor 2 auto-shares dosh server-wide ("consumer friendly ... equal playing field"), Deep Rock Galactic spends a team Nitra pool for resupply. RoR2 is the counterexample people complain about (gold split, items grabbed by whoever is fastest; popular ShareSuite mods). Isaac anchors price ratios: collectible items 15, pickups 5, hearts 3.

### Cited Findings
- **Isaac shop prices:** red heart 3; soul heart, bomb, key, battery, pill, card 5; grab bag 7; some active items 10 (Repentance+); shop-pool items 15; sale items half price. Stock by shop level: level 0 has 0–1 pickups + 1–2 items, level 4 (600 coins donated, a persistent meta upgrade) has 4–5 pickups + 1–2 items. Shops need 1 key except floor 1 in Repentance. — [Isaac wiki: Shop](https://bindingofisaacrebirth.wiki.gg/wiki/Shop)
- Steam Sale halves prices (15 → 7); Repentance changed stacking to price/(copies+1) after two copies made everything free; Restock refills bought slots at increasing prices. — [Isaac wiki: Steam Sale](https://bindingofisaacrebirth.wiki.gg/wiki/Steam_Sale); [PC Gamer: Repentance items](https://pcgamer.com/the-binding-of-isaac-repentance-items-guide)
- **Isaac co-op:** coins/bombs/keys shared pool; items per player. — [Isaac wiki: Repentance Co-Op](https://bindingofisaacrebirth.wiki.gg/wiki/Repentance_Co-Op)
- **Killing Floor 2:** a Tripwire reply says server-wide auto-share was chosen "to make sure all players feel they are on an equal playing field"; players can also drop dosh manually; late joiners asking for dosh is resented; players give dosh to perks that need expensive gear. — [Steam KF2 discussions](https://steamcommunity.com/app/232090/discussions/0/143387886724051063); [Ludo.guide KF2](https://www.ludo.guide/guide/killing-floor-2)
- **RoR2:** gold is split among players (per mod settings description), items belong to whoever grabs them; interactables (chests, shrines) scale with player count; mods like ShareSuite exist because of "loot being stolen or one player dominating"; Fair Share mod scales prices by each player's item count vs team average. Players disagree whether multiplayer gives less per player. — [Thunderstore: ShareSuite](https://thunderstore.io/c/riskofrain2/p/FunkFrog-and-Sipondo/ShareSuite/v/2.0.0/); [Thunderstore: Multitudes](https://thunderstore.io/c/riskofrain2/p/6thmoon/MultitudesDifficulty/v/1.1.0/); [Thunderstore: FairShare](https://thunderstore.io/c/riskofrain2/p/DeltaLines/FairShare/); [Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/632360/discussions/0/2141966124582714590)
- RoR2 money-costing interactables scale exponentially with a time-based difficulty coefficient, fixed on entering the stage (fan wiki; exact formula not found). — [RoR2 fandom: Barrel](https://riskofrain2.fandom.com/wiki/Barrel)
- **Deep Rock Galactic:** Nitra goes to a shared team depository; any player can order a resupply pod for 80 Nitra; the pod has 4 racks, each refilling 50% ammo and half health once; no end bonus for stockpiled Nitra (gold paid for resupplies before Update 4, 2017). — [DRG wiki: Resupply Pod](https://deeprockgalactic.wiki.gg/wiki/Resupply_Pod); [DRG wiki: Nitra](https://deeprockgalactic.wiki.gg/wiki/Nitra)
- **Hades Charon:** sells for Obols, and Darkness can be bought from him. Prices not retrieved. — [Inverse: Mirror of Night](https://inverse.com/gaming/hades-mirror-of-night-best-talents-build-darkness)

### Inferences
- **The brief's rule** ("halo pickup goes to everybody's pockets") is duplication: each player gets the full amount, unlike Isaac/DRG one wallet or RoR2 split. Duplication avoids both stealing and "who spends the team wallet" arguments, and lets players with different needs buy different things (KF2 behaviour), but it means party income scales with kill speed. Recommend fixing halo income per room (a per-room halo budget dropped from its enemies) rather than per kill, so 1–4 players earn the same per player per room, and prices can be fixed.
- **Price ratios:** Isaac's 3/5/15 (heart/pickup/item) is a well-tested ratio. For Meatgrinder: if a normal room yields ~6–10 halos per player and a dungeon has ~10–14 rooms, a player has ~80–120 halos at the merchant; consumables at ~10–15 and a map at ~20–30 lets a player buy 4–6 things per dungeon. These are inferred starting points for the spec, not sourced numbers.
- **Restock with rising price** (Isaac) is a clean sink for a 4-player party buying the same item: each purchase is per player (each has their own pocket), so stock should be per-player (each player sees and buys their own copy), avoiding first-come conflicts, like Isaac's extra boss item per extra player.
- **Halos persist?** The brief says "items remain from the last session". If halos also persist, the shop becomes meta progression; recommend halos reset per dungeon (Isaac coins) or carry a capped amount, to avoid hoarding.

### Gaps
- No developer source for RoR2's gold split rule or chest cost formula; no Hades Charon prices; no Killing Floor trader prices or exact auto-share amount.

## 6. Persistent progression in co-op with players of very different progress

### Takeaway
Games keep veterans and newcomers together either by scaling the veteran down (FFXIV level sync), lifting the newcomer up (Destiny 2 Fireteam Power, highest player lifts others to 5 below), or by making meta progress unlock variety rather than power (Isaac). Permanent power in a short-run roguelite is widely criticised (Rogue Legacy: difficulty "starts too hard and ends up too easy"), but defended because every run "has value".

### Cited Findings
- **FFXIV level sync:** applies automatically when a character joins a duty above its level; abilities learned above the synced level are lost for the duty (role actions kept), HP/damage and gear are scaled down; restored afterwards. — [FFXIV wiki: Level Sync](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Level_Sync)
- Criticism: jobs get core mechanics late, so synced play can feel stripped; forum proposals for alternative sync points. — [Massively OP: Wisdom of Nym](https://massivelyop.com/2017/09/25/wisdom-of-nym-final-fantasy-xivs-leveling-structure-as-it-stands/); [Square Enix forum](https://forum.square-enix.com/ffxiv/threads/498223-Rethinking-Level-Sync?p=6457342)
- **Destiny 2 Fireteam Power:** the highest-power player raises teammates to 5 below them for activity difficulty only; rewards still use each player's own power. — [Gfinity: Final Shape power rework](https://www.gfinityesports.com/article/destiny-2-the-finale-shape-will-feature-a-power-level-rework)
- Dares of Eternity: an 1150-power activity that new players (~1100) were auto-launched into; veterans lowered their own power to help ("Operation: Save the New Lights"). — [NME](https://www.nme.com/news/destiny-2-veterans-are-saving-trapped-newcomers-3130774); [mmogames](https://mmogames.com/articles/news/destiny-2-vets-save-newbies-from-dares-of-eternity-activity)
- **Meta progression critiques/defences:** permanent upgrades make difficulty "a slider that starts too hard and ends up too easy"; Rogue Legacy "put way too much emphasis on the metagame" at the expense of the run; defence (Game Maker's Toolkit): when you die "you don't feel like you've wasted your time", and nearly all players can eventually finish; FTL and Isaac "unlock variants, not explicit power ups". — [Steam discussion (Hades)](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1145350/discussions/0/4358999171576511867); [GMTK transcript: Roguelikes, Persistency and Progression](https://amara.org/subtitles/7doGHxwrhRpX/en/1/download/Roguelikes%20Persistency%20and%20Progression%20%20Game%20Makers%20Toolkit.en.txt); [Quarter to Three forum](https://forum.quartertothree.com/t/the-action-roguelite-thread/148031?page=2)
- **Isaac** persists a shop level (600 coins donated → more stock), i.e. a meta upgrade of variety/availability rather than character stats. — [Isaac wiki: Shop](https://bindingofisaacrebirth.wiki.gg/wiki/Shop)
- **Darktide** frames trees as co-op synergy multiplying ("you multiply that by the synergies you create with other players"). — [Shacknews](https://www.shacknews.com/article/137295/warhammer-40k-darktide-patch-13)
- **Killing Floor 2** late joiners lacking dosh is a known friction point. — [Steam KF2](https://steamcommunity.com/app/232090/discussions/0/1742231705676188110)

### Inferences
- **What persists in the brief:** talent level, relic collection, items. The talent level is the uneven-progress problem. Options for the spec, ordered by simplicity:
  1. **Per-dungeon talent cap** (FFXIV-style sync down): each dungeon tier has a max usable talent point count (e.g. Pearly Gates 5, next 9, …); points above it are inactive there. The veteran keeps choices (picks which nodes stay), the content stays tuned. Simple, host-authoritative, deterministic.
  2. **Party floor** (Destiny-style lift up): a newcomer in a group gets temporary points up to N below the host's dungeon cap, chosen from a default build. Helps a friend join a high dungeon but needs a default build per tree.
  3. Combine 1 and 2: every player plays at exactly the dungeon's point count. This makes progression a matter of *unlocked options* (which trees/nodes you own) rather than raw power, matching Isaac's "variety, not power".
- **Host gate:** the brief's "host dictates unlocked dungeons" matches the Destiny lesson: newcomers must not be dropped into content they can't handle without scaling; pair host gating with option 1 or 3.
- **Relics** are the second permanent power axis; keep them sidegrades (section 3) so a newcomer with no relics isn't simply weaker.
- **Short runs + permanent power:** the GMTK/Rogue Legacy critique implies that if talent points only grow, early dungeons become trivial for veterans; a per-dungeon cap avoids this and keeps every dungeon tuned for one point count.
- **Competing combos (kill streaks):** with uneven builds, a combo leaderboard rewards the veteran; normalising points per dungeon also makes the competition fair.

### Gaps
- No primary-source developer discussion found of mixed-progress design in a co-op roguelite specifically (e.g. RoR2 or Hades has no co-op persistence parity issue in the same shape).

## 7. Browser save systems (localStorage, export/import codes, versioning, tamper)

### Takeaway
localStorage is small (5 MiB per origin) but more than enough for a character save; the real risks are browser eviction (best-effort storage, LRU eviction under pressure, Safari deleting script-written data after 7 days of browser use without interaction, private mode wiping on close) and players clearing site data. An export code (versioned JSON, compressed, base64, with a checksum) is the standard backup; Cookie Clicker's is a URL-encoded base64 string ending in "!END!". A checksum catches truncated pastes, not cheaters; anti-tamper is not worth it among friends, but the host can clamp impossible values.

### Cited Findings
- Web Storage is limited to 10 MiB maximum, browsers store up to 5 MiB of localStorage and 5 MiB of sessionStorage per origin; exceeding it throws `QuotaExceededError` (wrap writes in try/catch). — [MDN: Storage quotas and eviction criteria](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Storage_API/Storage_quotas_and_eviction_criteria)
- Origin data is best-effort by default: it persists while under quota, with enough disk, and unless the user deletes it; `navigator.storage.persist()` requests persistent storage (Firefox prompts; Chrome/Safari decide from interaction history without a prompt); persistent origins are skipped by eviction. — [MDN](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Storage_API/Storage_quotas_and_eviction_criteria)
- Eviction is LRU by origin, whole origin at once ("all of its data, not parts of it"); Chrome uses at most 80% of disk. Safari "proactively evicts" script-created data when an origin has no user interaction in the last seven days of browser use (with cross-site tracking prevention on). Private browsing usually deletes data when the session ends. — [MDN](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Storage_API/Storage_quotas_and_eviction_criteria)
- Chrome team research cited by MDN: data is "very rarely deleted by the browser" for regularly visited sites. — [MDN](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Storage_API/Storage_quotas_and_eviction_criteria)
- MDN's page does not state explicitly whether localStorage is in the best-effort bucket; the 5 MiB number is described by a W3C list comment as "mostly arbitrary". — [MDN](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Storage_API/Storage_quotas_and_eviction_criteria); [W3C public-webapps-github](https://lists.w3.org/Archives/Public/public-webapps-github/2020May/1564.html)
- **Cookie Clicker export:** base64 of the save text, URL-encoded (`=` → `%3D`), with the suffix `%21END%21` ("!END!"); editing means stripping the suffix, decoding, editing, re-encoding. No checksum found. — [Cookie Clicker wiki: Cheating](https://cookieclicker.wiki.gg/wiki/Cheating)
- Truncated pasted exports are a real failure ("data incomplete or corrupted"); a copy-to-clipboard button was suggested; Idle Breakout advises exporting a backup before importing. — [itch.io post](https://itch.io/post/14202013); [Idle Breakout codes page via search](https://partnernews.mambu.com/default.aspx/A11KUK/994625/Idle%20Breakout%20Import%20Codes.pdf)
- **Versioning practice:** embed a schema version in every save from day one ("adding one later requires inferring the old version from save contents"); load = read version → migrate forward → validate → instantiate; keep the last good save as backup; forward-only migration chains (e.g. 38 → 39 → 40) never deleted, enforced by a compatibility self-test; write-then-rename to avoid partial writes. — [Supercraft: save migrations](https://crux.supercraft.host/blog/player-data-save-migrations-every-patch/); [itch.io devlog](https://itch.io/devlog/1624210/version-1302-released.amp); [Bugnet: save corruption](https://bugnet.io/blog/debugging-save-corruption-across-versions)
- A client checksum detects corruption but not tampering, since the client can recompute it; real enforcement needs a validator the player doesn't control. — [Supercraft](https://crux.supercraft.host/blog/player-data-save-migrations-every-patch/)

### Inferences
- **Size:** a Meatgrinder account (4 classes × talent picks, ~24 relic flags, 2 inventory stacks, unlocked dungeons, settings) is well under 10 KB of JSON; the 5 MiB limit is irrelevant. Keep one localStorage key per account plus a `.bak` copy of the previous good save (write new, then replace backup).
- **Eviction:** call `navigator.storage.persist()` once (no prompt on Chrome/Safari; a prompt on Firefox, so ask after the first dungeon, not on page load). Remind players to export after unlocking a relic (a "save code" button on the results screen), because Safari's 7-day rule and "clear site data" wipe everything.
- **Export code format (proposal):** `MG1.` + base64url(deflate(JSON)) + `.` + 8-hex CRC32 of the payload. The prefix carries the format version for humans and for import routing; the CRC catches truncated or mangled pastes before parsing; base64url avoids Cookie Clicker's `%3D` escaping. Provide both "copy code" and "download .txt file" (the brief mentions "a file"). Import order: check prefix → CRC → decompress → parse → migrate by `schemaVersion` → validate ranges → only then overwrite, after making a backup of the current save.
- **Anti-tamper:** not worth it for co-op among friends; any client check can be bypassed. The useful protection is host-side clamping in MP: the host (authoritative sim) accepts a joiner's loadout only if it's legal (talent points ≤ the dungeon cap, prerequisites met, relic IDs known), which also protects against corrupted saves and version mismatch, not only cheating. With a per-dungeon point cap (section 6), an edited save gives no in-dungeon advantage beyond unlocks.
- **Version mismatch in P2P:** peers on different builds may carry saves with different schema versions; the host should compare protocol and save schema versions on join and refuse with a readable message.

### Gaps
- MDN does not say explicitly that localStorage is subject to the same LRU/Safari eviction as IndexedDB (it is widely assumed); no primary source verified here.
- Candy Box and A Dark Room export formats were not researched; no source for a checksum in any well-known incremental game's export.
